# The Billionaire Night Owl

> I've got a friend of mine who's a billionaire who does that. And that's he's like, Alex, I can't wake up and do work anytime before 10 a.m.

## The Story
Hormozi has a billionaire friend who does his best work between 10 PM and 4 AM and cannot work before 10 AM.

## What It Illustrates
There is no universal successful sleep schedule; the key is finding the specific block of time where you get uninterrupted thinking and working done.
