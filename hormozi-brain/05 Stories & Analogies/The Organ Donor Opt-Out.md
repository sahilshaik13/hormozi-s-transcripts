# The Organ Donor Opt-Out

> how do I make the thing that I want someone to do appear like the default option as in they have to opt out of it.

## The Story
A study showed that one country achieved an 80% organ donor rate while others averaged 10%. The difference was that the successful country made being an organ donor the default option, requiring people to opt out rather than opt in.

## What It Illustrates
Frame upsells and memberships as a default status the customer already qualifies for, reducing the friction of making a new purchasing decision.
