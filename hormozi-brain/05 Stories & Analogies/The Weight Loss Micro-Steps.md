# The Weight Loss Micro-Steps

> can I say hey how can I make grocery shopping easier here's a list here's a pre-loaded instacart grocery list

## The Story
Losing weight requires eating better, which requires grocery shopping, prepping, and storing food. By providing a free, pre-loaded Instacart grocery list for specific demographics, you completely solve step one, naturally leading them to need help with prep and storage.

## What It Illustrates
Break big problems into micro-steps and completely solve the first one to create demand for the next.
