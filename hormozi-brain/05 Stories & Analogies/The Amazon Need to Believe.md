# The Amazon Need to Believe

> they took away every conceivable reason for someone that they would have to believe in order to be successful

## The Story
Amazon told sellers to simply upload their products to make money, removing the need to know marketing, sales, or customer service.

## What It Illustrates
The fewer things a customer needs to believe or learn to be successful, the easier and faster they will buy.
