# The Lumber Mill Sawdust

> They created a whole another Revenue line from stuff they already had and so right now in your business you have sawdust

## The Story
Lumber mills used to throw away the sawdust generated from cutting logs. Eventually, an engineer realized they could mix the waste sawdust with glue to create particle board, creating a massive new revenue stream from trash.

## What It Illustrates
Look for the byproducts, excess capacity, and waste in your current business operations that can be packaged and sold for pure profit.
