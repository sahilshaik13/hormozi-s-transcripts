# The Airplane Runway

> The bigger the plane, the longer the runway in order to get it to take off.

## The Story
You cannot take off a massive airplane on a tiny runway. The size of the plane dictates the length of the runway needed to get airborne.

## What It Illustrates
The more expensive your product (bigger plane), the more friction and education (longer runway) you need in your funnel.
