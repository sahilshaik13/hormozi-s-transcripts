# The Baton Pass

> you want to basically have the customer feel like you have this Baton Pass between every single step

## The Story
The customer journey should feel like a seamless baton pass from the ad, to the webinar headline, to the webinar intro, to the sales call, to onboarding.

## What It Illustrates
Total congruence across all marketing and sales touchpoints creates streamlined, lubricated sales.
