# The NFL Red Zone

> if they're in the Red Zone they [ __ ] close and I can tell you if you have this ability if you have this skill it will make up for a tremendous amount of deficiencies

## The Story
The top five Red Zone offenses in the NFL over the last five years made the playoffs 90% of the time, regardless of their defense or special teams. Closing is the Red Zone of business.

## What It Illustrates
Being exceptionally good at closing can make up for a tremendous amount of deficiencies in other areas of your business.
