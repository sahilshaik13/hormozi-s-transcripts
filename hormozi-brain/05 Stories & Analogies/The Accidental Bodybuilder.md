# The Accidental Bodybuilder

> you're not going to sneeze and get jacked

## The Story
Guys come into the gym wanting to work out but express fear of getting 'too big' like a bodybuilder. Hormozi points out that getting massive doesn't happen by accident or from a single sneeze.

## What It Illustrates
Worrying about scaling fulfillment before making a single sale is like worrying about getting too muscular before lifting a single weight.
