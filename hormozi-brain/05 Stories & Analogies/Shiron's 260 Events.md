# Shiron's 260 Events

> That was the thing that took a $200 million business to $1.2 million, just sheer volume.

## The Story
Shiron grew a real estate business from $200 million to $1.2 billion in less than 3 years. When asked how, he revealed he flew around and spoke at 260 events in 365 days.

## What It Illustrates
Massive, unreasonable volume of a proven input is the secret to scaling a business exponentially.
