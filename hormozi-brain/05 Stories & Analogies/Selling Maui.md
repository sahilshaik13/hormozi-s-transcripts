# Selling Maui

> sell the vacation not the plane fright right and so this is what everyone else talks about they're like sir for us to get you these results you're going to have to take your pants off you're gonna have to jump through this Loop

## The Story
When selling a vacation, you don't sell the TSA lines, the turbulence, or the baggage claim. You sell the final destination: Maui. The only difference in price is whether they take a boat, a commercial flight, or a private jet.

## What It Illustrates
Stop pitching the features, modules, and hard work (the plane flight). Pitch the ultimate outcome the prospect desires (the vacation).
