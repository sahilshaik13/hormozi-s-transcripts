# Tesla's Go-To-Market

> Tesla started with a $250,000 Roadster, clearly selling to a select few

## The Story
Tesla started by selling the $250,000 Roadster to a select few as a beta test. They used that capital and proof of concept to build the Model S, and eventually worked their way down to the mass-market Model 3.

## What It Illustrates
Start by selling expensive, unscalable products to fund the creation of cheaper, scalable products.
