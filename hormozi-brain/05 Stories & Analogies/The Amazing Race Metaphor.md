# The Amazing Race Metaphor

> I can be your backseat driver because it'll give you an unfair advantage

## The Story
In The Amazing Race, a couple won repeatedly by hiring a local to sit in their backseat and navigate for them. The show eventually had to ban this unfair advantage.

## What It Illustrates
Hiring a coach or expert is like putting a local in your backseat; it gives you an unfair advantage to reach your destination faster.
