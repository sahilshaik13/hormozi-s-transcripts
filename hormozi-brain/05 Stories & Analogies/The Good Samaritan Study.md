# The Good Samaritan Study

> The difference between the people who were 10 minutes early and 10 minutes late was a 6x difference in who stopped to actually help the person.

## The Story
Princeton seminary students were asked to present on the Good Samaritan. On the way, they passed a person needing help. The only correlation to whether they stopped was how late they were, not their moral self-assessment.

## What It Illustrates
Lack of capacity (in this case, time) literally blinds you to opportunities right in front of you.
