# The Quarterback and the Cheerleader

> cheerleaders don't ask the quarterback to leave the game when the game's on the line

## The Story
Gary Vee noted that cheerleaders support the team from the sidelines or clear the way on the field, but they never interrupt the game to ask the quarterback to leave when the game is on the line.

## What It Illustrates
A good spouse supports your goals and doesn't change the rules or demand you stop working when you are in the middle of executing your shared vision.
