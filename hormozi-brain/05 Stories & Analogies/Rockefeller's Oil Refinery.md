# Rockefeller's Oil Refinery

> he was willing to overpay for the story he saw it as an investment in his brand

## The Story
John D. Rockefeller overpaid to buy the largest oil refinery in Cincinnati. Because he could then claim to be the biggest, he easily acquired 20 smaller competitors in the next 30 days.

## What It Illustrates
It is worth overpaying for the story and brand value of being 'the best' or 'the biggest' because it makes future acquisition and marketing exponentially easier.
