# The Brick Exercise

> Our goal is to use a divergent thought process to think of as many easy ways to combine these elements to provide value.

## The Story
When asked to list uses for a brick, most people think of a few standard uses. But if you change the size, material, or shape of the brick, the possibilities become endless.

## What It Illustrates
Divergent thinking is required to create unique, high-value solutions from standard components.
