# The $50M Business Buyout

> now we have a $125 million thing that we paid 50 for that already paid us 40 back

## The Story
You buy a $50M business making $5M in profit using $15M cash and a $35M loan. By growing it 20% a year for 5 years, it generates $40M in cash flow to pay off the debt, and its profit grows to $12.5M. At a 10x multiple, the business is now worth $125M, turning your $15M investment into $120M+.

## What It Illustrates
How institutional investors and smart money use leverage, cash flow, and multiples to make massive leaps in wealth.
