# The Powerball Dread

> I remember having this moment of sheer dread where I was like What if I win

## The Story
When the Powerball hit a billion dollars, Hormozi bought a ticket but felt sheer dread during the drawing. He realized that if he won, he would lose the chance to prove to himself that he could build success on his own merits.

## What It Illustrates
The pursuit and the proof of competence are more fulfilling than the unearned financial reward.
