# The Labradoodle Breeding

> nicer wolves, breeding nicer wolves, breeding nicer wolves. Eventually you get a Labradoodle.

## The Story
If you breed nice wolves together over enough generations, you eventually get a Labradoodle.

## What It Illustrates
Subjective well-being and baseline happiness have a strong genetic and inherited component, meaning external achievements won't drastically change your baseline mood.
