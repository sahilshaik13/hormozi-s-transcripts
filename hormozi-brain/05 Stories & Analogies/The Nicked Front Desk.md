# The Nicked Front Desk

> That sounds like a pretty good place to start it for a discount.

## The Story
A vendor built a custom front desk for Hormozi's gym but nicked the corner. Instead of asking for a discount, his partner asked how much it would cost the vendor to take it back and fix it. The vendor said $1,500, which immediately became the discount amount.

## What It Illustrates
Get the other party to bid against themselves by pricing their own inconvenience.
