# Brushing Teeth

> you had external accountability that turned into an internal habit

## The Story
As a kid, you didn't want to brush your teeth, but your parents forced you to do it every night. Now as an adult, you do it automatically without being told.

## What It Illustrates
External accountability eventually turns into an internal habit, which is why coaching/accountability is necessary for success.
