# Harassing Courtney

> He obviously isn't trying to harass Courtney. She might describe his behavior as harassing. He might describe his behavior as being cordial.

## The Story
One employee thinks he is just being polite by saying hello, while Courtney feels she is being harassed. Without defining the exact physical behavior, both are using different labels for the same action.

## What It Illustrates
Subjective labels fail in management; you must break down expectations into crystal clear, observable behaviors.
