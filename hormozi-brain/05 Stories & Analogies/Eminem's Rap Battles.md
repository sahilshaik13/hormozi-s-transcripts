# Eminem's Rap Battles

> He pulls all the wind out of their sales. He pulls all the punches because he punches himself with that.

## The Story
Eminem figured out that if he owned all of his flaws upfront—saying he was short, white, etc.—his opponents had nothing left to use against him.

## What It Illustrates
Using damaging admissions pulls the wind out of your prospect's objections, building immense trust before you make your pitch.
