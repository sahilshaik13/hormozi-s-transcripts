# The Big Wheel vs. Small Wheel

> if you just do one revolution you get more than this one does in 10

## The Story
People try to spin a tiny wheel as fast as possible to cover distance. Instead, you should build a massive wheel where a single revolution covers more ground than 10 spins of the small wheel.

## What It Illustrates
Effort should be spent on high-leverage tasks (A+ problems) rather than high-frequency, low-impact tasks.
