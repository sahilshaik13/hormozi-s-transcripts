# The Cheesecake Factory Close

> if i say hey babe you want to go to uh cheesecake factory she might be like well no i don't want to go... if i switched that and i said would you be against going to cheese factory well i'm not against going... great let's go

## The Story
Hormozi explains how he gets his wife to agree to eat at the Cheesecake Factory. Instead of asking 'Do you want to go?' (which invites hesitation), he asks 'Would you be against going?' She replies that she isn't against it, resulting in an agreement.

## What It Illustrates
Phrasing a request as a no-based question lowers resistance in everyday negotiations as well as high-ticket sales.
