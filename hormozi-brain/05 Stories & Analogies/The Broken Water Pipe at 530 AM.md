# The Broken Water Pipe at 5:30 AM

> I said well what do you think I'm going to do he's like I I guess call a plumber and I was like okay what what would I do in the meantime he's like probably get a bucket

## The Story
Hormozi got a call at 5:30 AM from an employee panicking about a broken water pipe. Hormozi asked what the employee thought he should do, and the employee suggested calling a plumber and getting a bucket. Hormozi told him to just do that.

## What It Illustrates
Founders shouldn't be on demand for every minor crisis; employees can solve basic problems if you don't enable their helplessness.
