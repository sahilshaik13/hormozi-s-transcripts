# The Costco Supplement Accident

> As soon as I made that switch from saying, 'Hey, you don't need to buy this one; you can get that from over there,' everyone bought what I recommended afterward

## The Story
While selling supplements at his gym, Hormozi ran out of a specific product. Instead of making it awkward, he told the customer to buy it cheaper down the street at Costco. The customer was so grateful for the honesty that they immediately bought everything else he recommended.

## What It Illustrates
Sacrificing a small sale builds massive trust that secures the larger sale.
