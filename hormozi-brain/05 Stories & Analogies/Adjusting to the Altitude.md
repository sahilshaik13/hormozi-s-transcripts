# Adjusting to the Altitude

> you gotta let your lungs adjust the altitude like you just went up like three rungs of success

## The Story
When you climb a mountain, the air gets thinner and it becomes harder to breathe. You have to give your lungs time to adjust to the new elevation.

## What It Illustrates
Success brings new, uncomfortable pressures. The pain you feel isn't a sign to quit; it's just the natural process of adapting to a higher level of achievement.
