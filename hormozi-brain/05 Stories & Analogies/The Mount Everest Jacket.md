# The Mount Everest Jacket

> If I want to buy a jacket, I want to see that the jacket is still warm on Mount Everest because I think, okay, if it's cold, if it's warm, if it's still warm on the top of Mount Everest, it'll probably keep me warm from my car to the door

## The Story
When buying a winter jacket, a consumer wants to know it kept someone warm on Mount Everest, even if they only plan to wear it walking from their car to their front door.

## What It Illustrates
Consumers have an 'overkill bias' and will always choose to buy from the absolute highest authority to guarantee their desired outcome.
