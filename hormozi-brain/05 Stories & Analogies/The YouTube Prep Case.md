# The YouTube Prep Case

> my understanding of how much work it takes to do something at this level is wildly under what the reality of it is

## The Story
Hormozi asked a top YouTuber with 15 million subscribers for his video prep notes, expecting a few bullet points on a phone. Instead, he received a 22-page document for a 15-minute video, and learned top creators spend 4 weeks working 12-hour days for a single video.

## What It Illustrates
The standard for exceptional work is orders of magnitude higher than what average people assume.
