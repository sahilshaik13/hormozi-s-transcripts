# The Angry Boss

> I don't need to change this guy's marketing right I don't need to change this guy's beliefs about his own business he's just to change who he is

## The Story
A business owner complained on a call that his employees sucked and he couldn't keep good talent. Hormozi simply asked him, 'Have you met you?'

## What It Illustrates
Business problems are often just personal character flaws reflecting into the company.
