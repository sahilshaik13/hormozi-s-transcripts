# The GameStop/Dumb Money Analogy

> he bought down some hedge funds and up with with memes nothing but memes

## The Story
The WallStreetBets community rallied around GameStop stock not because of logical financial analysis, but because of memes, culture, and a 'David vs. Goliath' narrative. The leader used memes to take down hedge funds.

## What It Illustrates
Culture, memes, and a shared enemy/goal are far more powerful for community engagement than logical 'value posts.'
