# Dogs, Horses, and Tigers

> he said a tiger can eat a full meal but then if a bunny walks across his line of vision we'll kill the bunny for sport he's like you want tigers

## The Story
There are three types of salespeople. Dogs need their bellies rubbed or they starve. Horses will run as long as you whip them. Tigers will eat a full meal, but if a bunny walks by, they will kill it just for sport.

## What It Illustrates
You want to hire salespeople who inherently love the thrill of the hunt and don't need constant external motivation.
