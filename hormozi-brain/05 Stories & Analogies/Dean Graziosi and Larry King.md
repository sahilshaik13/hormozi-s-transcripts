# Dean Graziosi and Larry King

> You know what did change - Just the opening sentences, the hook.

## The Story
Dean Graziosi spent a fortune recreating a Larry King set for an infomercial, which crushed. Years later, he got the real Larry King to interview him, but the ad bombed. He realized the opening hook was about Dean's ego, not the viewer's needs. He flew Larry back just to re-record the first two sentences to focus on the viewer, and the infomercial made millions.

## What It Illustrates
The hook is the single most critical variable in an ad; changing just the first two sentences can turn a massive failure into a multi-million dollar success.
