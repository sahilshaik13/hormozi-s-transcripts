# Selling the Vacation to Maui

> You want to just talk about Maui you want to talk about the lick your fingers good what it's going to be like when they have a full stomach

## The Story
When selling a trip to Maui, amateur salespeople talk about the flight, the TSA line, the baggage check, and the seat selection. Master salespeople talk about the beach, the wind in your hair, and the Mai Tai in your hand.

## What It Illustrates
Sell the ultimate destination and the feeling of the result, not the features and friction of the process required to get there.
