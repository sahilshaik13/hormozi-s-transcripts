# The Iranian Revolution

> the family members that I had who had skills and then were able to scatter all over the world which my family is uh they were able to start all over again and rebuild what they had lost

## The Story
During the revolution, Hormozi's family fled Iran and had all their physical assets seized by the government. The family members who possessed valuable skills were able to scatter globally and rebuild their wealth, while those who only had physical assets struggled.

## What It Illustrates
Skills are the only true, un-confiscatable wealth.
