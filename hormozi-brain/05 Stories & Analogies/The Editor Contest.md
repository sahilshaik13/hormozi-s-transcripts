# The Editor Contest

> guess how many of the 1,200 the 1,200 people actually submitted an edit 60

## The Story
Hormozi's media company had 1,200 people express interest in an editing job. They sent a raw file to all 1,200 applicants to test their skills. Only 60 people (5%) actually submitted an edit.

## What It Illustrates
The competition is an illusion; most people will not do the actual work required, meaning the odds are in your favor if you just try.
