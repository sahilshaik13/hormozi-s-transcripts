# Victor Frankl's Meaning

> if he can be happy there then me making a [ __ ] out of money being young and healthy and married in the most developed country in the world do I really have any reason

## The Story
Victor Frankl was able to find meaning and be somewhat happy while surviving the horrors of the Holocaust.

## What It Illustrates
If someone can find happiness in the worst conditions imaginable, a young, healthy, wealthy entrepreneur has no objective reason to be miserable.
