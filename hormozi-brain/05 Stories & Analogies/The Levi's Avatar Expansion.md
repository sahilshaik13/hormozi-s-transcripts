# The Levi's Avatar Expansion

> they showed basically four or five different avatars who you're like oh I don't resonate with the Hipster but that one's mine right they basically took a shopping cart and added to their Tam

## The Story
Levi's ran an ad showing one pair of jeans worn by six completely different types of people (a hipster, a working man, etc.) in different settings.

## What It Illustrates
You can expand your Total Addressable Market simply by showing how your core product fits into the identities of different customer avatars.
