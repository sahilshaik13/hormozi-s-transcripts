# The Business Genie

> If you guess wrong…your business dies. High stakes genie, what can I say.

## The Story
A genie offers to double your new customers, double your purchase frequency, or double your prices. Doubling customers or frequency yields a 3.5x profit increase due to associated delivery costs. Doubling prices yields a 6x profit increase because costs remain flat.

## What It Illustrates
Raising prices is mathematically the most powerful lever for increasing net profit.
