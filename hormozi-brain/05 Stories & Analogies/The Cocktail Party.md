# The Cocktail Party

> In all that noise, a single sound pierces through it all and you turn around. Wanna know the sound? Your name.

## The Story
Imagine being in a loud, crowded club. Despite the thumping music and hollering, if someone says your name, it pierces through the noise and you instantly turn around.

## What It Illustrates
A good call-out cuts through market noise by being highly specific to the target audience.
