# The Forbes List Focus

> every other person on the Forbes list that just did one thing for the entirety of their career

## The Story
When looking at billionaires 20 years ahead of him, Hormozi realized almost all of them (like Bezos with Amazon or the founders of Panda Express) did just one thing for decades before diversifying. They didn't start multiple businesses at once.

## What It Illustrates
Massive wealth comes from extreme, long-term focus on a single vehicle, not from running multiple parallel businesses.
