# The Woman in the Red Dress

> This lady in the in the red dress has chlamidia she's got Daddy Issues she's got an ex-boyfriend with with a gun who who stalks her right you don't know yet.

## The Story
A new business opportunity is compared to an attractive woman in a red dress on a first date. She looks perfect on paper, but you don't yet know about her hidden baggage, crazy ex-boyfriend, or underlying issues.

## What It Illustrates
New opportunities always look flawless from the outside because you haven't discovered their specific problems yet. Stick to the business where you already know the booby traps.
