# The Video Game Spawn Point

> Every single one of us can today wake up like it's the first day of a video game.

## The Story
Imagine waking up today as if it's the first day of a video game. You just got transported into your current body, with your current job, family, and problems. You can detach emotionally from past mistakes and just start playing from this exact spot.

## What It Illustrates
You can choose to start fresh and take action at any moment, regardless of your past baggage.
