# The Media Company Constraint

> you're like a 97 out of a 100 on media and you're a zero out of 100 on [ __ ] to sell

## The Story
A YouTube channel with 40M subscribers wanted to optimize their media production to make more money. Hormozi pointed out their media was a 97/100, but they had nothing to sell (0/100).

## What It Illustrates
Working on the wrong constraint, even if you improve it, will not grow the business.
