# The Newspaper vs. Mask Entrepreneur

> he went from a few million dollars a year to a few million dollars a week all right same entrepreneur simply applied it to a growing marketplace

## The Story
A smart entrepreneur was struggling to grow his newspaper company. He switched from selling newspapers (a shrinking market) to selling masks in manufacturing (a growing market) and his revenue skyrocketed from millions a year to millions a week.

## What It Illustrates
The trajectory of the market (growing vs. shrinking) dictates business growth more than the intelligence of the entrepreneur.
