# The Bad 8th Grade Boyfriend

> The only thing worse than making a bad investment is letting one bad investment stop you from making a good one. Don't let that bad decision burn you twice.

## The Story
If you had a terrible boyfriend in 8th grade, you wouldn't swear off all men forever. You recognize there are good ones and bad ones.

## What It Illustrates
A bad past investment shouldn't prevent you from making a good future investment.
