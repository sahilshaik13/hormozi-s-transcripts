# Apple Stock Price Increase

> if you bought a Apple stock at 10 and now it's 20 like you're not going to be upset like in time prices change

## The Story
If you buy Apple stock at $10 and it goes to $20, you aren't upset. People understand that prices go up over time as demand and value increase. However, if prices drop, past buyers feel cheated.

## What It Illustrates
It is always easier and safer to start prices low and raise them than to start high and lower them.
