# The Wine Tasting Study

> in a very real way price confers value to the product or service that you provide

## The Story
Researchers gave tasters three bottles of wine labeled with different prices (cheap, mid-tier, expensive). Tasters ranked the expensive one as the best, even though all three bottles contained the exact same wine.

## What It Illustrates
Price inherently confers perceived value to a product or service.
