# The Panda Express Empire

> he didn't start coaching other Chinese restaurant owners when he had five locations he just stuck with it

## The Story
Mr. Panda didn't start coaching other Chinese restaurant owners when he had five successful locations. He stuck with the traditional model, eventually owning 2,600 locations, the real estate, and generating nearly $1 billion a year in cash flow.

## What It Illustrates
Sticking with a traditional business model through the slow early phases builds massive, sellable asset value.
