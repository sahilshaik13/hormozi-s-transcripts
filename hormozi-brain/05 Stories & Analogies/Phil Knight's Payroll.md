# Phil Knight's Payroll

> He used his resourcefulness, not his resources.

## The Story
When Nike was about to go under, Phil Knight didn't have money for payroll. Instead of giving up, he convinced his vendors to pay his payroll for him.

## What It Illustrates
Success is based on resourcefulness, not the resources you start with.
