# Cutting Grass with Scissors

> you can cut grass with scissors and it will take all day or you can make one call and have someone else do it

## The Story
You can choose to cut your grass using a pair of scissors, which will take you all day. Alternatively, you can make one phone call and pay someone else to do it.

## What It Illustrates
Effort does not equal output; high-leverage decisions accomplish the same goal in a fraction of the time.
