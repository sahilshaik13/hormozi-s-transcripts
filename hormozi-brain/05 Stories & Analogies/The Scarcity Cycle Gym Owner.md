# The Scarcity Cycle Gym Owner

> he was focusing on the sales he was making he was focusing on the money he was making rather than focusing on the process

## The Story
A friend started posting content, went viral, and made $100k/month. However, he would stop posting once he made money, run out of money, panic, and start posting again.

## What It Illustrates
Focusing on the money rather than the process creates an inconsistent, stressful cycle; you must focus on the process to break it.
