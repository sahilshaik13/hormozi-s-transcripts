# Leila's LA Nail Salon

> So you’re telling me, more than speed, availability is the biggest lever on getting more leads to show?

## The Story
While in LA, Leila called four nail salons. The first didn't answer, the second had no availability, and the third put her on hold. The fourth salon answered, had immediate availability, and booked her instantly.

## What It Illustrates
Availability is the absolute biggest lever for capturing revenue; if you aren't available when the customer wants to buy, you lose to whoever is.
