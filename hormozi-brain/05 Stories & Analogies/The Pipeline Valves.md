# The Pipeline Valves

> Let's say you've checked off three of the four boxes. The water's still going to be stuck here.

## The Story
Imagine a water pipeline with multiple valves. To get water to flow out the other side, every single valve must be opened. If you open three out of four valves, the water still won't flow.

## What It Illustrates
Success requires meeting all necessary conditions. Failing doesn't mean your efforts were wrong; it just means you haven't opened the final valve yet.
