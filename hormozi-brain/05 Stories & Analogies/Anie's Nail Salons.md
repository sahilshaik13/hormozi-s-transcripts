# Anie's Nail Salons

> The thing that I kept trying to tease out of her is I was like "She was just in a rush."

## The Story
A founder had one highly successful nail salon but rushed into messy partnerships to open three or four more locations quickly. She did this out of an imaginary rush to make more money, rather than patiently saving cash from the first location to own the subsequent ones outright.

## What It Illustrates
Rushing into expansion via bad structures (like unnecessary partnerships) creates messes that could be avoided with patience.
