# The Tesla Top-Down Strategy

> I love the top down approach because you have a brand reinforcer, but also from an operational perspective... it made more sense to start here.

## The Story
Tesla started by selling the highly expensive Roadster to a very small market. Once they established a premium brand and built operational capacity, they moved down-market to the Model S, and eventually the mass-market Model 3.

## What It Illustrates
It is easier to start with high-priced, low-volume, unscalable offers to build brand equity and cash flow before attempting high-volume, low-margin mass-market offers.
