# The Dating Lead Magnet

> We want to solve the tiniest problem we possibly can for the prospect and completely solve it and blow them away.

## The Story
A dating coach offers a lead magnet titled 'How to know if a girl wants you to kiss her' with a simple solution: try to brush her hair behind her ear. If she leans in, she does; if she pulls away, she doesn't.

## What It Illustrates
A great lead magnet solves the tiniest possible problem completely, which builds trust that you can solve their larger, more complex problems.
