# The Martha Stewart Effect

> When you follow these 18 steps, you get a cake... Guess what happens? She gains influence over you because the next time she gives you instructions, you are more likely to follow them.

## The Story
Martha Stewart gave people exact, step-by-step instructions to bake a cake. When they followed the steps and the cake tasted great, their family praised them, and Martha gained massive influence over them.

## What It Illustrates
Making small promises and keeping them builds 'say-do correspondence', making prospects highly likely to follow your next, bigger instruction.
