# The Build a Business Competition

> we did not have $100,000 to give out that way but our Hope was that by the time the competition ended we would actually have that type of money

## The Story
Shopify launched a competition with a $100,000 prize for the highest-selling new store, even though they didn't actually have the $100,000 at the time. They banked on the competition generating enough growth to fund the prize.

## What It Illustrates
Using bold incentives and gamification can help a startup cross the chasm from early adopters to the mainstream.
