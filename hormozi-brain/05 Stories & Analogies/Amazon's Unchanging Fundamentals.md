# Amazon's Unchanging Fundamentals

> he asked the question not what do I think is going to change in the next 10 years he said what things won't change in the next 10 years

## The Story
Jeff Bezos was mocked on late-night TV for Amazon being unprofitable for 9 years. Bezos chose not to take profits, instead reinvesting all capital into infrastructure based on what would never change: people will always want things faster, easier, and risk-free.

## What It Illustrates
Reinvesting capital to dominate unchanging customer desires yields a monopoly-like moat.
