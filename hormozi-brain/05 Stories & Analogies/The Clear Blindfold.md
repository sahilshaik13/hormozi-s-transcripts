# The Clear Blindfold

> imagine trying to hit a target with a blindfold on you're just gonna keep missing

## The Story
Imagine trying to hit a target with a blindfold on, constantly missing despite having perfect technique. The person next to you is nailing every shot simply because their blindfold is clear.

## What It Illustrates
Highly successful people aren't necessarily doing different actions; they just have shed false beliefs and have a more accurate view of reality.
