# The Mechanic / Surgeon Secretary

> The only way we'd be able to answer that specifically is after our team of specialists look under the hood.

## The Story
You wouldn't ask a mechanic's receptionist to diagnose your car over the phone, nor a surgeon's secretary to explain your heart surgery before looking at you.

## What It Illustrates
You cannot answer highly specific, personalized questions about a prospect's situation until they buy and you can 'look under the hood'.
