# The $30 to $3000 Software

> If people see the value, they will stay.

## The Story
A Shopify competitor realized their biggest customers cost them the most money at $30/mo. The CEO called all 300 top customers and raised their price to $3000/mo. They only lost one customer.

## What It Illustrates
If people see the value, they will stay, even through a 100x price increase.
