# Warren Buffett's Candies

> Warren Buffet demands control over one thing in this business: their prices.

## The Story
Warren Buffett bought See's Candies and demanded control over one thing: pricing. He raised prices over 50 times in 51 years, sometimes by 17% in a single year, adding over $1B in profit.

## What It Illustrates
Consistent, compounding price increases are essential for long-term wealth creation and outpacing inflation.
