# High School Weight to Six-Pack

> after you get someone down to their High School weight the next solution you might have for them would be our six-pack program

## The Story
A customer comes in wanting to lose weight. Once you get them to their high school weight, they realize they now have loose skin, need a new wardrobe, and want a six-pack.

## What It Illustrates
Solving the first problem effectively creates the second problem, which becomes your next offer.
