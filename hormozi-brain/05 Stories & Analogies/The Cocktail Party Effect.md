# The Cocktail Party Effect

> In all that noise, a single sound pierces through it all and you turn around. Wanna know the sound? Your name.

## The Story
In a loud, crowded ballroom, a dropped tray of dishes gets everyone's attention, while hearing your specific name gets only your attention.

## What It Illustrates
Ads must use specific callouts (verbal and nonverbal) to pierce through the noise and grab the attention of the target audience.
