# The Magic Mouthwash

> If they swish-and-spit on the spot, then the next round of tooth photos shows whiter teeth? We've got a winner.

## The Story
If you claim a product whitens teeth, people are skeptical. But if you take a picture of their teeth, give them a free sample to swish and spit on the spot, and show them the whiter result immediately, they will buy.

## What It Illustrates
Getting the prospect the maximum achievable result before they buy is the most compelling form of proof.
