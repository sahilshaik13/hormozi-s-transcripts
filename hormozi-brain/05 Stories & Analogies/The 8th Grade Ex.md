# The 8th Grade Ex

> you let a bad decision burn you twice once when you made your poor investment but a second time when you let that bad investment stop you from a good investment

## The Story
A prospect refused to buy because she had been burned by a bad coaching program in the past. Hormozi compared it to having a bad 8th-grade boyfriend and deciding to never date men again.

## What It Illustrates
You shouldn't let a bad past decision burn you twice by allowing it to prevent you from making a good future decision.
