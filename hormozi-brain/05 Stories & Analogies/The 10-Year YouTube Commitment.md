# The 10-Year YouTube Commitment

> I'd like to have this channel be something in 5 to 10 years I was like We'll look at it then and see if it was worth continuing to do

## The Story
When Hormozi hired a YouTube vendor, he told them he wanted to evaluate the channel's success in 5 to 10 years, not 90 days. The vendor was shocked because every other client demanded immediate leads.

## What It Illustrates
Thinking in decades rather than days sets you apart from the competition and removes the pressure of short-term outcomes.
