# The Brushing Teeth Analogy

> did your parents ever tell you to brush your teeth when you grew up you probably hated it

## The Story
When you were a kid, your parents forced you to brush your teeth even though you hated it. Over time, because they kept doing it, you built the habit and now you brush your teeth on your own.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates the value of accountability in a program (like fitness or coaching) to help prospects understand why they need someone to hold them to their goals.
