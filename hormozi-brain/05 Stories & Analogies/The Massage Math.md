# The Massage Math

> you've got one person getting a massage for a hundred dollars versus six people getting a massage thirty percent of the twenty after giving them that first trial

## The Story
Pitching a $100 massage to 100 people might get 1 buyer. Pitching a $10 massage to 100 people gets 20 buyers. If 30% of those 20 buy a $100 package later, you get 6 high-ticket customers instead of 1, easily covering the upfront loss.

## What It Illustrates
Lowering the barrier to entry with a discounted lead magnet drastically reduces overall customer acquisition cost.
