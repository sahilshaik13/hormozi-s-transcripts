# The Mercenaries

> when I say mercenaries I mean an outsourced sales team

## The Story
Hormozi compares outsourced sales teams to mercenaries. They demand a massive cut of the spoils (20% of revenue), cherry-pick the easiest battles (only taking highly qualified calls), and ultimately build no long-term enterprise value for the kingdom (the business).

## What It Illustrates
Outsourced teams are a temporary fix that misalign incentives and hurt the long-term sellability of a company.
