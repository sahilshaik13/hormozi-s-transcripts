# The Yogurt Shop Pricing

> because I had control over what I picked I'm the one who's responsible for the decision

## The Story
If a worker behind a counter fills a cup of yogurt and charges $8, the customer feels ripped off. If the customer fills their own cup, puts it on the scale, and it costs $8, they blame themselves for taking too much.

## What It Illustrates
When the customer is in control of setting their own goals and parameters, they take ownership of the resulting price rather than feeling sold to.
