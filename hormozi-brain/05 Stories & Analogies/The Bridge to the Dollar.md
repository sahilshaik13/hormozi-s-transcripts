# The Bridge to the Dollar

> you start building all of these half-built Bridges right you never actually get all the way across

## The Story
You start building a bridge to reach a dollar on the other side. You hit a problem, stop, and start building a second bridge to a different dollar. You repeat this until you have many half-built bridges and no dollars.

## What It Illustrates
Chasing missed opportunities instead of solving problems leads to wasted resources and zero actual returns.
