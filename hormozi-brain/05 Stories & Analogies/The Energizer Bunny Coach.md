# The Energizer Bunny Coach

> you can switch the coaches and all of a sudden the whole team does way better it's because the coach drives the culture

## The Story
A sales director is compared to a football coach. The coach doesn't play the game, but by managing the culture, repeating the same drills with enthusiasm, and pouring into the players, the output of the entire team drastically improves.

## What It Illustrates
A sales director's primary job is performance management and culture, not being the best individual closer.
