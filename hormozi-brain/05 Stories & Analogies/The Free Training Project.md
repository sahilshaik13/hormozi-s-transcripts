# The Free Training Project

> that's how I advertised to start my first business using warm Outreach

## The Story
At 21, Hormozi wanted to start a business but was afraid to charge money. He offered free online training to anyone who donated $500-$1000 to a charity. He reached out to his 101 friends, got 6 yeses, got them results, and eventually asked them to pay him directly.

## What It Illustrates
Warm outreach is the cheapest and most certain way to get your first customers and start a business.
