# Henry Ford's CMO

> We get so sick of our advertising so much before our customers or potential customers even remember our names

## The Story
Henry Ford's CMO walked past the same marketing campaign outside his office for 3 months. Exhausted by it, he asked Ford when they could stop running it. Ford replied that they hadn't even started running it yet.

## What It Illustrates
You will get sick of your own advertising long before your customers even know you exist or remember your message.
