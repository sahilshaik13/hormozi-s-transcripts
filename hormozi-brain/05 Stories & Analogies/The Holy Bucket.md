# The Holy Bucket

> why are there so many holes in my bucket because just pouring more water into the holy bucket like everyone understands this conceptually but we don't act that way

## The Story
Businesses constantly try to acquire new leads to grow, which is like pouring more water into a bucket full of holes.

## What It Illustrates
You must fix the product (plug the holes) before spending more on marketing (pouring water).
