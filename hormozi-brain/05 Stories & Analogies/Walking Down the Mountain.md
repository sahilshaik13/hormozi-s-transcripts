# Walking Down the Mountain

> Sometimes you have to walk down the mountain to walk it back up.

## The Story
You climb a hill and think you've reached the peak. Then the clouds clear, and you see a much larger mountain in the distance. To climb the real mountain, you first have to walk back down the hill you just climbed.

## What It Illustrates
Scaling often requires taking a temporary step backward in revenue to build a better foundation.
