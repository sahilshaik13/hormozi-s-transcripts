# The Ohio Real Estate Scam

> his business model was buy it for 19 flip it to 50 to people who don't know any better that was me

## The Story
Hormozi bought a $50k house sight unseen from a friend's contact. It had no electricity, a fake bedroom in a closet, and a ruined driveway. He later found out the seller bought it for $19k and flipped it to him with no improvements.

## What It Illustrates
If an investment sounds too good to be true, it is. Don't invest outside your sphere of confidence.
