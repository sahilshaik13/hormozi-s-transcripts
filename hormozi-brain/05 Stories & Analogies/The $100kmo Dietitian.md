# The $100k/mo Dietitian

> her really Niche was she talked about how to build insurance companies more profitably

## The Story
A dietitian with only 5,800 Instagram followers made $100,000 a month. She posted highly niched content about billing insurance companies, got very few likes, but made 10 sales a day and manually emailed buyers to upsell them.

## What It Illustrates
A hyper-niched audience with a high-converting backend system is vastly more profitable than a large, generalized following.
