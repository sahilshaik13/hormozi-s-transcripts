# The Time Traveler Pitch

> Let's say you could travel back in time, but you're in a different body, and you see your old self...

## The Story
Imagine traveling back in time to pitch your past self a guaranteed investment like Bitcoin or Nvidia. Even though you know it's a sure thing, your past self doesn't know you. You wouldn't argue; you would use absolute empathy to walk them through their fears.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates the level of patience and empathy required to sell a product you truly believe in to a skeptical prospect.
