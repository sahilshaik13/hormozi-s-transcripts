# The News Headline Analogy

> if you look at the way the headlines look on the news they're still the same and they all they do is they just change the characters

## The Story
News stations use the exact same structural formulas for their headlines every single day. They only change the specific characters and the specific story details.

## What It Illustrates
Ad headlines don't need to be reinvented; you just need to plug new variables into proven formulas.
