# Dad's Medical Journey

> education is the only thing that no one can take from you

## The Story
Hormozi's father fled Iran, failed medical school twice in France due to the language barrier, succeeded in Belgium, and then had to completely restart his residency in the US while working minimum wage jobs.

## What It Illustrates
Skills and education are the only assets that cannot be confiscated, and success often requires enduring long periods of zero visible progress.
