# The Steak Dinner Upsell

> if I give you a steak dinner and then you eat your steak dinner and then I say hey do you want a steak you're like no because the timing's wrong

## The Story
If you give someone a massive steak dinner and then immediately ask if they want another steak, they will say no because they are full. You have to offer the steak when they are starving.

## What It Illustrates
Pitch upsells at the point of greatest need, not right after delivering a massive feast of value like a live event.
