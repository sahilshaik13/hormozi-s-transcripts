# Jacob's Sales School

> I was telling Jacob if you want to be smart about it number one you would just show the data number two you would probably not accept ninety percent of people

## The Story
Hormozi advised his neighbor Jacob on how to build a legitimate sales school. Instead of just selling a course to anyone, Jacob should reject 90% of applicants, give the curriculum away for free, charge for call reviews, and only endorse the absolute best graduates.

## What It Illustrates
Applying the Harvard model to a niche online business creates a talent pipeline that employers will fight over, allowing you to charge premium prices.
