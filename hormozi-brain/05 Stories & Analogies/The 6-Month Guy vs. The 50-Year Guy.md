# The 6-Month Guy vs. The 50-Year Guy

> one guy starts a new business or new side hustle every six months; think about the other guy who does one business for 50 straight years and never does anything different. Who makes more money? You already know.

## The Story
Comparing two hypothetical entrepreneurs: one who starts a new side hustle every six months, and another who works on a single business for 50 straight years without deviating.

## What It Illustrates
Focus and longevity in one vehicle mathematically beat constant pivoting.
