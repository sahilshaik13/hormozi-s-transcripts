# The Envelope of Cash

> worst case scenario don't afford food you lose some weight I was like win win

## The Story
A prospect was terrified to hand over an envelope of cash for a weight loss program because it was her grocery money. Hormozi used humor to play out the worst-case scenario: if she couldn't afford food, she'd lose weight anyway.

## What It Illustrates
Playing out the worst-case scenario logically and with humor diffuses the intense emotional fear of making a purchase.
