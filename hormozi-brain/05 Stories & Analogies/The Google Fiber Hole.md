# The Google Fiber Hole

> we paid Google Fiber to drill a hole into the wall and here we are now they can up it's faster here now

## The Story
The editing team wasn't working in the office because their home internet upload speeds were faster. Instead of getting mad, Hormozi paid Google Fiber to drill a hole in the wall to increase the office speed.

## What It Illustrates
When you identify a legitimate blocker preventing your team from working optimally, just spend the money or effort to remove it.
