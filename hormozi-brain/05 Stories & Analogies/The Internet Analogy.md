# The Internet Analogy

> almost every business is on the internet. You don't have to be an internet business.

## The Story
In the early days, people thought they had to become 'internet businesses' to survive. In reality, every business just uses the internet as a tool to deliver what they sell.

## What It Illustrates
You don't need to pivot into becoming an AI tech company; you just need to use AI as a utility to deliver your core value better.
