# Nike and Michael Jordan

> they bet their entire basketball budget so they took what they were going to pay five to 10 players and just gave it to one

## The Story
Nike was struggling in the basketball market, losing players to Converse and Adidas. They bet their entire 5-10 player sponsorship budget on one rookie, Michael Jordan, hoping for $2M in sales. They did $122M in year one.

## What It Illustrates
Going all-in on the right super influencer can create an iconic brand and generate life-changing, disproportionate returns.
