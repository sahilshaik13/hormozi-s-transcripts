# The Bridge of Bricks

> if you see the education you have as these bricks on the road then you wouldn't say that the foundation of a house is in some way a ripoff

## The Story
Education is like buying bricks to build a bridge to money. If you buy a course and fail to reach the money, you might blame the course. But the reality is you are just missing foundational bricks that successful students already possessed from prior experiences.

## What It Illustrates
Don't blame the teacher if a strategy fails; identify the missing foundational skills you lack and acquire them.
