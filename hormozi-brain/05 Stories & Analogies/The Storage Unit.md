# The Storage Unit

> So, we advertise the first month as free, and it is. But what’s the first thing you need after you get a storage unit?

## The Story
A storage facility owner offers the first month free. However, customers immediately need a lock, boxes, tape, dollies, and insurance—all of which the owner sells at a premium.

## What It Illustrates
An attraction offer gets them in the door, but the real profit is made by immediately upselling solutions to the new problems the attraction offer creates.
