# The Begging Dog

> If on the sixth time you give it to them again, you've just taught them that they have to wait for extended periods of time.

## The Story
If you resist a begging dog for five days but give them food on the sixth day, you haven't taught them to stop begging. You've taught them that they just have to wait longer to get the reward.

## What It Illustrates
Intermittent reinforcement builds frustration tolerance; entrepreneurs must train themselves to endure longer periods of failure before getting the reward.
