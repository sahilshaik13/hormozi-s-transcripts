# The Salon Owner's Gift Card

> do you want me to take accountability for the fact that the gift card players worked for so many businesses and just not yours

## The Story
A salon owner complained Hormozi's gift card campaign didn't work. Upon questioning, it was revealed the owner didn't follow the instructions, letting customers use the cards on themselves instead of requiring referrals.

## What It Illustrates
Negative passive execution (half-assing) leads to failure, which the user then blames on the system rather than their own lack of adherence.
