# The College Reputation

> why don't you stop being a piece of [ __ ]

## The Story
In college, Hormozi had a bad reputation for drinking and sleeping around. He asked his dad how to change the narrative via PR, and his dad bluntly told him to stop acting like a piece of garbage.

## What It Illustrates
You cannot control your reputation externally; you must change your internal behavior to earn your own respect first.
