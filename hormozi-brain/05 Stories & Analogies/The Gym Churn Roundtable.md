# The Gym Churn Roundtable

> here's what i did to solve a problem that i did not know the answer to

## The Story
Hormozi realized gym owners were losing 68% of their clients a year, making their businesses unsellable. Not knowing the answer himself, he polled thousands of owners to find the 20 who maintained under 3% churn for six months. He interviewed them, cross-referenced their strategies, and discovered five universal actions they all took.

## What It Illustrates
All the solutions to your business problems already exist within the community of top performers; you just have to extract and consolidate them.
