# The Company as the Mule

> the company is the goose the company is the is the is the is the beast that feeds everyone it is the thing that plows the field it is the mule that everyone is fed off of

## The Story
The company is the beast of burden that plows the field and feeds everyone involved, including shareholders, customers, and employees.

## What It Illustrates
You must protect the health of the company above individual employee desires, because if the company dies, everyone starves.
