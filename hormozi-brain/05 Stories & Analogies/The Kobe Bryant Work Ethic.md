# The Kobe Bryant Work Ethic

> when he clocks out is when your work clocks in

## The Story
Kobe did three practices a day while others did one or two. He started out worse, eventually got equal to them, and then became unbeatable because his rate of progress was mathematically higher.

## What It Illustrates
To beat the top performer, you must match their effort just to keep up, and then add extra effort on top of that to actually get ahead.
