# The Slapping Strangers Analogy

> if you go up to strangers on the street and slap them and run away you're going to get a ton of views because it's crazy viral stuff but you're not going to get the right types of views

## The Story
If you go up to strangers on the street, slap them, and run away, you will get a ton of viral views. However, unless you sell a course on how to slap strangers, those views will not convert into money.

## What It Illustrates
Viral content is useless for business if it attracts an audience that doesn't align with your offer.
