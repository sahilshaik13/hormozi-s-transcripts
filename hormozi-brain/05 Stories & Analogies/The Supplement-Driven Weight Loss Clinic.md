# The Supplement-Driven Weight Loss Clinic

> they essentially give the service away for free but they were required to use the supplements as a part of it

## The Story
A friend owned a weight loss clinic franchise where they gave the actual training service away for free, but required clients to buy their supplements. They made 95% of their revenue on the supplements.

## What It Illustrates
You can offer a service for 'free forever' if you successfully bolt on and monetize a secondary need.
