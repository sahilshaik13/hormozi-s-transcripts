# The Newspaper Turnaround

> The need to believes are that newspapers with their inherent business model are going to continue to exist in order for that business to be viable.

## The Story
A friend built software to transition print newspaper ads to digital. The software worked perfectly, but the newspapers went out of business anyway because the market shifted.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates 'bad hard'—when the underlying assumption of the market is false, no amount of hard work will save it, and you must pivot.
