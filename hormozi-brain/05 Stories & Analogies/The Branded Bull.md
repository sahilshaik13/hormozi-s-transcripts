# The Branded Bull

> fundamentally the point of branding is to change behavior of the person who Sees it

## The Story
Historically, unmarked bulls were treated the same. Once a bull was branded with a logo, people treated it differently based on their relationship with the owner. If they liked the owner, they treated it well; if they hated the owner, they might steal or kill it.

## What It Illustrates
Branding is simply a marker of past experience that changes the future behavior of the consumer.
