# Tesla's Top-Down Strategy

> If I say, 'Hey, I've got this really expensive car... And then I say, Hey guys, many of you couldn't afford this, so I made another car that's similar but more affordable for you.' That brand narrative works because you've anchored high.

## The Story
Tesla didn't start by making a cheap car for the masses. They started with the $250,000 Roadster, then the Model S, and finally the more affordable Model 3.

## What It Illustrates
Anchoring high creates a powerful brand narrative. It is much easier to start ultra-premium and move down-market than to start as a budget discounter and try to sell something expensive.
