# The Dead Optimist

> all the optimists are dead, right? All those genes ended.

## The Story
In ancient times, the optimistic caveman who ate the unknown berries died, ending his genetic line, while the pessimist survived.

## What It Illustrates
Humans are biologically wired to overestimate downside risk, which holds us back in the modern world where the 'worst case' is rarely death.
