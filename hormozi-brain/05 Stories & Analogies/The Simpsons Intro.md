# The Simpsons Intro

> small variation same introduction you let the new people in and you give something to the people who already know you

## The Story
The Simpsons has used the exact same theme song and intro structure for decades, but they change one tiny detail every episode (like Bart writing on the chalkboard).

## What It Illustrates
A consistent introduction builds positive associations and caters to new viewers, while a slight variation rewards loyal fans with an Easter egg.
