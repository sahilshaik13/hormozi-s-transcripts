# Go Where the Fish Are

> you want to go where the fish are right you want to go where where the getting is good

## The Story
When fishing, you don't cast your line in an empty pond; you go exactly where the fish are actively biting.

## What It Illustrates
You should deploy your marketing resources into the platforms that are currently booming and underpriced (like TikTok Shop).
