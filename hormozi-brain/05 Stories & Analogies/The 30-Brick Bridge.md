# The 30-Brick Bridge

> when you're 28 bricks in you might not have made the first dollar yet but you're 90 of the way there

## The Story
Building a business is like laying bricks to build a bridge. You might lay 28 out of 30 bricks and still make zero dollars because the bridge isn't complete. People get disheartened, abandon the bridge, and start building a new one.

## What It Illustrates
Success requires completing the entire sequence of skills/actions; partial completion yields zero results, leading to a graveyard of half-built bridges.
