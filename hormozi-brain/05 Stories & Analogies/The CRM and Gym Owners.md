# The CRM and Gym Owners

> these businesses in and of themselves just stopped being in business

## The Story
A CRM company had high churn despite having a sticky product. They discovered that a third of their gym owner customers were simply going out of business every year.

## What It Illustrates
Churn can be structural to the market, meaning you can have a great product but still fail if your customers fail.
