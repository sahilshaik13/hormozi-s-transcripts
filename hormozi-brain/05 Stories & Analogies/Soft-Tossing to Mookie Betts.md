# Soft-Tossing to Mookie Betts

> if I'm feeding softballs to somebody then I don't need Ken griffy to hit it out of the park

## The Story
If you are feeding incredibly warm, highly qualified leads to a sales team, you are essentially throwing softballs. You don't need to hire and overpay a superstar baseball player like Mookie Betts or Ken Griffey Jr. to hit them out of the park.

## What It Illustrates
Match your compensation and talent requirements to the difficulty of the sale.
