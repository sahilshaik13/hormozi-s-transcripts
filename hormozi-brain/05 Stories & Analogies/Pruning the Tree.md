# Pruning the Tree

> They're not willing to cut the branches off to let the whole thing grow.

## The Story
A gardener must cut off certain branches of a tree or plants in a garden to allow the whole organism to grow larger and healthier.

## What It Illustrates
You must cut away distractions, smaller projects, and bad customers to allow your main business to reach its full potential.
