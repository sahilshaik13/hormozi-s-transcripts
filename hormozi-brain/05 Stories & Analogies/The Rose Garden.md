# The Rose Garden

> imagine a a Garden of Flowers where it's all roses all red roses it'd be a very strong association

## The Story
A garden with only red roses has a very strong, clear association. A garden with roses, daffodils, sunflowers, and weeds is chaotic and hard to define.

## What It Illustrates
A brand must be pruned to maintain a single, strong association rather than a chaotic mix of unrelated topics.
