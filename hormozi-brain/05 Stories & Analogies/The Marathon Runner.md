# The Marathon Runner

> she's in a buying window she's in a hyper buying frenzy and we need to capitalize on it in that moment

## The Story
If someone decides to run a marathon, they immediately buy shoes, shorts, a tank top, and headphones. If you only sell them shoes and wait 30 days to offer the shorts, they will have already bought them from a competitor.

## What It Illustrates
Customers buy in concentrated windows when adopting a new identity; you must offer all related solutions immediately.
