# The Gym Nutrition Upsell

> they wanted to lose weight still they just didn't feel like sweating they wanted to take supplements and eat differently

## The Story
Customers came into the gym but refused to buy fitness services because they didn't want to sweat. Hormozi offered them a nutrition seminar instead, and they ended up spending more than the gym-goers.

## What It Illustrates
If a customer says no to one offer, make a different offer to solve the same problem in a way they prefer.
