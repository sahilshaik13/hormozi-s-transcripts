# Selling the Vacation, Not the Flight

> Sell the vacation not the plane flight.

## The Story
When selling a trip to Maui, you don't talk about waking up early, TSA lines, crying babies, and layovers. You talk about the rainy, miserable weather they are in now (Baltimore), and the white sand, clear water, and cabana boys waiting for them in Maui.

## What It Illustrates
Sales pitches should focus on the pain of the current situation and the pleasure of the destination, not the painful mechanics of the process.
