# The Magic Business Genie

> that additional ten thousand dollars a month is pure profit which means we took our our profit from two thousand dollars a month to twelve thousand dollars a month which means we six x our income

## The Story
A genie offers to double your new customers, double your prices, or double your customer retention. Doubling the price is the winning choice because it requires no extra fulfillment cost, sending all new revenue straight to profit.

## What It Illustrates
Raising prices is the most powerful lever for increasing profit margins and personal income.
