# The Tallest Building

> you're going to block out the Sun and your Shadow will be so big that no one can even see these people

## The Story
Instead of trying to knock down all your competitors' buildings, just build the biggest building. Your shadow will be so large it blocks out the sun and no one will even see them.

## What It Illustrates
Shrink your competition by growing so large you drown them out.
