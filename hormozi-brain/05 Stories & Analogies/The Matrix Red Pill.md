# The Matrix Red Pill

> you've been down that road Neo you know that road and you know exactly where it ends

## The Story
Trinity tells Neo he knows exactly where the old road ends and that it's not where he wants to be, stopping him from getting out of the car to return to his old life.

## What It Illustrates
When tempted to go back to your old partying friends and comfortable life, remember why you left and that returning leads to a dead end.
