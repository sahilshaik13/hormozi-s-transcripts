# The Hangover Illusion

> punishment decreases in its Effectiveness over time you quickly forget and then you do it again

## The Story
After a night of heavy drinking, you swear you will never drink again. But by the next weekend, you drink again because the memory of the hangover fades, while the memory of the fun remains.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates why we forget the negative aspects of going home and only remember the positive traditions, tricking us into returning to toxic environments.
