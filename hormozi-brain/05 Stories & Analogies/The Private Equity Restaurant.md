# The Private Equity Restaurant

> I could already imagine how this board meeting went.

## The Story
A restaurant added a tiny 4% processing fee to the bottom of their menu. For a business with 9% net margins, this 4% top-line addition dropped straight to the bottom line, increasing total net profit by 44% overnight.

## What It Illustrates
Small percentage increases in price create disproportionately massive increases in net profit.
