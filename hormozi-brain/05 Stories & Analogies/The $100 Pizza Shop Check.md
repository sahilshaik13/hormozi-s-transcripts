# The $100 Pizza Shop Check

> I didn't sell her. I didn't say a price; she just handed me $100 for the amount of help that I'd given her.

## The Story
Hormozi helped a lady from his gym with her protein intake recipes at a pizza shop. After an hour and a half of just helping her, she unexpectedly wrote him a $100 check without him ever asking for money.

## What It Illustrates
People will gladly pay for solutions to their specific problems, even if you don't formally 'sell' them.
