# Laila's Loyalty in Hawaii

> when my world was Crum crumbling around me and I really needed her to come through she stood tall

## The Story
When Alex was broke and breaking up with Laila, she still flew to Hawaii, lived in an Airbnb with five guys, and ran his biggest gym launch ever. Her loyalty saved his business.

## What It Illustrates
True loyalty is shown when things are falling apart, and it earns lifelong commitment.
