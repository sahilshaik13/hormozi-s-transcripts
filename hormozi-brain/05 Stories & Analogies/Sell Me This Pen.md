# Sell Me This Pen

> if they say sell me this pen what you do is you take the pen and you put it in your pocket and you say how's it going what brought you in today

## The Story
Bad salespeople will just tell you to buy the pen. Good salespeople will put the pen in their pocket and ask you what brought you in today to understand your needs first.

## What It Illustrates
Sales is about understanding the person, not pitching the product.
