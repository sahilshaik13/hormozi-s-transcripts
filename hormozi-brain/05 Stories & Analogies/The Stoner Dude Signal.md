# The Stoner Dude Signal

> what the [ __ ] is the school games I wrote it down that was the problem

## The Story
Sam was struggling to figure out why the School Games funnel wasn't converting. A stoner friend from New Zealand texted him asking, 'What the f*** is the school games?' Sam realized that if people didn't already know what School was, the marketing made zero sense.

## What It Illustrates
Defining the actual problem is the hardest part of improving a product; use extreme cases (like a stoner or a child) to test the clarity of your messaging.
