# The $3,000 YouTube Editor

> he was in a season of learning not a season of earning

## The Story
Hormozi offered a job to a YouTube editor who countered for an extra $3,000 a year. The editor missed the opportunity to learn highly valuable skills from a top creator for a marginal pay bump.

## What It Illustrates
Don't sacrifice massive long-term learning opportunities for tiny short-term earning increases.
