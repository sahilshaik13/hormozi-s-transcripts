# The Epictetus Rock

> how do you get angry at a rock you can call a rock names you can shout at it you can stop your feet but the rock just stays a rock

## The Story
You can yell at a rock, call it names, and stomp your feet, but the rock just stays a rock and does not react to your anger.

## What It Illustrates
When people throw tantrums about your boundaries, you should remain completely unbothered and unchanging.
