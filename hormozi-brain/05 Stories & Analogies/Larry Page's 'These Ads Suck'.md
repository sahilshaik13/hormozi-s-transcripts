# Larry Page's 'These Ads Suck'

> he didn't sit down with him and be like look I think you need to do this and this and this and maybe this he just said these ads suck

## The Story
In the early days of Google, Larry Page printed out irrelevant search ads, wrote 'These ads suck' in red marker, and pinned it to the wall near the engineers. He didn't call a meeting. One engineer saw it, stayed up all night, and invented AdSense.

## What It Illustrates
Top-tier talent doesn't need micro-management, meetings, or step-by-step communication; they just need the problem clearly identified.
