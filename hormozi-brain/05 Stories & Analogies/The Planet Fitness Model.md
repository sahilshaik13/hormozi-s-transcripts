# The Planet Fitness Model

> We think, 'Oh, I'm going to add more stuff to provide more value.' But all you do is create a larger discrepancy between consumption and lack of consumption.

## The Story
Old health clubs offered pools, courts, and weights for a high price, but 80% of people only used cardio. Planet Fitness stripped away the unused features, catered to the 80%, and lowered the price.

## What It Illustrates
Adding features people don't use doesn't add value; it creates a discrepancy where customers feel they aren't getting their money's worth.
