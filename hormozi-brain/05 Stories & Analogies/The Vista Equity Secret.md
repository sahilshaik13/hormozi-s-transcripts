# The Vista Equity Secret

> we just know our our acquired companies's customers better than they do

## The Story
The head of packaging at a $100B private equity firm revealed their secret: they buy software companies, find the best customer cohort in the data room, turn off marketing to everyone else, and scale only that avatar.

## What It Illustrates
Extreme discipline in customer selection and marketing focus drives massive enterprise value.
