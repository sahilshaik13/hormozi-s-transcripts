# The House vs. The Business

> there is no piece of real estate on Earth that you can build for zero dollars and then it will someday be worth billions

## The Story
A house has a fixed bottom because of debt and a fixed ceiling because you can't move it to a better neighborhood. A business, however, can be started for zero dollars and scaled infinitely.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates the infinite upside and superior wealth-creation potential of private equity over real estate.
