# The Nike Price Tag

> either get the Nikes or don't complain about the price you can't have both

## The Story
If you choose to buy a pair of Nikes that cost $500, you pay the $500. You cannot buy the shoes and then complain about the price tag after the fact.

## What It Illustrates
You cannot choose an ambitious partner and then complain about the time and sacrifice required for their ambition.
