# The Yelp Hair Salon

> which one do you think she went to the 4.6 stars with 1,000 reviews

## The Story
Hormozi's wife needed a hair salon while traveling. She compared a 5-star salon with 20 reviews against a 4.6-star salon with 1,000 reviews. She chose the 4.6-star salon because the sheer volume of reviews provided more certainty.

## What It Illustrates
Volume of reviews often outweighs a perfect score, making it highly profitable to indirectly pay for review generation.
