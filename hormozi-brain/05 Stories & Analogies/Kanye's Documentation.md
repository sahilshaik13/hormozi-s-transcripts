# Kanye's Documentation

> And so you like Kanye was documenting in the very early days cuz he believed so hardcore that he was going to win, right?

## The Story
Kanye West filmed himself constantly during his early, unknown days because he had absolute conviction in his future success.

## What It Illustrates
If you truly believe you are going to win, you should document your early struggles because they will become the prologue to your success story.
