# The Commercial Building Software

> Let's just figure out how we can get 500 demonstrations turn into 100 deals instead of two. It's a 50x right there.

## The Story
A software company was stuck at the same revenue for 8 years, closing only 2 out of 500 demos. They wanted to double their sales team. Instead, they realized they were pitching cost-savings to building managers who didn't care about the owner's money and just didn't want extra work.

## What It Illustrates
A massive leak in demand is often a sales process problem (pitching the wrong avatar), not a volume problem.
