# The 24 vs 6 Jam Jars

> the 24 jars of gy had more people try them but less people buy and then the six got way more buyers like two or three times more buyers

## The Story
A supermarket tested displaying 24 jars of jam versus 6 jars. The 24 jars attracted more people to try them, but the 6 jars resulted in 2 to 3 times more actual buyers.

## What It Illustrates
Giving customers too many choices creates a paradox of choice that kills conversions; focused offers win.
