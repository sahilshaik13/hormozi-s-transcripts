# The Creator and the Blacksmith

> just like we measure the quality of a blacksmith by the strength of his steel I measure you by what you are at the end not the fire and the hammer

## The Story
A person asks the Creator for courage, patience, wisdom, and a good life. Instead of giving these directly, the Creator gives them monsters to fight, hard work, crushing failures, and a hard life. The Creator explains that a person is measured like steel—by what they become at the end, not by the fire and hammer it took to forge them.

## What It Illustrates
A good life is not an easy life; it is a hard life that forges you into a capable person.
