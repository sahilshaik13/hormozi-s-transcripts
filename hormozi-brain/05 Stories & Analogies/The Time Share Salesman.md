# The Time Share Salesman

> if you give me the best leads instead of wasting them on these guys who are new he's like I will make you so much money

## The Story
A top rep won a contest and got an hour with the CEO. He asked to only be fed the absolute best leads instead of wasting them on new guys. He 5x'd his income, and when the company rolled the strategy out nationwide, they 5x'd the business.

## What It Illustrates
Allocating the best leads to the best closers creates massive leverage and revenue growth.
