# The Levi's Jean Jacket Demonstration

> my perceived likelihood of achieving the dream outcome of looking cool has just gone up simply by them making a video that demonstrated it

## The Story
Levi's created a 90-second video showing three different ways to style a jean jacket with dark and light clothes. This simple demonstration taught the customer exactly how to wear it.

## What It Illustrates
Demonstrating how to use a physical product increases the customer's perceived likelihood of achieving the dream outcome (looking cool).
