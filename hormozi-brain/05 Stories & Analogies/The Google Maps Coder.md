# The Google Maps Coder

> one really high leverage coder remade the entire product over a weekend and then that is ultimately what became the codebase that we still use today

## The Story
Google bought an old version of Maps. One highly leveraged coder remade the entire product over a weekend, creating the codebase still used today.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates the outsized returns of top-tier talent in high-leverage roles, justifying paying them significantly more.
