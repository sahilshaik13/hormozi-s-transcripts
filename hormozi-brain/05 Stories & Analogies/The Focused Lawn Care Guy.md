# The Focused Lawn Care Guy

> who is going to win at lawn care it's obvious and yet we still live like this other guy

## The Story
One guy mows lawns all day every day because he has a deep emotional connection to giving people great lawns. Another guy does lawn care, dropshipping, consulting, and NFTs all at once.

## What It Illustrates
The person with singular focus and dedication to the basics will absolutely demolish the distracted hustler.
