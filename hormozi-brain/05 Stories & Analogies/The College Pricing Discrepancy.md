# The College Pricing Discrepancy

> when you go in person in college they charge you a arm and a leg for it but digital College almost ubiquitously is way way way lower priced even though it's the same information

## The Story
Universities charge an arm and a leg for in-person classes, but significantly less for digital versions of the exact same curriculum.

## What It Illustrates
The medium of delivery dictates the perceived value of the information, allowing you to re-price and re-sell the same core product.
