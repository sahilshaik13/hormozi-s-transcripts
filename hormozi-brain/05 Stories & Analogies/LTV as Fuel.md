# LTV as Fuel

> If advertising is the machine that makes a business grow, LTV is the fuel.

## The Story
Advertising is compared to a machine or engine that makes a business grow, while Lifetime Value is the fuel that powers that machine.

## What It Illustrates
You cannot scale your advertising machine effectively if you do not have the high-LTV fuel to fund it.
