# The Drunk Bathroom Floor

> most of this suffering when I look back on this will be very, very short in terms of the memory, even though this may be 4 hours of me wrapped around a toilet.

## The Story
When throwing up from drinking too much, Hormozi realized in the moment that he wouldn't remember the 4 hours of misery wrapped around the toilet.

## What It Illustrates
Suffering is temporary and will compress into a tiny, insignificant memory, making it easier to endure.
