# The Baseball Stadium of Gold

> it doesn't matter what you do to that big ball of gold a 100 years from now it'll still be a big ball of gold

## The Story
Warren Buffett noted that all the gold in the world would form a giant sphere in a baseball stadium, but in 100 years, it will still just be a sphere of gold doing nothing. The same money could buy all US farmland and 13 ExxonMobils, which produce continuous output.

## What It Illustrates
Storing wealth in non-productive assets is inferior to buying productive assets that generate continuous economic output.
