# The Glass Tug-of-War

> You have to get through that glass period where you pull it towards you.

## The Story
Life is a bloody game of tug-of-war where the rope is made of coarse glass in the beginning. As you pull it towards you, it cuts your hands, but eventually, the rope becomes neutral, and then sweeter and smoother.

## What It Illustrates
You must endure the initial, excruciating pain of hard work and sacrifice to eventually reach the smooth, rewarding part of life.
