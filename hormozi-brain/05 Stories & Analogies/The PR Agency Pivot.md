# The PR Agency Pivot

> we cut 85% we catered the messaging and we made it way more Niche down to this specific Avatar and we 10x the price

## The Story
A PR company had 85% churn within 2-3 months, but 15% of customers paid the most and stayed the longest. They cut the 85%, catered exclusively to the 15%, and 10x'd their prices.

## What It Illustrates
Strategic prioritization and niching down can 10x a company's net worth, even if it requires going to zero temporarily.
