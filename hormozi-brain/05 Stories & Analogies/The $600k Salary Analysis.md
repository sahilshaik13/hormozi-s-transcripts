# The $600k Salary Analysis

> she literally used company resources probably a hundred thousand dollars of payroll resources to create this solution that then just paid out more money from the company

## The Story
An overstaffed, underworked HR director spent 90 days analyzing salaries. She concluded 25% of the company was underpaid and gave them raises without adjusting the overpaid staff, costing the company $600,000 in profit for zero added value.

## What It Illustrates
Never increase compensation unless it solves a specific attraction or retention problem; otherwise, you are just giving away company profit for nothing.
