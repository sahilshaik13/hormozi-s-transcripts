# The Born-Again Christian

> they do not care how many people tell them to shove it because they are trying to save people's souls

## The Story
A born-again Christian doesn't care how many times they get rejected or told to shove it, because they genuinely believe they are saving people's souls.

## What It Illustrates
If you have true conviction in your product, you will sell hard and handle rejection easily because you know you are helping them.
