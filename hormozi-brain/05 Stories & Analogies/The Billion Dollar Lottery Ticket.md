# The Billion Dollar Lottery Ticket

> I realized that if I won that I would never be able to achieve anything that anyone thought was my own

## The Story
Hormozi bought a lottery ticket in college and felt sheer terror at the thought of winning, realizing unearned wealth would rob him of achieving anything on his own.

## What It Illustrates
Hardship and the struggle to build yourself are the actual prize; unearned success destroys your potential to see what you are truly made of.
