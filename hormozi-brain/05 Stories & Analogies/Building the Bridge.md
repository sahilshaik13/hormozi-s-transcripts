# Building the Bridge

> the last thing you did just gave you the one last brick you needed to finish the bridge

## The Story
A person takes three different courses and fails, then takes a fourth and succeeds. They credit the fourth course entirely, not realizing the first three gave them the foundational bricks needed to cross the bridge.

## What It Illustrates
Skills compound. Don't discount early education just because it didn't yield the final result immediately.
