# The Education System's Continuity

## The Story
Education naturally graduates people once they learn the material. To keep making money, the system invents new degrees like Masters and PhDs to keep people in the system.

## What It Illustrates
If your service solves a one-time problem, you must invent new ongoing problems to solve, or accept that it's not a recurring revenue business.
