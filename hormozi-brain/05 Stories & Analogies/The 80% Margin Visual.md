# The 80% Margin Visual

> But that sounds greedy when you're like, 'Oh my god, there's 80.' It's like, yeah, but we got to do all the other costs out of 80 and then whatever's left is our profit.

## The Story
If a service costs $20 to fulfill and you charge $100, you have $80 left. But out of that $80, you must pay for marketing, admin, and sales, which might cost $60. This leaves a 20% net profit.

## What It Illustrates
High gross margins are not greedy; they are mathematically required to cover operating expenses and yield a healthy net profit.
