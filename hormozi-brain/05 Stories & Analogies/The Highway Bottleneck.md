# The Highway Bottleneck

> If I take this one from four lanes to five lanes, is the traffic going to go faster? No.

## The Story
Imagine a highway that goes from four lanes, down to one lane, and back to four lanes. If you spend time and money expanding the initial four lanes into five lanes, the traffic won't move any faster.

## What It Illustrates
Improving parts of your business that are not the primary constraint only increases potential, not actual throughput.
