# The 56 Companies Excel Sheet

> he had like 56 companies that in total were making like $10 million a year

## The Story
An entrepreneur making $10M/year across 56 different companies was asked how easy it would be to 5x his biggest company if he dropped the other 55. He realized it would be a joke, ended the other partnerships, and massively grew the main one.

## What It Illustrates
Lack of priority, not lack of information, is what holds entrepreneurs back.
