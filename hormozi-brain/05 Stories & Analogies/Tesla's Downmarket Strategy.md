# Tesla's Downmarket Strategy

> he started with the Roadster which was the most expensive version of the car because he knew that the fewest amount of people would be able to afford it

## The Story
Elon Musk started with the expensive Roadster for a tiny market, then used the profits to build the Model S, then Model X, and finally the mass-market Model 3.

## What It Illustrates
When starting with no resources, sell an expensive product to a small, specific niche before going broad to the mass market.
