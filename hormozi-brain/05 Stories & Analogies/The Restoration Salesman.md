# The Restoration Salesman

> that guy all day long was knocking on doors walking in the front door with donuts asking them how they're doing

## The Story
A restoration company had a star salesman whose only job was visiting tradesmen. He brought them donuts and coffee, reminded them he existed, and paid them $1000 for referrals while keeping $500 for himself.

## What It Illustrates
The power of having a dedicated affiliate manager who consistently stokes the fire of B2B relationships.
