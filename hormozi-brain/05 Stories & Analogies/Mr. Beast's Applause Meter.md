# Mr. Beast's Applause Meter

> when the live streamers came out, it was like the entire auditorium or stadium or arena erupted.

## The Story
At a creator soccer game, traditional A-list celebrities got no applause, shorts creators got some, long-form creators got rowdy cheers, and live-streamers made the stadium erupt.

## What It Illustrates
The rawest, longest forms of media create the deepest audience connection and influence.
