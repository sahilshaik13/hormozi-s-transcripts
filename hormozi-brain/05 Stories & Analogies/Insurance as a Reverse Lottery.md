# Insurance as a Reverse Lottery

> insurance is a reverse lottery. Is that everyone is paying for a reverse ticket that if they get the bad thing to happen, everyone else chips in.

## The Story
Insurance is a system where everyone buys a ticket, and if something bad happens to one person, everyone else chips in to cover it.

## What It Illustrates
Selling risk is a highly lucrative business model because you get paid continuously when nothing happens.
