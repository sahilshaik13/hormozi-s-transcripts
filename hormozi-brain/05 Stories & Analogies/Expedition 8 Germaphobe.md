# Expedition 8 Germaphobe

> all of the energy that I had been putting into all of this concern around germs amounted to nothing.

## The Story
As an 8th-grade germaphobe, Hormozi went on a 5-day camping trip, decided to stop washing his hands, ate dirt, and didn't get sick or die.

## What It Illustrates
The things we obsessively fear (like germs or other people's opinions) rarely harm us, and the energy spent worrying is entirely wasted.
