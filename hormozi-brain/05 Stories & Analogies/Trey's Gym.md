# Trey's Gym

> Trey’s business went from bleeding $2,000 a month to pocketing $4,000 in profit. And all this only took a week to roll out.

## The Story
Trey charged $49/mo to help everyone, but attracted terrible customers and bled money. He tripled prices to save the gym, lost some members, but went from losing $2k/mo to profiting $4k/mo in a week.

## What It Illustrates
You don't need to wait for disaster to raise prices, and losing some customers for a higher price is highly profitable.
