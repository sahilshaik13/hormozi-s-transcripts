# The 11 AM Cold Plunger

> he's like by the time i'm done my morning routine it's like 11 a.m and i was like do you think you'd be more productive if you just prospected for three hours a day

## The Story
A guy hired a coach who gave him a morning routine consisting of walks, red light therapy, cold plunges, and a gratitude journal. By the time he finished, it was 11 AM.

## What It Illustrates
Adding non-work tasks to your morning decreases productivity; you are better off spending those hours actually doing the work, like prospecting.
