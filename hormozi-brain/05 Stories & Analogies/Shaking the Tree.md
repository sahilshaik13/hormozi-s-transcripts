# Shaking the Tree

> Churn going up right away raises eyebrows – but I call it “shaking the three.”

## The Story
When gyms first implemented the retention checklist, churn spiked by 50% in month one before dropping drastically in months two and three. Reaching out reminded ghost subscribers they were still paying.

## What It Illustrates
Implementing retention systems will cause a temporary spike in cancellations from people who weren't using the product anyway, which is a necessary step to establish a healthy baseline.
