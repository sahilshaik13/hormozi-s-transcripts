# Meditation vs. Xanax

> what if i gave you a pill called xanax you feel chill your perceived likelihood is 100 the time delay is 15 minutes the effort sacrifices you swallow some water and that is why you have a multi-billion dollar product

## The Story
Meditation requires high effort, high sacrifice, and months of time delay for a low perceived likelihood of feeling relaxed. Xanax requires zero effort, 15 minutes of time delay, and has a 100% perceived likelihood of working.

## What It Illustrates
Products that minimize time delay and effort while maximizing certainty of the dream outcome become multi-billion dollar industries.
