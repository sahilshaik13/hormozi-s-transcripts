# The Bouquet of Flowers

> you can strengthen your reputation by putting more and similar types of experiences together

## The Story
A brand is like a bouquet of flowers made of your life experiences and traits. If you add a weed or a broken flower (a scandal or bad association), it ruins the entire bouquet.

## What It Illustrates
Protect your reputation at all costs; one bad decision can permanently taint years of compounding goodwill.
