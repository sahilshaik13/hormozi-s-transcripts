# The Tale of Two Gyms

> So fundamentally, the business that can make more money from its customer than its competitors wins.

## The Story
Competitors ran $21 for 21-day low barrier offers, taking months to break even on a $100 acquisition cost. Hormozi ran 6-week challenges, collecting $600 upfront, plus supplements and paid-in-full upsells, making $1,000 in the first 30 days. Because he made $1,000 while competitors made $21, he could spend infinitely more on ads.

## What It Illustrates
The business that extracts more cash upfront from a customer can outspend competitors and monopolize the market.
