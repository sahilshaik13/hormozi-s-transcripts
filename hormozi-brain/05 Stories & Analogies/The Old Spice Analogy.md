# The Old Spice Analogy

> That’s an ad that wasn’t capped. It was good enough to convert everyone. That’s how you make ads that scale - you make better ads.

## The Story
Hormozi wasn't an Old Spice buyer, but he saw the 'dude on the horse' ads, thought they were funny, and started buying their products. 70% of the market did the same.

## What It Illustrates
Truly great ads aren't capped by a narrow target market; they are good enough to convert everyone.
