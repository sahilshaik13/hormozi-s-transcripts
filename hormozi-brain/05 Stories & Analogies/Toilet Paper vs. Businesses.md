# Toilet Paper vs. Businesses

> businesses are actually the opposite if you put a ton of profit together you get a size premium

## The Story
When buying toilet paper, buying in bulk is cheaper (wholesale discount). When buying businesses, buying in bulk (larger profits) is more expensive (size premium) because institutional buyers have massive minimum check sizes.

## What It Illustrates
Explains the counterintuitive nature of size premiums in business valuations.
