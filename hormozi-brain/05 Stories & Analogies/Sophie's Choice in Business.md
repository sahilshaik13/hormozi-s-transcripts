# Sophie's Choice in Business

> it's being willing to let one of your children die to let the other one live we get the Sophie's choice we actually do have to do that in business all the time

## The Story
To succeed, you have to be willing to let one of your 'children' (business ideas or opportunities) die so that the other one can live and thrive.

## What It Illustrates
Focus requires the painful acceptance that you cannot pursue every great opportunity.
