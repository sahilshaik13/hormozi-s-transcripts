# The Surgeon and the Secretary

> It would be like you asking the secretary how your heart surgery is going to be with the surgeon.

## The Story
Asking a salesperson technical delivery questions is like asking a hospital secretary how your heart surgery is going to go. You need to talk to the surgeon.

## What It Illustrates
Deflects technical questions that the salesperson isn't qualified to answer, preventing them from giving a deal-killing wrong answer.
