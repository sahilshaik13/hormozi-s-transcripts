# The Movie Trailer

> a movie is a great microcosm example of what a brand is in a very short thing

## The Story
A movie studio releases a trailer (what they say). Friends watch the movie and tell you it's great (what others say). You go watch it and love it (what you experience).

## What It Illustrates
Marketing makes the promise, word-of-mouth validates it, and the product delivers it. Businesses must loop this process to survive, unlike movies which are one-off transactions.
