# The Mental Weenie

> A tiny inconvenience sends you spiralally. Low tolerance. Think weeny tolerance.

## The Story
A person with zero out of ten mental toughness. A tiny inconvenience like slow Wi-Fi or bad traffic sends them spiraling. They drastically change their behavior, stay upset for a long time, and permanently lower their baseline.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates the extreme negative end of the mental toughness spectrum to help you identify poor behavioral responses in yourself.
