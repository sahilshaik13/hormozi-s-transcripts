# The Soggy Sandwich

> imagine you go to a restaurant and the sandwiches are mediocre or soggy... you get 50% off a sandwich if you get somebody else are you still like are you going to refer somebody

## The Story
If a restaurant sells a mediocre or soggy sandwich, offering a customer 50% off to refer a friend won't work. The customer won't risk their reputation with their friend for a bad product, regardless of the financial incentive.

## What It Illustrates
A good incentive system can never outweigh a bad product.
