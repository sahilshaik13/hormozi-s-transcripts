# The Toothbrush Metaphor

> what we're doing is we're going to artificially become that external motivation that you need in the beginning to create the Habit

## The Story
As a kid, you hated brushing your teeth, so your parents provided external accountability by forcing you. Now as an adult, you do it automatically because the positive reinforcement became internal motivation.

## What It Illustrates
Coaching and accountability act as the temporary external motivation needed until a habit becomes internal.
