# Ms and Fahad

> in the first 30 days two 20-y old guys with no following and no money got to 44,000 in monthly recurring Revenue

## The Story
Two 20-year-olds with no following and no money partnered with a 50-something former bodybuilder who had 50k followers. They built and managed a paid community for him, doing all the operational work while he just made content.

## What It Illustrates
You don't need money or a following to make life-changing wealth; you just need to provide the operational execution for an influencer's audience.
