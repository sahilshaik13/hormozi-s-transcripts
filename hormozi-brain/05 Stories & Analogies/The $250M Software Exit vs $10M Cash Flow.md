# The $250M Software Exit vs $10M Cash Flow

> He could have done an education business and maybe made $10 million a year for that whole time. He's skilled enough of an entrepreneur. And so the question is, how do you want to make money?

## The Story
A friend lived on $70k/year for 12 years to sell a software company for $250M, while he could have made $10M/year in education the whole time.

## What It Illustrates
Both paths are valid; it's a choice between future enterprise value and current lifestyle cash flow.
