# Sephora Makeup Girls

> with a new identity comes new priorities

## The Story
Two little girls at Sephora were told by an employee that because they are growing into women, they need to start budgeting for makeup every month.

## What It Illustrates
Adopting a new identity requires shifting how you allocate your resources and priorities.
