# The Chicken and the Pig

> The chicken's interested he's like the pig the pig's committed.

## The Story
A football coach asks a player if he is interested or committed to playing college ball. He points to the player's breakfast of eggs and ham. He explains that for the breakfast to happen, the chicken was interested, but the pig was committed.

## What It Illustrates
True commitment requires a permanent sacrifice (eliminating alternatives), whereas interest allows you to keep producing without ultimate sacrifice.
