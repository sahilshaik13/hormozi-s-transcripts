# The Newspaper Funnies

> people buy newspapers because they want to feel educated but what they read is they flip to the back and often read the Funnies

## The Story
People buy newspapers because they want to feel educated and informed. However, the first thing they actually read is the funnies section in the back.

## What It Illustrates
Email marketing should leverage entertainment and humor to drive open rates, even if the underlying goal is to sell a product.
