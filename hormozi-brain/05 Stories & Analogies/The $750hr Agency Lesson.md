# The $750/hr Agency Lesson

> I usually go into the intention with an agency owner to say, 'Hey, here's my intention. I will sign up with you, but I will do it with the intention of learning everything I can from you so I can eventually replace you.'

## The Story
Unable to afford a $5,000/month agency retainer, Hormozi asked the agency owner his hourly rate. He paid $750/hour for weekly screen-share sessions until he mastered running national ad campaigns himself.

## What It Illustrates
Acquiring the skill of customer acquisition is far more valuable than outsourcing it, and experts can be bought for their time to teach you.
