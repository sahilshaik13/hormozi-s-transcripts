# The Restaurant Reviews

> the damaging admissions the reviews that are negative give credence to the things that are positive

## The Story
Comparing a restaurant with 4.7 stars and 3,000 reviews to one with 5 stars and 19 reviews. People trust the 4.7-star restaurant more because the negative reviews give credence to the positive ones.

## What It Illustrates
Imperfection with volume is more believable and trusted than unproven perfection.
