# The Old Spice Commercial

> That ad took Old Spice from a small player in the men's body wash into the majority of the market with one campaign.

## The Story
Old Spice was a small player in men's body wash until they released the 'guy on a horse' commercial. That single, universally appealing ad was so good it captured the majority of the market in one campaign.

## What It Illustrates
Proves that ad spend ceilings are often just creative ceilings; a truly great ad can convert an entire market.
