# The $25k Marketer Call

> Alex, you have a level-10 skill in a level-2 opportunity.

## The Story
Hormozi paid $25,000 to speak with a famous marketer to scale his gyms. After hearing Hormozi's insane unit economics (turning $1 into $34 in 48 hours), the marketer told him to quit running gyms and start teaching other gym owners how to do it.

## What It Illustrates
Sometimes you have a high-level skill trapped in a low-level vehicle; pivoting to a better opportunity can unlock massive wealth.
