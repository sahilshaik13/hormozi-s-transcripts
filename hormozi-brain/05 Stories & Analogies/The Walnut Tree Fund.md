# The Walnut Tree Fund

> I don't want to know where the Venezuelan tree farmers are. I don't have those connections... And so the beauty of this one is that you have maximum leverage.

## The Story
A fund was created solely to plant black walnut trees, wait 30 years, harvest the walnuts after year 3 for cash flow, and eventually sell the premium wood. The fund managers had a highly specific, proprietary edge.

## What It Illustrates
Fund management works best when organized around a singular, proprietary thesis that outside investors cannot easily replicate.
