# Insomnia Cookies' Private Chain

> for them they had tons of margin because it was cookies they were selling flour and sugar for big margins and the amount that a location could make it was tremendous compared to the cost

## The Story
Insomnia Cookies built tiny 800-square-foot locations that only required an oven, a window, and cheap ingredients (flour and sugar). Because the cost to build a location was minuscule compared to the massive profit margins it generated, they owned all the locations themselves.

## What It Illustrates
If the cost to build a location is extremely low relative to its profit, you should own the entire chain privately rather than franchising.
