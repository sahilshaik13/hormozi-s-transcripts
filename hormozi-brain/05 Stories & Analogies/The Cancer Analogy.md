# The Cancer Analogy

> i now have a problem you know i have cancer i can cure it

## The Story
Once you listen to a prospect's symptoms and accurately label their specific problem, it is the equivalent of a doctor diagnosing a disease and offering the exact cure.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates the power of the 'Label' step in sales; diagnosing the problem gives you the authority to prescribe the solution.
