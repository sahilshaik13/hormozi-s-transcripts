# The Charity vs. Business Analogy

> I'm going to say that this isn't really a business. I'm going to say this is a charity.

## The Story
When reviewing a business unit that loses money but is kept alive for 'impact' and 'giving back to the community', it should be labeled a charity, not a business. A business must prioritize profit to survive.

## What It Illustrates
Impact-driven projects that destroy cash flow will eventually kill the host business.
