# The Wonder Bread Sandwich

> because everyone who has your food literally tells everyone that it [ __ ] sucks and never come back

## The Story
People start a business, slap bologna between two pieces of Wonder Bread, market it aggressively, and wonder why they aren't growing. The reality is everyone who tries it tells others it sucks.

## What It Illustrates
Marketing a bad product creates negative word of mouth (the invisible hand), making customer acquisition disproportionately expensive over time.
