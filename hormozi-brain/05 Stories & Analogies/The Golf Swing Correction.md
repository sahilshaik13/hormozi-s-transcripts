# The Golf Swing Correction

> if he just said hey take another 20 swins and just put one thumb under the other one i'd have one thing to work on and i could probably get better

## The Story
If a golf coach tells you to lose 40 pounds, change your wrist angle, move your feet, and adjust your thumb all at once, you will fail to swing. If they tell you to just move one thumb, you can improve.

## What It Illustrates
When coaching salespeople, give them only one specific piece of feedback to implement at a time.
