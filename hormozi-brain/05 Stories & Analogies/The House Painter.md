# The House Painter

> we tie the purchase to the outcome

## The Story
Instead of selling a transactional service ('I will paint your house'), a painter can sell a diagnostic outcome ('You need four coats to weatherproof this house by X date, and if we miss it, I give you Y').

## What It Illustrates
Any transactional service can be repackaged as a custom, goal-oriented solution to increase perceived value and price.
