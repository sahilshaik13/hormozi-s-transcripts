# Warren Buffet & See's Candies

> If he made it the sole decision he was in charge of, you can bet it was probably a good investment of his time.

## The Story
Buffet bought See's Candies and let managers run everything except pricing. Every year for 50+ years, he raised prices by 10-17%.

## What It Illustrates
Raising prices annually is one of the highest-leverage decisions a business owner can make.
