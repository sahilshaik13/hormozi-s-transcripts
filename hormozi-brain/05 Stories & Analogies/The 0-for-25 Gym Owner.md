# The 0-for-25 Gym Owner

> he went over whatever over 20 or something and then something switched and he went five for nine right overnight

## The Story
A gym owner failed to close his first 25 sales calls using a standard script. After realizing he was selling for his own agenda, he shifted his mindset to genuinely caring about the prospect's agenda and immediately went 5 for 9 using the exact same script.

## What It Illustrates
Mindset and genuine care can instantly override a lack of sales skill and transform conversion rates.
