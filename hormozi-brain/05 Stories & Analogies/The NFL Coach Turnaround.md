# The NFL Coach Turnaround

> how can you have one coach go from a losing team in the NFL to almost undefeated team in one year because of the leadership because of the culture

## The Story
A losing team in the NFL can become an almost undefeated team in a single year simply by changing the head coach.

## What It Illustrates
Leadership, culture, and what a manager tolerates dictate a team's success far more than the individual talent of the players.
