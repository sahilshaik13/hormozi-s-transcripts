# The Unbelievable Dollar Ad

> for every dollar you give me i'll give you a thousand dollars back he ran an ad in the paper and he got zero responses

## The Story
A famous marketer ran a newspaper ad offering to give a thousand dollars back for every one dollar given to him. He received zero responses.

## What It Illustrates
An offer that is too good to be true without a logical 'reason why' lacks believability and will fail to convert.
