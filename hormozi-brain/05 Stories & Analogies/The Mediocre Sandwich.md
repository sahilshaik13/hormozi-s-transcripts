# The Mediocre Sandwich

> have you tried your sandwiches they are terrible why would you want more people to know

## The Story
Gym owners constantly ask for new marketing hacks to get leads, but their core product (the sandwich) is terrible. If they just made a great sandwich, they wouldn't need to market it.

## What It Illustrates
Fix the product before you try to scale the marketing. Marketing a bad product just lets everyone know you are mediocre.
