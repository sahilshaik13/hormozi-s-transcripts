# The Roulette Chip

> The Ju way of travel is you find the good deals. There are thousands of them out there. You plan your vacation around them and it's like placing a chip on the roulette table after the marble drops.

## The Story
Planning a vacation to a specific place at a specific time is like placing a chip on a roulette board before the spin and hoping you get lucky. The 'Joot' way of traveling is finding the deal first and planning around it, which is like placing your chip after the marble drops.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates the difference between traditional, expensive travel planning and flexible, high-ROI travel hedging.
