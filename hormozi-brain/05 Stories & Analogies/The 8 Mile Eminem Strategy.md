# The 8 Mile Eminem Strategy

> it's kind of like Eminem when he when he said all the negative things in 8 Mile that anyone else could say about him so no one else could say anything bad

## The Story
In the movie 8 Mile, Eminem's character raps about all his own flaws and negative traits before his opponent can use them against him.

## What It Illustrates
Claiming your negatives upfront removes their power and builds unshakeable trust with the audience.
