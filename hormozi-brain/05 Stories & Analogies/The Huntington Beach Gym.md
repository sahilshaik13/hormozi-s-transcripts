# The Huntington Beach Gym

> me having that one-on-one time gave me the cash flow that I needed nothing from the business so I could just keep reinvesting

## The Story
Hormozi ran a group training gym but took on one client for 90-minute, 1-on-1 sessions, 5 days a week. This single client paid him $4,500 a month in cash, which he used to reinvest and grow the broader business.

## What It Illustrates
A single high-ticket, unscalable client can provide the cash flow needed to scale a business without taking on debt.
