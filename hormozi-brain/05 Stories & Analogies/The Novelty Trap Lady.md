# The Novelty Trap Lady

> If you really want to build something big, the trade-off is you don't get the novelty in your life.

## The Story
A woman running five different businesses making $1-2M a year complained she gets bored easily. Hormozi spelled out her trade-off: she was choosing the immediate gratification of novelty over the delayed gratification of building one massive business.

## What It Illustrates
You must explicitly choose between short-term stimulation (novelty) and long-term massive success (focus).
