# The Sprinkler vs. Laser Pointer

> Tik Tock is like a a bomb or like a sprinkler right LinkedIn is like you've got a hose with a a laser pointer on it

## The Story
TikTok is like a bomb or a sprinkler that sprays content everywhere indiscriminately. LinkedIn is like a hose with a laser pointer that allows you to shoot exactly who you want.

## What It Illustrates
Different platforms serve different targeting purposes; LinkedIn is superior for highly targeted B2B outbound.
