# The Clutch Player

> if you always give the ball to somebody when the game's on the line they're always going to want to give the ball to that person

## The Story
In sports, when the game is on the line, the team gives the ball to the player they trust most. By continually taking the ball and performing in these high-pressure moments, that player proves their reliability to both the team and themselves.

## What It Illustrates
Trust and leadership identity are exclusively forged in moments of high pressure.
