# The Marathon Finish Line

> Where does the winning happen on your own? Because no one cheers for you not drinking for a day

## The Story
In a marathon, crowds only gather at the starting line and the finish line. No one is standing in the middle miles cheering for you when you are alone, skipping the club, or eating a boring meal.

## What It Illustrates
Winning happens on your own, in the boring, uncelebrated moments of consistency.
