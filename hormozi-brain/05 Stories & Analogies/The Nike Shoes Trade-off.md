# The Nike Shoes Trade-off

> you can't pick the price for the shoes you pay it or you don't

## The Story
People want $300 Nikes. Some think they should get them for free. Some offer $30 and complain it's too expensive. The successful person keeps bidding higher and higher until they pay the full $300 required to get the shoes.

## What It Illustrates
Success has a fixed price. You cannot negotiate it; you must simply be willing to pay it.
