# The Popcorn Kernel

> that guy understood the cost of everything down to the colonel of popcorn

## The Story
A venture capitalist invested in Regal Cinemas despite having no interest in the theater industry, purely because the founder knew the cost of everything in his business down to the single kernel of popcorn.

## What It Illustrates
Betting on a highly competent, obsessed jockey is the safest and highest-leverage investment you can make.
