# NFL Red Zone Offense

> The top five red zone offenses in the league now last five years made the playoffs 90 of the time

## The Story
In the NFL, the top five red zone offenses over the last five years made the playoffs 90% of the time, regardless of how bad their defense or special teams were.

## What It Illustrates
Being able to close (score in the red zone) makes up for a tremendous amount of deficiencies in other areas of your business.
