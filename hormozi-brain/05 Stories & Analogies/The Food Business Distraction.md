# The Food Business Distraction

> 80% of my customer support complaints came from this 90% of my profit every month came from my supplements

## The Story
Hormozi started a meal prep company because he had a fitness audience. It made $600k/mo but only had a 7% profit margin, taking up 80% of customer support for only 10% of his total profits.

## What It Illustrates
Splitting focus away from your core, high-margin business costs millions in opportunity cost.
