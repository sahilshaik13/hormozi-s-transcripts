# The $3 Million Software Mistake

> just please just two million three million dollars of development later i've made the mistakes let me give you a three million dollar gift don't do it

## The Story
Hormozi attempted to build software by outsourcing the development to a third-party team without having an internal technical co-founder. It resulted in a massive loss of capital and time.

## What It Illustrates
Do not build a software-enabled service using outsourced dev agencies; you must have in-house technical leadership.
