# The Technology Ages

> throughout all of human history, humans plus superior technology beat humans with inferior technology.

## The Story
Throughout history, humans with superior technology have always beaten humans with inferior technology, from the Stone Age to the Bronze Age to the Iron Age.

## What It Illustrates
You are not competing against AI; you are competing against other humans who are using AI.
