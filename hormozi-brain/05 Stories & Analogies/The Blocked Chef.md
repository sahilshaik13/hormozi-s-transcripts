# The Blocked Chef

> he's like bro I don't have eggs doesn't matter how much I do

## The Story
You could have the best, most motivated chef in the world who knows how to make an omelet and knows you want it in five minutes. But if he doesn't have eggs, he can't make it.

## What It Illustrates
Sometimes failure to execute has nothing to do with skill or motivation, but simply an environmental blocker that the manager must remove.
