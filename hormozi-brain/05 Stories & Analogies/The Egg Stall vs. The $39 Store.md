# The Egg Stall vs. The $39 Store

> the cost of failure of starting a business today is by far the lowest it's ever been

## The Story
Harley's grandfather had to mortgage his house to start an egg stall, risking his family's shelter if he failed. Today, starting a business costs $39 a month.

## What It Illustrates
The historical, life-ruining risk of entrepreneurship is gone; the cost of failure is now negligible.
