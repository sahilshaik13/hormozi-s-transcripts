# Jacob the Garbage Man

> Yellow is the new gold.

## The Story
A 15-year-old kid joined Hormozi's sales team and took all the 'garbage' (yellow/unqualified) leads that the senior reps cherry-picked over. By treating every bad lead as free practice and working double the volume of the top guys, he eventually became the #1 sales rep.

## What It Illustrates
Humility, extreme volume, and treating every interaction as practice will eventually outperform natural talent or cherry-picking.
