# The Diluted Water

> we're able to shift the entire concentration of the team without disrupting the business

## The Story
If you have a pot of highly diluted water (a bad team) and you start adding green dye (good, high-performing employees), the water turns light green. The bad employees realize the standard has changed and voluntarily leave, eventually leaving you with a completely green pot.

## What It Illustrates
You can replace a toxic culture gradually by injecting high performers, which naturally forces out the low performers without disrupting the business.
