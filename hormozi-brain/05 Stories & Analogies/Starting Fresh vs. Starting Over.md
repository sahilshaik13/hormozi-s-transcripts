# Starting Fresh vs. Starting Over

> He said you couldn't possibly start over he said you could take everything from you no one can take the man you become the knowledge you have

## The Story
During his divorce, Tony lamented having to 'start over.' His friend Peter Guber corrected him, noting he was keeping his skills, network, and knowledge, so he was actually 'starting fresh.'

## What It Illustrates
Changing a single phrase can instantly shift a state of despair into one of opportunity.
