# The Hot Dog Stand

> the secret is that you want to starve and cry because when the football game lets out at three o'clock in the afternoon and everyone's starving and drunk you want to be the guy on the hot dog stand

## The Story
A marketing professor asks his class what the best competitive advantage for a hot dog stand would be. Students guess better ingredients or location. The professor reveals the true answer: a starving crowd.

## What It Illustrates
The most important factor in business success is finding a market with desperate, ravenous demand.
