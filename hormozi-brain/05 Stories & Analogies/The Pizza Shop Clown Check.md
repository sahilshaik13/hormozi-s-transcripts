# The Pizza Shop Clown Check

> she reaches in and she grabs her checkbook and she writes a $100 check to me and the check was literally this big it was a clown check

## The Story
A woman at the gym asked Hormozi for diet advice. They met at a pizza shop, he talked about fitness for an hour, and she unexpectedly wrote him a $100 check from a massive, clown-sized checkbook.

## What It Illustrates
It provided the lightbulb moment that a personal passion could be transformed into a profitable business.
