# The Moon Landing vs. AI

> it is the equivalent in my opinion of the moon landing except the only the only difference is that it will actually change all of our Lives

## The Story
The moon landing was a massive historical achievement, but for the average person, it was mostly just a picture to prove the US won the Cold War. AI is equivalent in historical magnitude, but it will actually alter the daily fabric of everyone's lives.

## What It Illustrates
The practical, everyday impact of AI is unprecedented and far more disruptive than previous historical milestones.
