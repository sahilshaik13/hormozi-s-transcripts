# The Dented Car at the Mastermind

> The game you're judging me on is a stupid game

## The Story
Hormozi drove a beat-up car with a cracked windshield and a dented door to his own high-end business event. A client judged him for not 'looking rich', but Hormozi knew he was playing a completely different, long-term financial game.

## What It Illustrates
Ego and the desire to look successful to others is a trap that keeps people poor.
