# Mad Men 'It's Toasted'

> the perception is still that it's different even if it is the same

## The Story
Don Draper pitches a cigarette company struggling to compete. He asks how they make the cigarettes, and they mention toasting the tobacco. Even though everyone does it, Draper uses 'It's Toasted' as their unique slogan.

## What It Illustrates
State the facts and tell the truth. Find a unique slice of your process to highlight, even if competitors do it but don't talk about it.
