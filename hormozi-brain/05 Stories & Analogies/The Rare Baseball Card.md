# The Rare Baseball Card

> you just take it he's like and you get to tell that story someday about the skin that you have and the things you've gone through

## The Story
You own an incredibly rare, valuable baseball card. While you are away, your two-year-old toddler gets into the box and completely destroys it. You can't hit the kid or get mad at your wife; you just have to take the loss.

## What It Illustrates
When catastrophic business or life events happen completely out of your control, you just have to absorb the blow, adjust your systems, and use it as a story of resilience.
