# The Penthouse Purchase

> He said 'I wouldn't have sold it for a penny less.' It felt so classy

## The Story
Hormozi bought an expensive penthouse and the seller immediately told him he wouldn't have sold it for a penny less. Even though it might have been a lie, it made Hormozi feel classy and satisfied with the fast acceptance.

## What It Illustrates
Managing the emotional state of the other party after a deal is struck is crucial for goodwill.
