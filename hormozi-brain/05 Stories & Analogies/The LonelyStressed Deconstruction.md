# The Lonely/Stressed Deconstruction

> in general you're stressed when you're alive noted what's your point right

## The Story
Someone claimed they were stressed because they were lonely. Hormozi asked them to define loneliness, then asked if they had ever been stressed when they weren't lonely, or stressed when things were going well. The person answered yes to all.

## What It Illustrates
People often falsely link two decoupled concepts. Breaking down their logic proves they are just stressed because they are alive, invalidating the false correlation.
