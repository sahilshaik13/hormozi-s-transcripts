# Mozart and Buffett

> You're like, I want to be like Mozart and you're age 30 and you want to start investing. It's like, well, he already had like 19 concertos by this point because he started at age seven.

## The Story
People at age 30 want to invest like Warren Buffett or compose like Mozart, forgetting that both started their crafts at age 7.

## What It Illustrates
You cannot compare your beginning to the middle or end of a generational outlier's journey; pure investing is the slowest path to wealth.
