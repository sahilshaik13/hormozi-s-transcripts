# The Red Card Slap

> same condition new behavior that's learning

## The Story
If someone shows you a red card and slaps you, it hurts. If they show it again and slap you, it hurts. If they show it a third time and you duck, you have learned.

## What It Illustrates
Learning is defined strictly as changing your behavior when presented with the same condition.
