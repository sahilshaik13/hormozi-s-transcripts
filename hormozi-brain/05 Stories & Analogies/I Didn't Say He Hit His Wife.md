# I Didn't Say He Hit His Wife

> it's the same sentence but simply emphasizing different parts of it communicate different things

## The Story
Repeating the exact same sentence while emphasizing a different word completely changes the meaning of the sentence.

## What It Illustrates
Tonality and emphasis in a sales script are just as important as the words themselves.
