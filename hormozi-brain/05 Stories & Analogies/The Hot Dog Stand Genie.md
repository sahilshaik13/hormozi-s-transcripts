# The Hot Dog Stand Genie

> if your hot dog stand is on the corner it doesn't matter how cheap they are how good they taste how fast they cook the moment that hungry crowd walks out and sees your hot dog stand you're going to sell it every time

## The Story
A hot dog vendor is offered one wish for a competitive advantage. He asks for cheaper, tastier, or faster-cooking hot dogs. The genie tells him the best advantage is a starving crowd.

## What It Illustrates
The market (demand) is the strongest variable in business success, outweighing the offer and persuasion.
