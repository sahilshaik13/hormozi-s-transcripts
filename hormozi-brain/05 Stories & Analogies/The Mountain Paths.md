# The Mountain Paths

> none of the paths will work if you're constantly trying to jump from path to path.

## The Story
Climbing a mountain has multiple paths. If you switch paths every time yours gets steep, you have to walk back down to the bottom to start the new one, ultimately taking much longer.

## What It Illustrates
Constantly changing strategies resets your progress; sticking to one path is faster even if it gets difficult.
