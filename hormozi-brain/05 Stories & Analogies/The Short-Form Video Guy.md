# The Short-Form Video Guy

> he started making short videos and he started accepting payments because he had removed the hidden costs

## The Story
A video editor pitched Hormozi by offering to do his short-form videos for free, explicitly stating he would pull existing content, edit it, and post it without Hormozi having to do any work. Hormozi said yes, and eventually started paying him.

## What It Illustrates
By removing all hidden costs (friction, time, effort) from a free offer, you guarantee a 'yes', prove your value, and easily transition to paid work.
