# The Turkey Talk

> You see, the beauty of the 5-Day Pass is everyone thinks they can get tan in five days. And they can. But, it’s never as tan as they want to get.

## The Story
A tanning salon owner sold a $5 5-day VIP pass. When customers came in wanting to get super dark in 5 days, he explained that just like cooking a turkey too fast burns it, tanning too fast burns skin. He then credited their $5 toward a monthly membership.

## What It Illustrates
Use a cheap decoy offer to get leads in the door, then educate them on why they actually need the premium, long-term solution.
