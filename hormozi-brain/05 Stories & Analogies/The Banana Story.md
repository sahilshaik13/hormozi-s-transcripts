# The Banana Story

> there's a very big difference between being dumb and being inexperienced

## The Story
A kid who had never seen a banana bit into it with the peel on. Another kid called him an idiot, but an adult corrected him, saying the kid just didn't know any better.

## What It Illustrates
There is a massive difference between being dumb and being inexperienced. Do not judge yourself for making obvious mistakes when you lack reps.
