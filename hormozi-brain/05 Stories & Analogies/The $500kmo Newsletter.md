# The $500k/mo Newsletter

> I went from calls once per month to calls once per week. Then I added all these other things…but everything I added increased churn.

## The Story
A friend running a highly profitable newsletter had the lowest churn when he only did one Q&A call and one physical newsletter a month. When he tried to add weekly calls and more features, churn actually increased.

## What It Illustrates
Adding more deliverables often creates overwhelm, which is the primary driver of customer churn.
