# The Three Stone Cutters

> all three men are doing the same work fundamentally having an entirely different experience of what that work means to them.

## The Story
A traveler asks three stone cutters what they are doing. The first says he is doing backbreaking, miserable work. The second says he is earning a living for his family. The third smiles and says he is building a cathedral to inspire generations.

## What It Illustrates
The exact same work can be experienced as a miserable job, a career, or a calling entirely based on your perspective and connection to a larger purpose.
