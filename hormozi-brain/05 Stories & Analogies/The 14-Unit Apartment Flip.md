# The 14-Unit Apartment Flip

> He ended up buying a 14 unit apartment building with someone else's money he didn't have any bought the apartment building and then sold it four months later for 500 000 more

## The Story
A friend bought a 14-unit apartment building using entirely other people's money. He sold it four months later for a $500,000 profit. This taught him that flipping more expensive commercial buildings yields massive returns without needing personal capital.

## What It Illustrates
Using leverage and OPM (Other People's Money) allows you to capture massive upside in real estate.
