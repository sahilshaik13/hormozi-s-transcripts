# The Movie Protagonist

> what would the character in the story do to move the plot along towards the happy ending?

## The Story
Imagine waking up as the main character in a movie that is guaranteed to have a happy ending. Even if terrible things happen—like losing a job, a breakup, or a death—the character must take action to move the plot toward that happy ending.

## What It Illustrates
Forces you to ask how a bad event can serve you and what the ideal next action is to reach your goals.
