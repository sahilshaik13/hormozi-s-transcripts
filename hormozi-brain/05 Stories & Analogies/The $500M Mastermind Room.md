# The $500M Mastermind Room

> I was like these guys aren't smarter than me and I didn't feel like they worked any harder than me and I was like why are they making so much more money than me

## The Story
Hormozi attended a meetup with 8 entrepreneurs generating $500M/year collectively. He was the smallest at $30M/year. He realized they weren't smarter or harder working; they had simply been in business longer and were targeting much larger markets.

## What It Illustrates
Scale is dictated by the size of your market and the time you've spent compounding your efforts, not just intellect or hustle.
