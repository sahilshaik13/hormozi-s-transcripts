# The Leaky Bucket

> I call this a leaky bucket business. And it’s a scary way to live.

## The Story
A gym owner with 250 members and 15% monthly churn loses 83% of their members in a year. They have to acquire 208 new clients just to stay exactly the same size.

## What It Illustrates
High churn makes business growth mathematically impossible and creates a constant state of anxiety.
