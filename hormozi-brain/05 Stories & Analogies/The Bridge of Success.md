# The Bridge of Success

> The actual outcome of getting a system to work all the way through that is binary, but the process of building the system is actually very linear.

## The Story
Success isn't jumping over a massive chasm filled with alligators in one giant leap. It's laying down bricks one by one, falling, fixing the path, and continuing until you walk all the way across.

## What It Illustrates
The outcome of success is binary, but the process of building it is linear, iterative, and full of small failures.
