# The Christmas Tree Org Chart

> that is usually the beginning of the core team and then a christmas tree is down so each of them right now have five people hanging off of them

## The Story
The core early team acts as the top of a Christmas tree. As the company scales, each of these initial hires ascends to management and has a team of five or more people 'hanging off of them' like branches.

## What It Illustrates
Early hires should ideally be capable of transitioning from individual contributors to managers who build departments beneath them.
