# Lloyd's Newspaper Business

> He applied his same skill set to a business he had zero experience in and was able to win. That’s the power of picking the right market.

## The Story
A brilliant entrepreneur struggled for years selling a great software product to newspapers because the industry was shrinking. When COVID hit, he pivoted to manufacturing masks and made millions instantly.

## What It Illustrates
Your market matters more than your product or sales skills; a dying market is a headwind, while a growing market is a tailwind.
