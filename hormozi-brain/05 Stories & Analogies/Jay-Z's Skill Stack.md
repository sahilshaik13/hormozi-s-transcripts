# Jay-Z's Skill Stack

> someone who can just rap, a little bit more value than someone who has rhythm. Somebody who can rap and write lyrics, more valuable.

## The Story
Jay-Z started with rhythm, then learned to rap, write lyrics, sell, market, build a label, and recruit other artists like Beyonce. Each new skill made the previous ones exponentially more valuable.

## What It Illustrates
Skills stack on top of each other, turning a basic talent into a massive enterprise.
