# The Unemployed Resume Coach

> no one wants to buy my stuff because they all say they don't they don't have jobs

## The Story
A coach targeted unemployed people to help with their resumes. The market had massive pain, was growing, and was easy to find, but nobody bought because they didn't have jobs or money.

## What It Illustrates
A market must have purchasing power; pain and growth are useless if the prospects cannot pay.
