# The Harvard Resume Stamp

> how can I make it so that my business is like Harvard Man

## The Story
Just like seeing Google or Facebook on a resume makes you assume a person is smart, you should build your company's reputation so that working there is a prestigious stamp of approval.

## What It Illustrates
Building a strong internal training and fulfillment brand attracts top talent who will even take a pay cut just to acquire your skills.
