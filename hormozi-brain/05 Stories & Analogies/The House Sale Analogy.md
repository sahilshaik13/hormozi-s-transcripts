# The House Sale Analogy

> when you're about to sell the house and you fixed all the things that are wrong with it you're like you know this house ain't so bad right

## The Story
When you live in a house, you ignore squeaky doors and cracks. Right before you sell it, you fix everything to increase its value. Once it's fixed, you realize the house is actually amazing to live in.

## What It Illustrates
Fixing keyman risk to make a business sellable also makes it significantly more enjoyable and passive to run.
