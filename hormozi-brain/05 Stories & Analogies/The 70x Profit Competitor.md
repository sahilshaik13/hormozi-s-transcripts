# The 70x Profit Competitor

> But the main difference was his customers were worth $5,000 and mine were worth $42,000 (in the first year).

## The Story
A competitor in the gym space acquired the same volume of customers as Gym Launch but targeted everyone, resulting in a $5,000 LTV. Hormozi targeted only the top 20% avatar, resulting in a $42,000 LTV and 70x more profit.

## What It Illustrates
Updating your messaging to attract only the customers who stay the longest and pay the most is a massive leverage point.
