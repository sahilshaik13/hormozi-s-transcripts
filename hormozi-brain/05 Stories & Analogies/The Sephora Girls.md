# The Sephora Girls

> When you have a new identity, you have new priorities.

## The Story
Two 13-year-old girls were buying makeup. The clerk told them that as they become women, they need to start budgeting for this every month. The girls accepted this new financial priority because they wanted the new identity.

## What It Illustrates
Stepping into a new identity requires adopting the spending habits and priorities of that identity.
