# The Planet Fitness 'Friendly Goodbye'

> they call the friendly goodbye... when he like gets the next gig or gets to the next job or feels fat again or whatever it is he signs back up because it was no big deal

## The Story
Planet Fitness requires 10,000 members per gym to be profitable, but only a small percentage use it. They have a 5% monthly churn. To survive, they make canceling incredibly easy and friendly, so when people decide to work out again, they rejoin without hesitation.

## What It Illustrates
Making it easy to leave actually increases the likelihood that customers will return.
