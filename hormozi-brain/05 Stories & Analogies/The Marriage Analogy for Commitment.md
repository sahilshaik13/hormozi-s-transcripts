# The Marriage Analogy for Commitment

> When you get married you eliminate all other women or all other men that you're going to be dating that's how you commit

## The Story
When you get married, you don't just decide to like one person; you actively eliminate all other men or women you could potentially date. That act of elimination is what defines the commitment.

## What It Illustrates
True commitment in business requires cutting off alternative opportunities and not straddling the line between two models.
