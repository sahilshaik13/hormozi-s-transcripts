# The Drug Dealer

> If anything, like a drug dealer, you’d wanna give the strongest ‘hit’ first. It keeps them coming back for more.

## The Story
Drug dealers give away a free sample because they know the product is so good the user will come back and pay for more.

## What It Illustrates
Do not dilute the value of your free lead magnets; give your strongest 'hit' first so they come back for your paid stuff.
