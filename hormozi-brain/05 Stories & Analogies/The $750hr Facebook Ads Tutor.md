# The $750/hr Facebook Ads Tutor

> I showed up like it was Harvard on steroids for these calls

## The Story
Instead of paying an agency $5,000 a month he couldn't afford, Hormozi convinced an expert to teach him Facebook ads for $750 an hour. He treated the 8 sessions like Harvard, recording and documenting everything.

## What It Illustrates
It is highly efficient to buy procedural knowledge directly by paying an expert their hourly rate to teach you their exact process.
