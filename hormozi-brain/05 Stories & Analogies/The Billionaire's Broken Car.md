# The Billionaire's Broken Car

> it wasn't a function of what he did it was a function of who he helped

## The Story
A man pushes a broken-down car for a stranger on the side of the road. The stranger turns out to be a billionaire and pays off the man's mortgage in return.

## What It Illustrates
The value derived from your work is often about who you help, not necessarily what you provide.
