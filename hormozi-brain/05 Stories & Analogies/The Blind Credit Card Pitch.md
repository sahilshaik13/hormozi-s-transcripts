# The Blind Credit Card Pitch

> I have no idea what Alex is selling but here's my credit card just bill me

## The Story
When launching his software 'Allen', gym owners who missed the webinar called in and offered their credit cards without even knowing what the product was, just to get in line.

## What It Illustrates
When you consistently keep your promises over time, the brand association becomes so strong that customers will buy based purely on past trust.
