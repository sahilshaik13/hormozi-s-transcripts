# The Single Battery

> we all have one battery and the problem with your spouse and your kids and your chores and your employees is they all drain from the same source

## The Story
Every person operates from a single energy source. Your spouse, your kids, your chores, and your employees all pull power from this exact same battery.

## What It Illustrates
You must ruthlessly eliminate non-essential drains because you cannot separate personal energy from business energy.
