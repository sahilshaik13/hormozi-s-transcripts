## The Story
When Hormozi started Gym Launch, he didn't have a lot of money and the business was heavily offer-driven, making it easy for competitors to copy. He chose a licensing model to generate immediate cash flow and capture market share faster than anyone else.

## What It Illustrates
Licensing is the ultimate vehicle for speed and cash flow when your IP is hard to defend and you lack upfront capital.
