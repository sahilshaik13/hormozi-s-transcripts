# The Free Pool Frame

> you're really just taking it from one pocket and putting to another You're the one who gets to keep the pool

## The Story
A pool contractor struggles to sell a $100,000 pool. Hormozi advises framing it against neighborhood home values: if the pool adds $100,000 to the home's resale value, the pool is essentially free to enjoy.

## What It Illustrates
Reframing a sunk cost as a wealth-transfer or investment completely removes the buyer's friction.
