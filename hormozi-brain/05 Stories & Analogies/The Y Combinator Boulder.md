# The Y Combinator Boulder

> in the beginning a startup feels like pushing a boulder up a hill and then as soon as you get product Market fit... it feels like you're chasing the boulder down the other side

## The Story
In the beginning, a startup feels like pushing a heavy boulder up a steep hill. Once you hit product-market fit and word-of-mouth takes over, it feels like you are sprinting down the other side of the hill desperately chasing the boulder.

## What It Illustrates
Decentralized word-of-mouth creates a compounding business that grows faster than you can manage it.
