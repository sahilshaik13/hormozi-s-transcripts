# The Smoothie King Sandwich

> I was like man I gave up 2 hours of my 6-hour shift every shift to go by lunch

## The Story
While making $6.75 an hour at Smoothie King, Hormozi realized a $13 lunch cost him two hours of his six-hour shift. He recognized he was trading a third of his daily life just for a sandwich.

## What It Illustrates
Translate the cost of consumer goods into the hours of life required to buy them to radically change your spending behavior.
