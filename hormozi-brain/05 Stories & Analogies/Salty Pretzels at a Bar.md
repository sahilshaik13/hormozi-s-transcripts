# Salty Pretzels at a Bar

> The salty pretzels solve the narrow problem of hunger. They also reveal a thirst problem solved by a drink, which they can get, in exchange for money.

## The Story
Bars give away salty pretzels for free. The pretzels solve the narrow problem of hunger, but reveal a new problem of thirst, which the bar solves by selling drinks.

## What It Illustrates
A lead magnet should solve a narrow problem for free while revealing a new problem that your core offer solves for money.
