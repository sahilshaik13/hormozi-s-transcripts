# The Record-Crushing Sales Guy

> he didn't have higher close rates but for some reason he was able to get 30 to 50% more sales than everybody else

## The Story
A new sales rep joined a portfolio company and broke all existing sales records. When management audited his calls, they found his close rate wasn't higher than anyone else's. His secret was simply asking 'who else do you know who might also want this?' after every single close, generating 30-50% more sales from the resulting introductions.

## What It Illustrates
A simple, consistent referral ask at the point of sale massively compounds results without requiring better sales skills.
