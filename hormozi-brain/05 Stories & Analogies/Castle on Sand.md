# Castle on Sand

> It's building a castle on a foundation of sand. As soon as the tide comes out and pulls the sand away, so too goes the castle.

## The Story
Building a massive business by selling to highly volatile small businesses is like building a castle on a foundation of sand. When the economic tide goes out, the sand washes away, and the castle collapses.

## What It Illustrates
Your business's stability is entirely dependent on the stability of your customers.
