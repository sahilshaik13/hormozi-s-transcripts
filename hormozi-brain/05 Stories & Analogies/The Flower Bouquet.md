# The Flower Bouquet

> Think about it like curating a garden: you want some flowers to grow, and you want to pull out the weeds.

## The Story
Brand elements (products, values, people) are like individual flowers scattered on a table. By deliberately assembling them over and over, they form a bouquet. If one flower is rotten, you don't throw away the concept of the bouquet; you overwhelm it with beautiful flowers.

## What It Illustrates
A brand doesn't exist on its own; it is merely the deliberate assembly of associations. Mistakes can be fixed by overwhelming the bad with the good.
