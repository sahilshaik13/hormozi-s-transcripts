# The Transplanted Sales Guy

> It would be like saying, 'Hey, can I take one of your sales guys and put them in my business?' The first shot, you think they're going to be good? Of course not.

## The Story
Taking an AI setter and dropping it into a new business is like taking a top human sales guy and dropping him into a new business without training. On the first shot, they will both suck.

## What It Illustrates
AI cannot fix a broken or non-existent sales process; it only scales what already works.
