# The Slumdog Millionaire

> a lot of times we just need to expand the time Rison and realize that we might be playing our own Slum Dog Millionaire movie

## The Story
A kid in the slums goes through a lifetime of terrible, unlucky experiences. Later, he gets on a game show and realizes every bad experience perfectly gave him the answer to a trivia question to win the million dollars.

## What It Illustrates
Bad experiences and hardships are just giving you the unique answers and skills you will need for a future test.
