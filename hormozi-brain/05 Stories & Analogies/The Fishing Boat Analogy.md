# The Fishing Boat Analogy

> if you have one source of getting customers you've also only got one that needs to break in order to fail

## The Story
A fisherman with only one line starves if that line breaks. A boat with multiple crew members and multiple lines (channels) survives even if one line breaks or one crew member falls overboard.

## What It Illustrates
Single channel and keyman risks compound; multiple channels and redundancies create a stable, sellable asset.
