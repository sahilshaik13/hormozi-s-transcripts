# Toyota vs. Ford

> the consumer is the one that creates your brand

## The Story
Elon Musk pointed out that Ford's marketing constantly talked about being reliable and high quality, whereas Toyota's marketing never explicitly said that. Yet, consumers universally recognize Toyota as the reliable, high-quality brand.

## What It Illustrates
The consumer creates your brand based on what is actually true, not just what your marketing claims.
