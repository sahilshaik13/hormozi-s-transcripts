# Skimming the Top

> just skim a tiny amount off the top and then grow the goodwill of the base

## The Story
Instead of draining the entire pool of demand by selling to every single person who wants to buy, you treat your audience like a growing reservoir. You only take a small fraction off the surface, allowing the total volume to grow larger over time.

## What It Illustrates
Restricting supply preserves goodwill and makes future sales easier.
