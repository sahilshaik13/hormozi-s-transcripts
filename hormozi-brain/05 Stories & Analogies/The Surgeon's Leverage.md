# The Surgeon's Leverage

> Have you ever seen a surgeon say, 'I'll do the surgery and then you can pay me later.'

## The Story
If you want surgery, the surgeon tells you to pay first, and then they perform the operation. You never see a surgeon do the work and ask for payment later.

## What It Illustrates
The more leverage and specialized skill you have, the further up the payment pyramid you can go, commanding terms where you get paid before doing any work.
