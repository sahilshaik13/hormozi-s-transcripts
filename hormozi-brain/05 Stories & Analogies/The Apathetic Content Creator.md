# The Apathetic Content Creator

> He was trying to be cool about the fact that he didn't try hard, but all it looked like was that he was losing.

## The Story
A guy bragged to Hormozi about outsourcing all his content creation down to two hours a week to show how little he cared. Hormozi simply replied, 'Yeah, it shows.'

## What It Illustrates
Trying to look cool by not putting in effort just makes you look like you are losing.
