# College Admissions

> we have to be strict it's kind of like college admissions

## The Story
When explaining strict qualifications for a guarantee or bonus on a sales call, compare it to the strictness of college admissions.

## What It Illustrates
It frames the offer as exclusive and puts the prospect in the position of hoping to be accepted.
