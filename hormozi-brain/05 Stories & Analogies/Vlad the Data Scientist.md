# Vlad the Data Scientist

> Four data points correlate to positive appointments.

## The Story
During COVID, Hormozi hired a brilliant Russian data scientist to analyze a million rows of appointment data. Vlad discovered four specific variables that perfectly correlated with show rates: availability, scheduling window, follow-up volume, and response rate.

## What It Illustrates
Lead nurture and show rates are driven by objective, measurable data points, not luck or magic.
