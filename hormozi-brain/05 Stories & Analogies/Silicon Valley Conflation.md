# Silicon Valley Conflation

> the reason lifetime value and lifetime gross profit get conflated or confused is because many of the most formal businesses that are out there come from Silicon Valley

## The Story
Tech and software companies have near 100% gross margins, meaning their lifetime revenue and lifetime gross profit are essentially the same number. Everyday businesses copy their metrics and fail.

## What It Illustrates
Non-software businesses must account for COGS, manufacturing, and labor, meaning they must run their math on gross profit, not revenue.
