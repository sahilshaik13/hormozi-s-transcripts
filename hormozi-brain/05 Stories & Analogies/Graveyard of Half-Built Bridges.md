# Graveyard of Half-Built Bridges

> they never get one of these dollars across to the other side into their bank account

## The Story
Companies spend resources building multiple bridges across a river but never finish any of them. Because no bridge reaches the other side, no money actually crosses into their bank account.

## What It Illustrates
Starting multiple projects without finishing them drains resources with zero financial return.
