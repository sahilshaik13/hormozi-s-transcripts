# The Snowblower ROI

> your ability to earn more using money is going to always be higher than the S&P 500

## The Story
Putting $10,000 into the S&P 500 yields $1,000 in a year. But using that money to buy a snowblower allows you to clear 20 driveways a day instead of 3, 7x-ing your active income.

## What It Illustrates
In the beginning, investing in your own skills and earning capacity yields vastly higher returns than passive market investing.
