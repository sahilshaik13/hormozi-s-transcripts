# Pushing the Boulder

> getting product market fit is like pushing a boulder up the hill. And once you have it, it's like chasing uh getting chased by the boulder on the other way down

## The Story
Building a software company before product-market fit is like agonizingly pushing a heavy boulder up a steep hill. But the moment you achieve product-market fit, you are running down the other side of the hill with the boulder chasing you.

## What It Illustrates
Software has a brutal, slow start, but achieves violent, infinite scale once the product actually removes friction for the user.
