# The Three-Sided Ladder

> you need all three to move to the next level of the ladder

## The Story
Entrepreneurship is like climbing a ladder that requires a left pole, a right pole, and middle rungs. These represent beliefs, skills, and character traits. You can have skills built ten stories high, but if your character is lagging, you cannot climb.

## What It Illustrates
Growth is bottlenecked by your weakest component among beliefs, skills, and character.
