# The Casino Bankroll Constraint

> we do not have unlimited swings we do not have unlimited resources and so we have to be incredibly measured with which swings we choose to take

## The Story
Jeff Bezos compares business to baseball, where unlike baseball's 4-run max, business has uncapped runs, justifying taking many 10% probability bets. Hormozi counters with a casino analogy: if you only have $20 and minimum bets are $10, you only get two swings, meaning an 80% chance of total ruin.

## What It Illustrates
Because entrepreneurs have limited resources (swings), they cannot blindly take high-upside/low-probability bets; they must prioritize sure-thing problem solving first.
