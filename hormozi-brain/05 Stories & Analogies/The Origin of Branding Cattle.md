# The Origin of Branding Cattle

> Brand comes when you brand cattle right that was the original use of a brand

## The Story
Cowboys branded cattle to change the behavior of people who saw the cow. Without a brand, someone might steal or eat it. With a brand, they return it to the owner.

## What It Illustrates
The entire point of a brand is to elicit a desired behavior from a specific audience.
