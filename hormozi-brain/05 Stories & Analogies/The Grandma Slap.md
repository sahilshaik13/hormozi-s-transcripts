# The Grandma Slap

> If I have a a grandma in public come and just do a running slap and just slaps me across the face, that video will probably get views. But does it get the grandma views? No.

## The Story
If a grandma runs up and slaps you in public, the video will get millions of views from people who like humor, but it won't get you any customers.

## What It Illustrates
Viral views are useless if the content doesn't attract your specific target demographic.
