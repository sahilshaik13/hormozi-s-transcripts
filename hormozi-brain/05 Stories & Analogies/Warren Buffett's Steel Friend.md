# Warren Buffett's Steel Friend

> it wasn't about how hard you row but the boat you're in

## The Story
Two equally smart friends graduate college. One goes into investing, the other into the shrinking US steel industry. Despite equal effort, one becomes a billionaire and the other struggles.

## What It Illustrates
Market trajectory matters more than individual management or effort.
