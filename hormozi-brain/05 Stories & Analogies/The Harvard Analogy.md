# The Harvard Analogy

> Number one is that Harvard has standards. So they have many people apply and only a few get in.

## The Story
Harvard charges massive fees but makes no promises, rejects most applicants, and fails people. They sell the network and brand, not just the curriculum.

## What It Illustrates
True brand value and demand come from exclusivity and curation, not just information.
