# The Fridge vs. The Menu

> We should ask the question, 'What the do I want to eat?' And then go get the ingredients and go make it happen.

## The Story
Most people treat business strategy like making dinner by looking in the fridge to see what ingredients they happen to have. Instead, they should decide exactly what they want to eat, then go out and get those specific ingredients.

## What It Illustrates
Strategy should be dictated by the desired end goal, not limited by current resources.
