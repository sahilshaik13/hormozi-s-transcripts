# Smoothing the Wheel

> everyone gets so distracted with like the I'm going to reinvent the wheel rather than just making the wheel a little smoother so it runs faster

## The Story
Instead of trying to invent a completely new wheel to go faster, you just make the existing wheel a little smoother.

## What It Illustrates
Optimization of existing processes often yields better growth than constantly seeking new, shiny strategies.
