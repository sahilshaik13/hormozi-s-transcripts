# The $16,000 Suit

> The only thing worse than making a $1,000 offer to a person with a $100 budget… is making a $100 offer to someone with a $1,000 budget.

## The Story
Hormozi went to buy a suit with a $500 budget. The tailor first put him in a $16,000 suit. When Hormozi gasped at the price, the tailor immediately offered a $2,200 alternative, which Hormozi bought with relief.

## What It Illustrates
Presenting a massively expensive premium offer first makes your core offer look like an incredible deal by comparison.
