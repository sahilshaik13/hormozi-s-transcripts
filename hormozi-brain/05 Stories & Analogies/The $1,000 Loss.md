# The $1,000 Loss

> Does it change the food you eat? Does it change the car you drive? Does it change where you live? What does it actually change? It usually changes absolutely nothing.

## The Story
If you lose $1,000 of your $10,000 life savings, it feels traumatic. However, it actually changes nothing about your daily routine, the food you eat, or where you live.

## What It Illustrates
Most suffering from loss is a voluntary choice, not a material change in reality.
