# Eminem in 8 Mile

> in 8 Mile Eminem tries to claim all of the flaws that he has so that no one else can say anything

## The Story
Eminem claims all of his own flaws in rap battles so his opponents have nothing left to say against him. He also responds to hate by simply asking 'who?' to show he is too big to care.

## What It Illustrates
Claim your negatives publicly to disarm haters and overwhelm the marketplace.
