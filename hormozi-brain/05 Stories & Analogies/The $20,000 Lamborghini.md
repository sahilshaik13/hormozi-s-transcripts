# The $20,000 Lamborghini

> If I said it was $20,000 for a brand new Lamborghini, they would say that's a great deal.

## The Story
If you offer a poor person something for $20,000, they will immediately say it is expensive. If you offer a rich person a brand new Lamborghini for $20,000, they will say it is the deal of the century.

## What It Illustrates
Wealthy buyers do not evaluate absolute cost; they evaluate the ratio of cost to value.
