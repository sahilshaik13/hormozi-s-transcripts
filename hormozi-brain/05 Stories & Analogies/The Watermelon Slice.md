# The Watermelon Slice

> your density of sales per thousand eyeballs goes down as you go to more unaware people

## The Story
The tip of a watermelon slice has many seeds packed tightly together, representing warm, highly aware audiences. As you go deeper into the slice, the seeds spread out, representing colder, unaware audiences.

## What It Illustrates
As you scale advertising to colder audiences, your conversion density drops, requiring higher LTV to sustain the broader reach.
