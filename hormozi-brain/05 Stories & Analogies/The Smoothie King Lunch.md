# The Smoothie King Lunch

> so you're working 6 hours for 20 bucks

## The Story
At 15, Hormozi worked at Smoothie King making $7.25 an hour. He noticed coworkers spending $15 on lunch next door, effectively blowing away nearly half of their daily post-tax earnings on one meal.

## What It Illustrates
Always calculate the cost of items in terms of the hours of your life it took to earn that money.
