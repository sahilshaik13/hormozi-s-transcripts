# Warren Buffett's Time

> The proof point is you live in time and you earn money in time, which means everyone has an hourly rate.

## The Story
People think Warren Buffett just writes checks and makes passive income. In reality, he spends thousands of hours analyzing macro markets and doing due diligence on a thousand deals just to pick one.

## What It Illustrates
Even the greatest investors trade their time for money; they just have a massively high hourly rate.
