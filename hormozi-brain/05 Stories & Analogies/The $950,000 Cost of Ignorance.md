# The $950,000 Cost of Ignorance

> Did you know that it's costing you $950,000 per year to not know how to make a million dollar a year?

## The Story
A speaker writes $1,000,000 on a whiteboard and asks an audience member making $50,000 a year if they want to make a million. He then writes a minus sign, explaining that not knowing how to make a million is costing them $950,000 every single year.

## What It Illustrates
Ignorance is your most expensive recurring cost. Investing heavily in education to learn high-income skills is the most logical financial decision you can make.
