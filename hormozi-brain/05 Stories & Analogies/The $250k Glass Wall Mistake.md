# The $250k Glass Wall Mistake

> My glass wall at my second location was almost the same price as my entire first location.

## The Story
Hormozi spent $250,000 building out his second gym with fancy equipment and granite countertops, driven by ego. It made the exact same amount of money as his first bare-bones gym where people lifted duct-taped sandbags from Home Depot.

## What It Illustrates
Customers buy the result, the community, and the accountability, not the fancy aesthetic wrapping. Understand the actual 'job to be done'.
