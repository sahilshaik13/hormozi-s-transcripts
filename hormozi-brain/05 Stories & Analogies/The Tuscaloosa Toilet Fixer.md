# The Tuscaloosa Toilet Fixer

> if you're like hey i'm the fastest toilet fixer in this side of tuscaloosa you might only be competing in three guys

## The Story
Instead of trying to be a billionaire business guru, start by being the fastest toilet fixer in Tuscaloosa. You prove you can fix 500 toilets, then you talk about plumbing, then local business, and eventually enterprise.

## What It Illustrates
Authority is built sequentially through undeniable evidence in a narrow niche before expanding to broader topics.
