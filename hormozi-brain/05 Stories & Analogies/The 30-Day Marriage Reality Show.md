# The 30-Day Marriage Reality Show

> it's kind of like one of those dating shows where they say will you get married in 30 days and they like try and cram a whole relationship into that period

## The Story
Challenges are like reality dating shows where couples are forced to decide on marriage in 30 days. They cram a normal six-month relationship timeline into a highly condensed, intense period.

## What It Illustrates
Short, intense marketing events can artificially manufacture the trust and brand affinity that normally takes months to build.
