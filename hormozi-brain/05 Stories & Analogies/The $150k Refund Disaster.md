# The $150k Refund Disaster

> Doing the thing that scared me most - giving away my secrets - led to the biggest breakthrough in my life.

## The Story
Hormozi lost $150,000 in a single day when gym owners sabotaged his launches and demanded refunds. Broke and desperate, he pivoted to selling his lead-generation secrets over the phone for $6,000 a pop, saving his business and launching a multi-million dollar empire.

## What It Illustrates
Doing the thing that scares you most (giving away your secrets) can lead to your biggest breakthrough.
