# Game of Thrones

> if you look forward to you don't need to be motivated because you do it on your own because you like it

## The Story
You never have to hype yourself up or find motivation to sit down and watch your favorite TV show like Game of Thrones. You just do it because you look forward to it.

## What It Illustrates
A good program shouldn't require endless willpower; it should be designed so the client actually looks forward to doing it.
