# Gary Halbert's Hypothetical Max

> whenever I want to write a sales letter I imagine that I can only write one letter for my life or I'm going to die

## The Story
Copywriter Gary Halbert imagined a scenario where he could only send one sales letter to save his life. He would handwrite the envelope, lick the stamp, and include a lumpy object to guarantee it was opened.

## What It Illustrates
When you are small and have limited leads, you must use maximum, unscalable effort to ensure the highest possible conversion rate.
