# The Electric Shock Machine

> people can actually endure a significantly larger amount of pain for other people.

## The Story
If hooked up to a shock machine for themselves, people tap out quickly. But if told that every shock they take spares a loved one in the next room from being shocked, their pain tolerance increases tenfold.

## What It Illustrates
Connecting your struggles to the service and protection of others unlocks a massive reserve of endurance and motivation.
