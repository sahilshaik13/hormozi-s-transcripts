# The Info-Business Rollup

> He combined them all to be a four million dollar Top Line million and a half bottom line business for no money out of pocket

## The Story
A friend acquired four separate $1M information businesses for zero money down by agreeing to pay the owners from future revenue. He combined them into a single $4M top-line business generating $1.5M in profit.

## What It Illustrates
You can acquire and multiply the value of businesses simply by structuring deals around future cash flow rather than upfront cash.
