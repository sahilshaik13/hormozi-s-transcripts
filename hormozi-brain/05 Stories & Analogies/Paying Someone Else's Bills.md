# Paying Someone Else's Bills

> you stopped doing it as soon as you paid someone else's bills because you were willing to pay other people's bills tonight your own

## The Story
When an unexpected bill comes up, people suddenly find a way to hustle and make the money. But once the bill is paid, they stop doing the very thing that worked.

## What It Illustrates
People have the ability and knowledge to make money, but often only apply it when forced by external pressure, rather than sustaining it to build their own wealth.
