# The Mechanic's Diagnosis

> Hey, you’re asking me what's wrong with your car, and it would be unethical for me to answer without looking under the hood first.

## The Story
A mechanic cannot ethically tell you what is wrong with your car without looking under the hood first. If they do, they are just trying to sell you something.

## What It Illustrates
Explains to prospects why you cannot answer specific technical questions before doing a proper assessment.
