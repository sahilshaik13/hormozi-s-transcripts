# The Yeti Mug

> you can have something hot on the inside and does it's not hard to hold it and it stays hot for a while the difference is that there's separation

## The Story
A Yeti mug keeps hot things hot and cold things cold because there is a layer of air separating the inside from the outside environment.

## What It Illustrates
You must create emotional space between external events and your internal state so that outside chaos doesn't dictate your internal temperature.
