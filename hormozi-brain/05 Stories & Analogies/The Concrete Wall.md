# The Concrete Wall

> you have to be willing to confront with the sledgehammer in hand not knowing how thick the wall is going to be and start hammering away at it

## The Story
Business growth is like facing a massive, thick concrete wall with a huge pile of money on the other side. You don't know how thick the wall is, but you have to pick up a sledgehammer and keep swinging until you break through.

## What It Illustrates
Unlocking enterprise value requires relentless, long-term commitment to solving a single, difficult problem without getting distracted.
