# The Door-to-Door Cleaning Salesman

> he just did it in front of me on a stain I actually had

## The Story
A salesman pitched a cleaning product by immediately getting on his hands and knees to scrub an impossible gum stain out of Hormozi's gym turf. The visual proof resulted in an instant $100 sale.

## What It Illustrates
Showing a prospect exactly what they will experience is infinitely more powerful than telling them.
