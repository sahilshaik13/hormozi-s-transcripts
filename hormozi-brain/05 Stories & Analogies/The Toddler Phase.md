# The Toddler Phase

> Think about how embarrassing you are probably to your parents or were to your parents when you were a child learning how to be a human.

## The Story
When you start anything new, you will suck and be embarrassing, just like a toddler learning how to be a human in front of their parents.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates that the 'cringe' phase is a natural, unavoidable part of growth, not a reason to avoid trying.
