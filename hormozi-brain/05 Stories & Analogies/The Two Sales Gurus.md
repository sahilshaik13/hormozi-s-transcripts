# The Two Sales Gurus

> But assuming the information was literally identical, the person who has the most credibility wins and not just by a little bit, by a lot of it.

## The Story
Imagine two people giving the exact same six tips on how to scale a sales team. One is an AI or a novice who has never sold anything. The other is a veteran who has built 10 sales teams. The veteran will have a significantly larger following because their real-world experience lowers the risk for the listener.

## What It Illustrates
Information alone is no longer a differentiator; the credibility and proof of the messenger are what drive audience trust and action.
