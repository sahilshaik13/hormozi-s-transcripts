# The Boiling Ocean

> the change that's about to happen is a phase shift where you're swimming, but then all of a sudden the water boils and now you're in gas

## The Story
You spend your whole life learning to swim, moving from pools to lakes to oceans. But suddenly, the water boils and turns to gas. It doesn't matter how good of a swimmer you are because the fundamental physics of the environment have changed.

## What It Illustrates
AI is a phase shift that changes the fundamental rules of business and labor, rendering old skills obsolete.
