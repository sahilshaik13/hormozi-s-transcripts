# The 5 Horsemen of Retention

> when I interviewed all of them all of all 20 of them were only doing five things in common and those became the five Horsemen retention

## The Story
When trying to solve gym churn, Hormozi interviewed 20 gyms that had less than 3% churn. While each gym did 20+ different things, he found only 5 things they all had in common, which became his definitive retention framework.

## What It Illustrates
Frameworks are built by drinking in massive amounts of data and isolating the few overlapping variables of success.
