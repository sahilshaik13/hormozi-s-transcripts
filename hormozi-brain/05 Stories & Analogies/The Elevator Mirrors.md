# The Elevator Mirrors

> you can just put mirrors in the elevators and then people stare at themselves and don't notice the time pass

## The Story
People used to complain about slow elevators. Instead of replacing the motor to make the elevator logically faster, building managers installed mirrors. People stared at themselves, didn't notice the time pass, and the complaints stopped.

## What It Illustrates
Sometimes the best way to solve a customer's problem is psychologically rather than logically.
