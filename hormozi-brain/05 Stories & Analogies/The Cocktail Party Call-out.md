# The Cocktail Party Call-out

> a call out is whatever you do to get the attention of your audience

## The Story
In a loud ballroom, if someone drops a tray of dishes, everyone looks. If someone yells 'Mom', all the moms look. If someone says your specific name, only you look.

## What It Illustrates
Advertising call-outs range from broad to hyper-specific; you must make them specific enough to get the right people, but broad enough to get as many as possible.
