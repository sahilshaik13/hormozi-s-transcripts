# The Fraternity Party Planner

> The bar for having a reason to party is quite low.

## The Story
A fraternity party planner doesn't need a profound reason to throw a party. They just say 'Timmy got his wisdom teeth out, kegger!' or 'It's Tuesday, let's drink!'

## What It Illustrates
You don't need a logically profound reason to run a promotion; you just need to provide a reason in general to trigger compliance.
