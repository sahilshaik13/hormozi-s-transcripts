# COVID Gym Closures

> they were people who wanted to have a reason that was socially acceptable for losing

## The Story
When COVID hit, many gyms closed within the first three weeks. Others fought for nine months until they literally ran out of cash.

## What It Illustrates
The early closures weren't due to lack of money; the owners had lost the will to fight and used the pandemic as a socially acceptable excuse to quit.
