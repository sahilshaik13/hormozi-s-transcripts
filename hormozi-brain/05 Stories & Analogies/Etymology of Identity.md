# Etymology of Identity

> repeated beingness is what your identity is so your actions what you do literally becomes who you are

## The Story
The root of the word 'identity' comes from 'entity' (beingness) and 'identical' (repeated). Therefore, identity literally translates to 'repeated beingness'.

## What It Illustrates
Your actions literally become who you are; you can actively change your identity simply by changing what you repeatedly do.
