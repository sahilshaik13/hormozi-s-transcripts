# 100 Golden BBs

> it's kind of like 100 golden BBS there's no one one silver bullet that solves all this stuff

## The Story
There is no single silver bullet to fix email deliverability. Instead, it requires 100 small, consistent actions—like plain text, good links, and reply prompts.

## What It Illustrates
Deliverability is built through a holistic reputation of high-quality engagement over time.
