# The Dream House Paradox

> They are literally structurally impossible for them to all be in the same thing.

## The Story
You cannot have a house that is simultaneously in a ski town, on the beach, secluded, and in walking distance to downtown shopping.

## What It Illustrates
It is structurally impossible to 'have it all'; you must make conscious trade-offs in life and business.
