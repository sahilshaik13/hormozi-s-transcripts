# The Brand Pyramid

> If we want to grow this little slice here, what do we do? Grow the bottom. We grow the base.

## The Story
A brand is like a pyramid where the top tiny slice represents paying customers, and the massive bottom represents people who just consume and like the content.

## What It Illustrates
To get more buyers at the top, you don't just force people up an elevator; you widen the massive base of non-buyers.
