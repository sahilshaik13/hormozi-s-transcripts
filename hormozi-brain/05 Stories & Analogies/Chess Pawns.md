# Chess Pawns

> There's not a single chess master that I know of who has ever won a game without losing any at the highest levels.

## The Story
In a game of chess, it is impossible to win at the highest levels without sacrificing pawns. If you refuse to sacrifice any pieces, you will lose the entire game.

## What It Illustrates
You must be willing to make hard decisions, like laying off staff, to save the overall company from failing.
