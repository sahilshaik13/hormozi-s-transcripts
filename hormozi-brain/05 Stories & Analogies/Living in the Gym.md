# Living in the Gym

> I lived inside my gym like it was fine it was just not legal

## The Story
To avoid paying rent and keep his personal expenses at absolute zero, Hormozi illegally slept inside his own gym facility while building his business.

## What It Illustrates
Extreme sacrifice and minimizing personal overhead is often required to survive and scale a business.
