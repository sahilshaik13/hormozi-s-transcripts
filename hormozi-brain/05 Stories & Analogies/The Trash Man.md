# The Trash Man

> I went to all the big apartments and said I’d do their trash for a whole year free if they contracted me to do the next five years paid.

## The Story
A neighbor built a highly lucrative trash business by offering large apartment complexes one year of free trash service in exchange for signing a five-year paid contract.

## What It Illustrates
Frontloading a massive continuity discount can secure long-term, highly profitable contracts if you can survive the initial cash flow dip.
