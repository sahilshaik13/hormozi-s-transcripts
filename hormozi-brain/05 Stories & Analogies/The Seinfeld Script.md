# The Seinfeld Script

> the tone the pauses that you would have would not be the same as his right his intonations would be different than yours are and so as a result he would be funny and you would not be

## The Story
If you took Jerry Seinfeld's exact comedy script and read it on stage, you wouldn't be funny. You would lack his specific intonations, pauses, and delivery.

## What It Illustrates
Sales scripts fail not because the words are wrong, but because the salesperson lacks the correct tone and cadence of the master who wrote it.
