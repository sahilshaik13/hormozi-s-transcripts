# Exiting the Matrix to Create Art

> When you make things just for the sake of making them like art then they have they take on a whole different form

## The Story
A friend who sold his company for $200M noted that 99% of people operate from a place of lack, trying to get money from others. Exiting the matrix means creating things purely for the sake of making them, like art.

## What It Illustrates
Operating from intention and abundance changes the form and impact of what you create.
