# Itty Bitty Packages

> a jumbo source of protein in itty bitty packages called the shrimp

## The Story
He describes shrimp not just as seafood, but as a highly efficient delivery mechanism for macronutrients, framing them as tiny packages holding massive protein.

## What It Illustrates
High nutritional leverage can be found in small, easy-to-consume formats.
