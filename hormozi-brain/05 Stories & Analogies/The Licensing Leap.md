# The Licensing Leap

> the year that we made the transition halfway through is what jumped us from two or three to seven and it was just in the last like four months that we saw this crazy growth and then we did 28 million the next year

## The Story
Hormozi had a chain of gyms and possessed all the necessary sales and lead-gen skills, but lacked the belief to license the model. After borrowing his wife's belief in him and making the transition, the business scaled from $3M to $28M in a year.

## What It Illustrates
You can have all the skills and character required for massive scale, but you will remain stuck until you adopt the belief required for the next level.
