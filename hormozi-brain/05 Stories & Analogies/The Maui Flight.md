# The Maui Flight

> cellification not the plane flight

## The Story
When selling a trip to Maui, you don't advertise the TSA lines, the turbulence, the lost baggage, or the bad plane food. You sell the beach, the drinks, and the destination.

## What It Illustrates
Stop selling the features, modules, and hard work of your program. Sell the final result the prospect actually wants.
