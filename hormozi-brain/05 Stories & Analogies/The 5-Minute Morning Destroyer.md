# The 5-Minute Morning Destroyer

> that five-minute meeting blows their entire morning because they can't do the development work they need

## The Story
A manager asks a maker for a quick 5-minute meeting at 10:00 AM. To the manager, it's just one of 20 small slots. To the maker, it creates an open loop that ruins their focus beforehand and breaks their flow afterward, destroying their entire morning.

## What It Illustrates
A seemingly cheap interruption for a manager is disproportionately expensive for a maker.
