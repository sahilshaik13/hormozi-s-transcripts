# The Pretzel, Water, and Cookie

> if you properly solve a problem for a customer there's almost always another problem that gets presented as a result of the solution

## The Story
If you solve someone's hunger with a salty pretzel, they naturally become thirsty. You then solve that with water. Once their thirst is quenched, their dessert stomach is empty, so you offer a cookie.

## What It Illustrates
Solving one problem for a customer always creates the next problem, allowing you to build an ecosystem of products.
