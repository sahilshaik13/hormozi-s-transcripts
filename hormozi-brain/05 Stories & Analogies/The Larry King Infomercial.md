# The Larry King Infomercial

> he re-recorded the first first 30 seconds with the hourlong interview and then reran the infomercial and did 100 million in sales

## The Story
Dean Graziosi ran a $250k ad campaign with Larry King that flopped. He realized the hook was wrong, flew Larry King back out to re-record just the first 30 seconds, reran the ad, and made $100 million.

## What It Illustrates
The first 5 seconds and the hook dictate the success of the entire advertisement.
