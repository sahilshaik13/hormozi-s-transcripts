# The Life Raft in the Pool

> When you have your life raft and you're the only boat in town, they've got to pay whatever you want to scoop them out of the water.

## The Story
If you are the only life raft in a pool full of struggling people, they have to pay whatever you want to scoop them out of the water.

## What It Illustrates
Niche specificity eliminates competition and gives you absolute pricing power.
