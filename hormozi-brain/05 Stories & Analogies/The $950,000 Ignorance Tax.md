# The $950,000 Ignorance Tax

> right now you pay reality $950,000 every single year for not knowing how to make a million dollars

## The Story
A speaker asked a woman making $50,000 a year how much she makes. He then wrote $1,000,000 on a whiteboard and told her she pays reality $950,000 every year for not knowing how to make a million dollars.

## What It Illustrates
The cost of not having a skill is the massive amount of money you lose out on every single year, making education the highest ROI investment.
