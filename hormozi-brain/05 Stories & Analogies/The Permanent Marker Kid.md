# The Permanent Marker Kid

> I needed to close the card to pay for the paint and so it's like I can scold the kid and lose the sail or I can close the sail and keep my cool

## The Story
While trying to close a sale with a mother, her child was drawing on the gym wall with a permanent marker. Hormozi had to suppress his anger and close the sale so he could afford the paint to fix the wall.

## What It Illustrates
When you are broke, you must swallow your pride and endure extreme discomfort to secure the resources you need to survive.
