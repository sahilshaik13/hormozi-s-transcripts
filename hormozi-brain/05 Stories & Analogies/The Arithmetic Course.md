# The Arithmetic Course

> the day before you needed to learn how to do arithmetic arithmetic is super valuable the day after you learn it it has zero additional value

## The Story
If you buy a course to learn arithmetic, it is incredibly valuable right up until the moment you learn it. The day after you learn it, the ongoing value of that course drops to zero.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates 'one-time value' and why charging a high monthly recurring fee for information causes massive churn.
