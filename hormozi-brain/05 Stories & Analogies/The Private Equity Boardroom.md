# The Private Equity Boardroom

> when I saw this the town on this side I was like oh that's why these guys are going to make $3 billion over the next 5 years

## The Story
When selling Gym Launch, Hormozi sat in a boardroom and compared his team (who built a $100M company) to the private equity firm's team. The stark contrast in talent made him realize why the PE firm would make billions.

## What It Illustrates
The hardest part of entrepreneurship is learning what 'good' talent actually looks like at each new level of scale.
