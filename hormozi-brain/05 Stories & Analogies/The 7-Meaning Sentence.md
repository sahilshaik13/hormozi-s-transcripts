# The 7-Meaning Sentence

> each of those sentences has different meaning right because of the emphasis that we put on the words

## The Story
Taking the sentence 'I didn't say he hit his wife' and emphasizing a different word each time it is spoken. Depending on which word is stressed, the sentence takes on seven entirely different meanings.

## What It Illustrates
Emphasis and cadence completely change the meaning of the words you are saying.
