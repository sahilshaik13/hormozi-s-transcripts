# Gym Launch Market Expansion

> I asked our our team one question I said of these different potential Avenues or avatars that we could pursue who do we provide the most value to by dollars made

## The Story
Gym Launch saturated the micro-gym market, speaking to 20,000 out of 50,000 total gyms in the US. To grow, they evaluated adjacent markets and expanded up-market to health clubs because they could immediately add tens of thousands of dollars to their bottom line.

## What It Illustrates
How to strategically choose an expansion market based on where you can deliver the highest financial value.
