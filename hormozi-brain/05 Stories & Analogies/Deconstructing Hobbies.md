# Deconstructing Hobbies

> Just changing how I talk about something can make it sound cringe.

## The Story
Hormozi describes bodybuilding as 'obsessing over muscles oiling up and posing in tiny trunks', chess as 'staring at a board for hours in dead silence', and writing a book as 'locking yourself away for months'.

## What It Illustrates
Anyone can make absolutely anything sound cringe just by changing how they talk about it and framing it dismissively.
