# The Sell-Out Band

> whenever a band goes from like a small town to like they go they go bigger audience they go more mass Market people are like the band's selling out

## The Story
When a local band gets big and reaches a mass market, early fans accuse them of selling out. In reality, the band simply morphed their packaging to reach and help the maximum number of people.

## What It Illustrates
Evolving your content or product to appeal to a broader audience isn't selling out; it's scaling your impact.
