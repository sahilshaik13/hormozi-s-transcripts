# The Bank as a Middleman

> they take your money for 0.1 percent a year and then they give it to the government and the government gives them four percent a year

## The Story
When you put money in a bank, you are loaning them money at 0.1% interest. The bank then turns around and loans that exact same money to the U.S. government by buying bonds at 4% interest, making 40 times what they pay you while taking on zero risk.

## What It Illustrates
Banks are just arbitraging your cash; you can cut them out and buy government debt directly for higher returns and lower risk.
