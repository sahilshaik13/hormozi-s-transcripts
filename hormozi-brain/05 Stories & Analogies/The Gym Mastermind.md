# The Gym Mastermind

> I might as well ask them who have 5 10 years each in you know industry experience and I'll just start day one 10 years ahead.

## The Story
Hormozi paid to join a mastermind for gym owners before he even owned a gym. He wanted to learn where to put locations, how to pay rent, and how to find trainers from people with decades of experience.

## What It Illustrates
It is better to ask for help and learn from others' experience than to make mistakes yourself out of ego.
