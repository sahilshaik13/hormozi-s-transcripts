# The Glass Timeline

> you have to eat the glass so that you can take the pain today and you're trying to take as much of the pain from tomorrow as you can today

## The Story
Life is a timeline you pull towards yourself. The beginning is covered in coarse, broken glass, but it eventually gets smoother and sweeter. You have to aggressively pull the glass towards you and endure the pain now to reach the sweet part before you die.

## What It Illustrates
You must front-load your suffering and work double-time today to enjoy the compounding benefits of success later in life.
