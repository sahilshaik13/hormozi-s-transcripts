# The Awards Dinner Epiphany

> The bottom of the list feeds the top of the list.

## The Story
While stressing over how to replace himself in ads, Hormozi attended a customer awards dinner. Listening to their success stories, he realized their stories were the exact marketing material he needed.

## What It Illustrates
Your customers' success stories are your best marketing assets.
