# The $1,000 Book Pitch

> that's how you increase leverage to sell and offer set for 25 buckets for a hundred thousand dollars

## The Story
Hormozi asks the audience to stand if they would pay $1,000 for a book. Initially, only a few stand. He progressively adds bonuses: consulting, traffic breakdowns, recruiting help, media exposure, and a guarantee. Eventually, the whole room stands.

## What It Illustrates
Leverage means getting more output (money) for the same input (selling one book) by making the offer significantly better.
