# The $50k Consulting Day

> it is remarkable how low the bar is to beat everyone else

## The Story
Hormozi charged a client $50,000 for a single consulting day. He woke up at 5 AM and spent 4 hours prepping, arriving with 5 pages of specific notes. The client was blown away, having never seen anyone so prepared.

## What It Illustrates
The bar for preparation in business is remarkably low, making it very easy to stand out with just a few hours of focused work.
