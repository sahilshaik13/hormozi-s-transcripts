# The De Beers Diamond Monopoly

> believe it or not there's more diamonds there are dishwashers in the United States but they're valued more because they control Supply to keep the prices high

## The Story
The De Beers group bought up the majority of diamond mines and distribution networks. Even though diamonds are more common than dishwashers, they artificially restrict the supply to keep prices exorbitant.

## What It Illustrates
Exclusive control over a resource allows you to dictate supply, demand, and pricing, creating a massive competitive moat.
