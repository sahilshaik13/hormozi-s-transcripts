# The Shovel Analogy

> if you buy a shovel you'll be significantly better than people who don't have a shovel

## The Story
If you are competing against people digging holes with their bare hands, buying a shovel costs money upfront but makes you significantly faster and better than the competition.

## What It Illustrates
Capital expenditure (capex) can be used as a competitive moat to outpace and price out competitors.
