# The Pound of Broccoli

> Hey, I don’t want you to have to eat a pound of broccoli, which is why I had this.

## The Story
When a customer is on a budget, Hormozi removes a supplement from their stack but explains they will now have to eat a massive amount of broccoli to get the same nutrients. Faced with the inconvenience, the customer usually opts to just buy the supplement.

## What It Illustrates
Never remove a product to save a prospect money without reintroducing the painful problem that the product was solving.
