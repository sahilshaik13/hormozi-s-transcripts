# The Muscle Building Analogy

> When you lift weights, your muscles don't get bigger. they get smaller and then bigger. You supercompensate.

## The Story
When you lift weights, your muscles don't immediately get bigger. They actually get smaller and tear down first, and then they supercompensate and grow larger.

## What It Illustrates
Growth requires a temporary step backward or a period of pain before the outsized return is realized.
