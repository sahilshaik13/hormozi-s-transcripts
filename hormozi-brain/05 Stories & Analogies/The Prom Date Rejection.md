# The Prom Date Rejection

> you lose nothing from no you only stand something to gain from yeses

## The Story
If you ask a girl to prom and she says no, you still have no prom date (the exact same position as before), but you gained experience. If you ask 100 girls, one will eventually say yes.

## What It Illustrates
You lose nothing from a 'no' and only gain from a 'yes', making rejection a risk-free learning experience.
