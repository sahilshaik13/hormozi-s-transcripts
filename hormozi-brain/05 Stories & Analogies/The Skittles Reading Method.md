# The Skittles Reading Method

> he reinforce him doing it every time he had a bigger reinforcement for when every time he hit a new record

## The Story
A trainer wanted to teach a distracted kid to read. He gave the kid one Skittle for reading three words. When the kid read four words (a new personal best), he gave him two Skittles. By the end of the session, the kid was reading entire pages.

## What It Illustrates
Immediate, escalating positive reinforcement for breaking personal records rapidly builds new skills.
