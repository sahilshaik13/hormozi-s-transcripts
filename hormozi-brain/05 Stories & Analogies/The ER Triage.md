# The ER Triage

> if you get hurt the first thing you do when you go to the emergency is they have triage they basically figure out how badly hurt you are and where you need to go

## The Story
When you go to the emergency room, the first step is triage to figure out how badly hurt you are and where you need to go. An escalations team does the exact same thing for a business.

## What It Illustrates
You need a dedicated catch-all team to assess and route customer issues so they don't bottleneck the sales team.
