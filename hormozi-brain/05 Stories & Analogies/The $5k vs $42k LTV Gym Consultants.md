# The $5k vs $42k LTV Gym Consultants

> he had the same sales velocity as me so he was selling the same number of people as I was but we were making 70 times more profit than he was

## The Story
Alex and a competitor had the same sales velocity and CAC. The competitor accepted anyone who wanted to make money in fitness and had a $5k LTV. Alex strictly required a signed lease, an employee, and 30+ members, resulting in a $42k LTV and 70x more profit.

## What It Illustrates
Qualifying your customers strictly and turning away beginners drastically increases lifetime value and profit.
