# Backyard Football vs. Fundamentals

## The Story
A private equity CEO mentor explained that kids play 'backyard football' with complex trick plays and end up fumbling. Pros play 'fundamentals football'—just two big guys pushing up the middle.

## What It Illustrates
Don't get cute or fancy in business; execute the basic fundamentals flawlessly.
