# The Manager Who Wanted to be Poor

> He said, 'I'll just be poor then.' And I remember him saying that and it was like my world like twirled around me.

## The Story
Hormozi promoted his best trainer to manager. Six months later, the employee asked to step down because he hated the stress, even though it meant his pay would be cut in half.

## What It Illustrates
Not everyone is motivated by money or upward mobility; you must manage people according to their own desires, not yours.
