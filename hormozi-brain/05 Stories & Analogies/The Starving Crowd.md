# The Starving Crowd

> You could have the worst hot dogs, terrible prices, and be in a terrible location, but if you’re the only hot dog stand in town and the local college football game breaks out, you’re going to sell out.

## The Story
A marketing professor asks students what single advantage they'd want for a hotdog stand. They guess location, quality, or price. The professor reveals the true answer: a starving crowd.

## What It Illustrates
A desperate market with high demand will buy even if your product, pricing, and marketing are mediocre.
