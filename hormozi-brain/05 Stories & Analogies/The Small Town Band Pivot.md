# The Small Town Band Pivot

> Losing audience is a part of life. You just wanna make sure you gain more than you lose.

## The Story
When a local band hits it big and goes mainstream, they inevitably lose some of their original local fans who accuse them of 'selling out.' However, they gamble this small loss for a massive gain in global audience and financial security.

## What It Illustrates
Brand pivots will always alienate some existing followers, but the goal is to ensure a net positive gain in audience size and profitability.
