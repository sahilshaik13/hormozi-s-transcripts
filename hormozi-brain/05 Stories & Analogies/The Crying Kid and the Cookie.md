# The Crying Kid and the Cookie

> kids cry parent gives them a cookie and you now teach them to cry to get cookies

## The Story
A kid cries, so the parent gives them a cookie to calm them down. The parent thinks they solved the crying, but they actually just trained the kid to cry whenever they want a cookie.

## What It Illustrates
Misapplied rewards reinforce the exact negative behavior you are trying to stop.
