# The S&P 500 Machine

> imagine investing in the S&P 500 put $3,400 in at the end of the year it's 78,000 you'd be like holy cow this is the most amazing investment in my life

## The Story
Imagine an investment where you put in $3,400 and at the end of the year it turns into $78,000. Anyone would consider that the greatest investment of their life and pour money into it.

## What It Illustrates
Business owners often have incredibly profitable acquisition channels but artificially cap their spend due to fear, missing out on massive compounding returns.
