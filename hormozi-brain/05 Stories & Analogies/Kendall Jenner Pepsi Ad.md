# Kendall Jenner Pepsi Ad

> So it was good advertising but bad branding. Many customers hated this pairing.

## The Story
Pepsi ran an ad featuring Kendall Jenner at a protest. It got massive reach but immediate backlash, forcing them to pull it in six days because it hurt sales.

## What It Illustrates
High reach (good advertising) paired with something the audience dislikes (bad branding) results in lost revenue.
