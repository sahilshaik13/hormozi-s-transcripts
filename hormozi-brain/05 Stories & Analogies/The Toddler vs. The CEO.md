# The Toddler vs. The CEO

> the less skilled you are the more broken down the command must be in order to follow it

## The Story
If you tell a CEO to 'grow the business', they can do it. If you tell a toddler, they fail. To get the toddler to do it, you must break the command down to 'click the blue button, type facebook.com'.

## What It Illustrates
The less skilled a person is, the more broken down and operationalized the instructions must be.
