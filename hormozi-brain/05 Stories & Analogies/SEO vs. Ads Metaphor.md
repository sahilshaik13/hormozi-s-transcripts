# SEO vs. Ads Metaphor

> Use metaphors to connect stuff people don't know with stuff they do.

## The Story
Explaining SEO by comparing ads to working for commission (you only get paid when you work) and SEO to an investment account (you put money in early, and it compounds over time to become your main income).

## What It Illustrates
Use metaphors to connect technical jargon prospects don't understand with everyday concepts they do understand.
