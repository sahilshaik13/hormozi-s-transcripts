# The Dietitian's Billing System

> She took something that she learned from her profession and made a business around something really, really narrow.

## The Story
A registered dietitian working 12-hour days had to figure out a complex insurance billing system to see side clients on her one day off. She quit to teach this narrow skill to other dietitians and now makes almost $1M/year with only 5,800 followers.

## What It Illustrates
Solving one very specific professional problem is enough to build a highly lucrative business without needing a massive audience.
