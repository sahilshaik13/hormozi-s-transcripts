# The Mentor's Social Media Volume

> I just need to do way more.

## The Story
Hormozi asked a more successful creator for advice. The mentor simply had Hormozi compare their daily social media posts side-by-side. The mentor had posted 10 times on LinkedIn and 3 times on Instagram that day, while Hormozi had posted once or not at all.

## What It Illustrates
The gap between you and the people ahead of you is usually just a massive discrepancy in sheer volume of output.
