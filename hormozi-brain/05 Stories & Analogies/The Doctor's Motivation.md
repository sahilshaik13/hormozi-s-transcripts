# The Doctor's Motivation

> the only reason she lost weight was because the doctor told her that she wasn't going to see my grandkids.

## The Story
Hormozi's mother tried to lose weight for 30 years without success. She only finally lost the weight when a doctor told her she wouldn't live to see her grandkids.

## What It Illustrates
Motivation happens when you find the specific trigger that drastically changes the relative value of an outcome for an individual.
