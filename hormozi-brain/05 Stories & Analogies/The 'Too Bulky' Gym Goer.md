# The 'Too Bulky' Gym Goer

> you're talking about how you're not going to be able to fit in your clothes and you haven't even lost weight

## The Story
People walk into a gym and say they don't want to lift weights because they don't want to get 'too bulky', ignoring the years of work required to reach that extreme.

## What It Illustrates
Worrying about how a process will handle $100M in revenue when you currently make $0 is a false excuse to avoid taking the first step.
