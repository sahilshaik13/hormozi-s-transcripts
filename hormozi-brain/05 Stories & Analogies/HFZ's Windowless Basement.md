# HFZ's Windowless Basement

> They operationalize putting someone in a hole and not letting anything bother them.

## The Story
An accelerator called HFZ beats Y Combinator's returns by flying founders out and putting them in windowless basement rooms. They handle all life tasks so the founders only face the work, removing all distractions.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates the immense compounding value of uninterrupted flow and refusing to context-switch.
