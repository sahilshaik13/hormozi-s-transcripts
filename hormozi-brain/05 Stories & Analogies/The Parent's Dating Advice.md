# The Parent's Dating Advice

> they're right with every single person you ever date except for the person you marry and then they're 100 wrong

## The Story
Parents will predict that you will break up with every person you date. They will be right 99% of the time, but 100% wrong about the one person you eventually marry.

## What It Illustrates
Naysayers will be right about your many failures, but completely wrong about the one success that permanently changes your life.
