# Baseball vs. Business

> In business, every once in a while, when you step up to the plate, you can score a thousand runs.

## The Story
In baseball, no matter how hard you hit the ball, the maximum number of runs you can score is four. In business, stepping up to the plate can occasionally result in scoring a thousand runs.

## What It Illustrates
Business has a long-tail distribution of returns, meaning taking bold risks is mathematically favorable because one massive win pays for all the strikeouts.
