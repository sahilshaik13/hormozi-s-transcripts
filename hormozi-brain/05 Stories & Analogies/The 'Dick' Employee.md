# The 'Dick' Employee

> stop being a dick is actually a pretty terrible directive and the reason is because they don't know what they're doing

## The Story
A highly productive employee was acting like a jerk, and previous leaders telling him to 'be nicer' failed. Hormozi gave him a specific list of behaviors to stop doing at the start of calls. The employee complied, and within a month, his reputation completely turned around.

## What It Illustrates
Telling someone to change their personality doesn't work; giving them specific, unbundled instructions on how to behave does.
