# The Jingle Bells Story

> failed musician failed at everything but it's like he won you know what I mean it's like if all of that was in order to have this one Timeless thing

## The Story
A man who was objectively a failure at everything in his life wrote a little song called Jingle Bells. That one success impacted millions of lives and rewrote his entire history.

## What It Illustrates
One massive win can completely erase a lifetime of failures.
