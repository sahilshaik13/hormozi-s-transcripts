# The 365 Cookie Batches

> he spent 365 days doing the work to figure out the hundred tiny details that make a perfect cookie

## The Story
A friend wanted to start a cookie business. He learned from top bakers, then baked a new batch every day for a year (365 batches), giving them away for feedback. When he finally opened, he had a massive local success.

## What It Illustrates
Doing the unscalable, tedious work of perfecting the product creates an unbeatable competitive advantage.
