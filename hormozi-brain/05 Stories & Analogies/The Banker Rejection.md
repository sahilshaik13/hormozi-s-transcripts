# The Banker Rejection

> If selling means becoming an employee of the very business I used to own, what’s the point? It’s unsellable.

## The Story
In 2019, Hormozi tried to sell Gym Launch, which was making millions a month. The bankers rejected it because the business relied entirely on his face for ads, making it unsellable unless he stayed on as an employee.

## What It Illustrates
A business that relies on the founder's face for customer acquisition is a risky, unsellable asset.
