# The Marathon Middle

> It's the mundane middle. You have to master the middle. That's where all the gains come in.

## The Story
Skill development is like running a marathon. People cheer for you when you start, and they cheer for you when you cross the finish line, but for the 26 miles in between, no one cares and it is just a long, grueling grind.

## What It Illustrates
You must master the mundane, unglamorous middle of the process, because that is where all the actual gains are made.
