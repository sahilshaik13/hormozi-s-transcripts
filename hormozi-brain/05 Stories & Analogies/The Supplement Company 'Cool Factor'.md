# The Supplement Company 'Cool Factor'

> I tried to sell him the way I’d want to be sold with the dollars and cents. But what took me too long to realize was - he was also getting something else from the other company my products didn’t deliver - cool factor.

## The Story
Hormozi built a supplement company with superior ingredients and offered a 40% affiliate commission compared to competitors' 10-20%. Despite the obvious financial upside, his friend wouldn't switch over because Hormozi's brand lacked the 'cool factor' of the competitor.

## What It Illustrates
Product quality and superior financial incentives are not enough to change consumer behavior if your brand lacks positive, desirable associations.
