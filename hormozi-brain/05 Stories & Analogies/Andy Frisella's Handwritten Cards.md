# Andy Frisella's Handwritten Cards

> in the beginning you'd be like you're the owner how could you write handwritten cards to every single person that buys the product that's not scalable

## The Story
When starting 1st Phorm, Andy wrote handwritten cards to every customer. As they scaled to hundreds of millions, he transitioned to signing them, and eventually having others write them.

## What It Illustrates
Unscalable gestures can evolve into 2.0 and 3.0 versions that still capture 80% of the original impact at scale.
