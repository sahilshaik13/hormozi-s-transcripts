# The Wonder Bread Restaurant

> because everyone who has your food literally tells everyone that it [ __ ] sucks and never come back

## The Story
Someone starts a restaurant by slapping a slice of bologna between two pieces of Wonder Bread, then markets it aggressively. They wonder why the business fails, not realizing that every customer tells others how terrible the food is.

## What It Illustrates
Shortcutting product quality to scale marketing faster will destroy a business through negative word-of-mouth.
