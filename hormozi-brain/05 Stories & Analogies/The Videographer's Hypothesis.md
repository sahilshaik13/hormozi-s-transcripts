# The Videographer's Hypothesis

> wow Timmy who's making these video clips is actually thinking about what I care about which is how I'm going to get more customers

## The Story
A videographer sets a goal to double the amount of shorts produced to get more website traffic. At the end of the quarter, they doubled the shorts (input succeeded) but traffic didn't increase (hypothesis failed). They then iterate the hypothesis to focus on story-based content instead of just volume.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates how lower-level employees can use hypothesis-driven goals to align their daily work with overarching business drivers and iterate when things don't work.
