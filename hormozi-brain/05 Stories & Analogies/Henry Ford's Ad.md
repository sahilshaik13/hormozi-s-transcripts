# Henry Ford's Ad

> we get so much sicker of our own content before our audience even remembers your name

## The Story
Henry Ford walked past his marketing department's mockups for three months and complained he was sick of seeing the ad. His marketing director replied they hadn't even started running it yet.

## What It Illustrates
Creators get sick of their own marketing long before the audience even notices it.
