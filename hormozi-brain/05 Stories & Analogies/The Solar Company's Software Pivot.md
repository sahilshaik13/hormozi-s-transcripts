# The Solar Company's Software Pivot

> if you have a service based business that has the exact same economics as a software company you will get software like multiples

## The Story
A highly profitable solar sales company wanted to license their internal recruiting software to get a 'software multiple' valuation. Hormozi warned them that investors buy unit economics, not labels, and their software revenue would pale in comparison to their solar revenue.

## What It Illustrates
You cannot trick investors into higher valuations by pretending to be a different business model.
