# The Real Estate Arbitrage

> what you really want is the Arbitrage between something that you are fine with that would quote devalue your property that someone else isn't fine with

## The Story
Rory Sutherland suggested realtors should advertise the negative aspects of a property, like being next to a noisy pub. While a dealbreaker for some, it's a massive pro for someone who loves nightlife.

## What It Illustrates
In dating, advertising your flaws helps you find the specific person who views your negatives as positives, creating a perfect match.
