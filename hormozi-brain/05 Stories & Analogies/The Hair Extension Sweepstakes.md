# The Hair Extension Sweepstakes

> Well, if I were selling hair extensions, I would probably have sweep stakes and lottery tickets inside of the hair, which then creates a marketing motion that I can put around it

## The Story
A hypothetical hair extension brand puts lottery tickets inside their products. Winners are flown out to the salon where the founder installs the extensions in person. This live installation is documented and used as marketing material.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates how to engineer real-world, un-fakeable proof into a standard consumer product business to create a self-sustaining marketing loop.
