# Michael Jordan's Random Tuesday Game

> I know that there's one guy who's bringing his son and they saved up for months to go to this one Bulls game

## The Story
Michael Jordan played with maximum intensity every game because he knew there was always a father and son who had saved up for months to see him play just that one time.

## What It Illustrates
Bring high energy and value to your content, even if the audience is tiny, because there is a real person on the other side.
