# The Google AdSense Origin Story

> he didn't sit down with them and be like look I think you need to do this... he just said these ads suck

## The Story
Larry Page printed out terrible Google search ads, wrote 'These ads suck' on the paper, and pinned it to the wall in the engineering department. An engineer saw it, stayed up all night, and invented AdSense.

## What It Illustrates
High-level talent doesn't need micromanagement or long meetings; they just need the problem clearly identified.
