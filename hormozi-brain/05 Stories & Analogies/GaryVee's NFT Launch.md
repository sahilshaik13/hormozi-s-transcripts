# GaryVee's NFT Launch

> he spent i want to say 10 years or 12 years building his personal brand and then he finally had his ask which was hey buy my nft

## The Story
Gary Vaynerchuk spent 10 to 12 years building his personal brand by giving away free content. When he finally made a massive direct response ask by launching an NFT, he made around $100 million in six months.

## What It Illustrates
A decade of delayed asks and brand building can result in an astronomical payload when a direct response mechanism is finally used.
