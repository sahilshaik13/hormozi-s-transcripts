# The 5% Inconsistent Cell Phone

> just having that little 5% inconsistency and the business owner of the phone's like what are they talking about this thing does the internet it does this it does that but just one out of 20 times you want to kill somebody

## The Story
If a cell phone works perfectly 95% of the time but fails 1 out of 20 times you use it, you would want to throw it against a wall, despite all its amazing features.

## What It Illustrates
Inconsistency destroys the perceived value of a product, no matter how good the core features are.
