# The Zero-Profit Ad Agency

> so what you guys have figured out is a way to take $2,000 and turn it into zero

## The Story
An agency boasted a 5:1 return, turning a $2,000 CAC into a $10,000 LTV. However, after deducting $1,000 for ad spend and $1,000 for account rep payroll per month, their gross profit was exactly zero.

## What It Illustrates
LTV must be calculated as Lifetime Gross Profit, not top-line revenue, or you will scale yourself into bankruptcy.
