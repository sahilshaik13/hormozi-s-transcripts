# The Baseball Fat Pitch

> Don't be in the bleachers when the fat pitch comes. You want to be at the plate.

## The Story
You don't wait for the perfect pitch to arrive before you start practicing your swing, doing sprints, and going to the gym. You prepare beforehand so you can smash it out of the park when it comes.

## What It Illustrates
You must build capacity and prepare before the opportunity arrives, otherwise you get lapped by those who did.
