# The Mercedes Flyer Mistake

> I was looking at his results from 150,000 Flyers over 30 days and comparing them to my results of 300 in a Day

## The Story
Hormozi put out 300 flyers to promote his gym. He got one call from a guy claiming he damaged his Mercedes. His mentor revealed he tests with 5,000 flyers and distributes 150,000 a month.

## What It Illustrates
Beginners fail because they test with insignificant volume. You need massive volume to see real results.
