# The Volatile SEO Agency

> they're not going to think oh well he I 10x my money on month one this guy's already paid for himself for almost the whole year no they're going to think well I got a good return last month and I got a crap return this month and so I'm going to cancel

## The Story
An SEO agency gets a client a 10x return in month one, but only a 1x return in month two. If billed monthly, the client cancels in month two because they only look at recent performance. If billed annually, the client looks at the cumulative return over the year and stays.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates the power of the 'Lookback Window' to smooth out volatile delivery and retain clients.
