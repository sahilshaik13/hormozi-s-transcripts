# The Video Game Reinforcement

> the difference between a video game and life is that that interval isn't set and so you're not necessarily going to have a quick win at level one

## The Story
Video games are designed to give you quick, easy wins at level one to hook you, slowly extending the time between rewards. Life is like a video game, but the interval isn't set, meaning you might get zero rewards until level 20.

## What It Illustrates
You have to be willing to suck for a very long time without external reinforcement before you achieve mastery.
