# The Construction Buddy

> the money or you were leaving on the table is the focus that you're not giving to the one thing that matters

## The Story
A friend running a $3M roofing business started doing general contracting and flipping houses because he didn't want to 'leave money on the table.' Meanwhile, Diane Hendricks built a massive fortune by focusing exclusively on roofing.

## What It Illustrates
Doing multiple things at once dilutes focus and prevents you from building a billion-dollar enterprise.
