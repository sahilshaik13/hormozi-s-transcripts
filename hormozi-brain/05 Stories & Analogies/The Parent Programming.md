# The Parent Programming

> what person do you think in your life has super high max status, power, credibility, and likeness? Maxed out earlier on in your life. It's your parents

## The Story
Parents control your food, give you instructions that keep you safe, and look like you. This gives them max SPCL early in your life.

## What It Illustrates
Influence is deeply programmed through repeated cycles of status, power, credibility, and likeness.
