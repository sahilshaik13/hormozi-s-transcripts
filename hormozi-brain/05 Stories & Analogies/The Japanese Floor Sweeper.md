# The Japanese Floor Sweeper

> I imagine that they're paying me a million dollars every single day to sweep the floors.

## The Story
A man swept floors for 35 years and did an amazing job. When asked how he made such boring work interesting, he explained that he imagined he was being paid a million dollars every day to sweep the floors.

## What It Illustrates
The way you approach your work can fundamentally change the experience of the work itself.
