# The 13-Year-Old DJ

> I had this idea to start my own DJ company and hire myself

## The Story
Harley wanted to be a DJ but no company would hire a 13-year-old. His dad made him business cards, giving him the confidence to start his own DJ company and hire himself.

## What It Illustrates
Entrepreneurship is a tool to solve problems and bypass traditional gatekeepers.
