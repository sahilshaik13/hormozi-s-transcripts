# The Crumpled Paper Catch

> Well, it's very hard to catch all four at the same time. But if I just said, "Hey, real quick, I want you to understand something." catch, you'd probably be able to catch that.

## The Story
If you throw four crumpled pieces of paper at someone simultaneously, they will drop all of them. If you throw just one, they will catch it easily.

## What It Illustrates
Communicating multiple marketing benefits at once guarantees the audience absorbs none of them; stick to one clear message.
