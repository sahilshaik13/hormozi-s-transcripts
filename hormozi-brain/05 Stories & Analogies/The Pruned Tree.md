# The Pruned Tree

> the way to keep a tree healthy is to prune it and pruning literally means you're cutting things off

## The Story
A business is like a tree. If resources go into little offshoots (B+ problems), the tree stops growing taller. If you cut those off, all resources go into the trunk, driving maximum vertical growth.

## What It Illustrates
Eliminating good but non-essential ideas is required to maximize revenue growth.
