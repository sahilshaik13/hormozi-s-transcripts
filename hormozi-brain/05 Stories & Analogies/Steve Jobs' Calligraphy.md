# Steve Jobs' Calligraphy

> the lessons you learn on the first thing you do don't become apparent sometimes until the third fourth or fifth thing you do

## The Story
Steve Jobs took a seemingly useless calligraphy class. Years later, when building the Mac, that specific knowledge allowed Apple to create beautiful, diverse fonts.

## What It Illustrates
Skills learned in one domain provide unique, compounding advantages in future, seemingly unrelated domains.
