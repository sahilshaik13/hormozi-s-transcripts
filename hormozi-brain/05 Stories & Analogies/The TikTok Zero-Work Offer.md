# The TikTok Zero-Work Offer

> I actually bought the Sops automatically and we were able to massively scale our output on short content within six or so months

## The Story
An agency offered to repurpose Hormozi's existing content into TikTok shorts requiring zero effort from him. After seeing traction, instead of hiring a premium agency to learn the nuances, he hired a Director of Brand who had previously worked at a premium agency, instantly acquiring the SOPs.

## What It Illustrates
You can bypass the agency learning phase entirely if you can directly hire an individual who already possesses the premium agency's SOPs and expertise.
