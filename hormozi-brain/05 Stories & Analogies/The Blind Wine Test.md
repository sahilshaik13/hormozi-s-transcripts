# The Blind Wine Test

> there is a bi-directional relations between price and value

## The Story
Scientists gave people the exact same wine but labeled them as cheap, medium, and expensive. People rated the 'expensive' wine as tasting the best.

## What It Illustrates
Price itself is a component of value; charging more makes people perceive the product as better.
