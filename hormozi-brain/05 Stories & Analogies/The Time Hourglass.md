# The Time Hourglass

> the most amount of money least amount of time that is what you are you're spending your time doing

## The Story
Imagine an hourglass representing your time and money. At the bottom are tasks that take the most time but make the least money. At the top are tasks that take the least time but make the most money.

## What It Illustrates
You must continuously outsource the bottom of the hourglass to work your way up to the top.
