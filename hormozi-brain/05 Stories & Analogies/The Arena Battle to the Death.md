# The Arena Battle to the Death

> You beat everyone. Not almost everyone, everyone.

## The Story
To be in the top 0.01%, you must imagine entering a stadium of 10,000 people in a battle to the death. You cannot just beat almost everyone; you must emerge as the single survivor.

## What It Illustrates
The reality of elite achievement requires a level of sacrifice and ruthlessness that is incompatible with a normal, balanced life.
