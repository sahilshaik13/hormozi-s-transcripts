# The Fitness App Friend

> the difference was his beliefs his skills were the same right his character traits are the same he changed his beliefs

## The Story
A top CrossFit competitor built a fitness app doing $20k/month but refused to promote it because he felt ashamed he wasn't the #1 overall winner. Once he changed his belief that the app was 'good enough', revenue jumped to $150k/month with the exact same skills and character.

## What It Illustrates
A single limiting belief can artificially cap the financial output of your existing skills.
