# Netflix vs. Blockbuster

> Netflix took over Blockbuster because they made consumption of movies and TV shows immediate and your effort and sacrifice... was zero

## The Story
Netflix defeated Blockbuster not by changing the dream outcome (watching a movie), but by reducing the time delay to zero and eliminating the effort and sacrifice of driving to a store and returning a tape.

## What It Illustrates
The competitive moats in big businesses are built on the bottom side of the value equation.
