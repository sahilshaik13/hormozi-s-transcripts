# The Nutrition Consultation Check

> I looked I was just like looking at the check looking at her looking at the check looking at her and I was like okay don't act surprised

## The Story
After setting state records in powerlifting, a woman at the gym asked Hormozi for nutrition help. They met at a pizza place, he gave her advice for an hour, and she unexpectedly handed him a $200 check.

## What It Illustrates
Getting exceptionally good at a skill and having visible evidence of it will naturally lead to people paying you for your expertise.
