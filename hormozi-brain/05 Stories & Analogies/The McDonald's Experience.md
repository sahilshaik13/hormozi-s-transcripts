# The McDonald's Experience

> I want people to know what they're going to get think about McDonald's it's like I don't want any surprises

## The Story
When you go to McDonald's, you expect a Big Mac, fries, and a Coke. You don't want surprises; you want the exact same reliable experience every time.

## What It Illustrates
Email layouts should be consistent and templated so the audience knows exactly what value they are going to get.
