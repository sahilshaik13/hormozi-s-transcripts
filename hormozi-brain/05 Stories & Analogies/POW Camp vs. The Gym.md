# POW Camp vs. The Gym

> in both instances there is physical labor that's happening and everything looks exactly the way it was but in one instance it's seen as psychological torture in the other it seen as self-care

## The Story
In POW camps, prisoners are forced to dig holes and fill them back up as psychological torture to show the futility of labor. In a gym, people pick up heavy weights and put them right back where they found them, which is viewed as self-care.

## What It Illustrates
The exact same physical action can be torture or growth depending entirely on the label we assign to it; you are the asset getting built.
