# The Branded Cow

> Either way, for better or for worse, the brand affected what you did; it affected your behavior.

## The Story
In the wild, an unbranded cow is just a cow, and you might ignore it or eat it. But if a cow has a brand burned into it that you recognize as your neighbor's, your behavior changes—you return it. Even if you don't recognize the specific brand, knowing it is branded changes how you treat it.

## What It Illustrates
Branding fundamentally alters human behavior toward an otherwise commoditized asset.
