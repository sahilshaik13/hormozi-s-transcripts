# The Level 3 Boss

> It's basically like going back to level two when you're at level three and you don't know how to beat that boss you just keep beating level two again because it feels good

## The Story
When an entrepreneur faces a hard problem they don't know how to solve (the Level 3 Boss), they often retreat to doing tasks they already know how to do (beating Level 2 again) because it feels rewarding. But the Level 3 Boss is still waiting.

## What It Illustrates
Don't avoid the primary constraint of your business just because it requires learning something new and facing failure.
