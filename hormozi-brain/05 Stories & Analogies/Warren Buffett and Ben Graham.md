# Warren Buffett and Ben Graham

> graham said you're getting the better end of the deal the actual answer was you're overpriced so free was too expensive for Graham

## The Story
A young Warren Buffett offered to work for legendary investor Ben Graham for free. Graham rejected him, telling Buffett he was 'overpriced'. Graham knew that training Buffett would cost him significant time and effort.

## What It Illustrates
Free is never truly free for the consumer; it still costs them time, effort, and risk. You must respect the investment a 'free' client is making in you.
