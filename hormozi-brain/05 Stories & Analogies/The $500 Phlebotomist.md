# The $500 Phlebotomist

> you would triple your earning capacity after two days and spending 500

## The Story
A high school graduate making minimum wage can spend $500 and two days to get certified as a phlebotomist, instantly tripling their income to $25/hour.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates the massive, immediate ROI of investing in skill acquisition compared to putting small amounts of money into traditional investments.
