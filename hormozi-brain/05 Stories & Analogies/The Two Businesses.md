# The Two Businesses

> unless you have a compounding vehicle within your business the only way to grow is to continue to Market and sell more but I promise you there's a cap to that

## The Story
Business 1 adds 100 customers a year but loses 100% of them, ending year three with 300 customers. Business 2 adds 100 customers a year but keeps them all, also ending year three with 300 customers.

## What It Illustrates
Revenue retention is superior to acquisition because it builds a compounding base, whereas high churn puts a hard cap on growth.
