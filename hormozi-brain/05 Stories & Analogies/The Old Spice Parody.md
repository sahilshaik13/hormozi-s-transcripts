# The Old Spice Parody

> look at your Banky now look at mine now look at your Banky now back to mine

## The Story
The pitch is delivered in the rapid-fire, surreal style of the famous Old Spice commercials, transitioning from bank accounts to parties to engagement rings to being on a horse.

## What It Illustrates
Using familiar, high-energy pop culture formats can make a business pitch highly engaging and memorable.
