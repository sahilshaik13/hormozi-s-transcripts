# The Chef and the Omelette

> If I asked the best chef in the world, hey, can you make me an omelette... He might be like, I don't have any eggs.

## The Story
If you ask the best chef in the world to make an omelette, but he has no eggs, he cannot do it. However, a true leader will then ask why he didn't go get eggs.

## What It Illustrates
Legitimate circumstances can block execution, but it also reveals an employee's agency and problem-solving ability when faced with obstacles.
