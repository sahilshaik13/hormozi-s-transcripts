# Henry Ford's Wages

> Henry Ford paid people better than his competitors so that he could scoop up the entirety of the talent at his plants.

## The Story
Henry Ford is known for the assembly line, but he also paid his workers significantly better than his competitors.

## What It Illustrates
Breaking industry standards on pay is a strategic move to monopolize top talent, rather than a reason to conform to average wages.
