# The Magic Wand

> all the riches you want are on the other side of a few hard conversations

## The Story
Hormozi asked a friend with dozens of failing businesses what would happen if a magic wand eliminated all but his best business. The friend admitted it would be easy to grow, but he avoided it because he didn't want to have the hard conversations required to shut the others down.

## What It Illustrates
Success is often hidden behind the uncomfortable conversations we are avoiding.
