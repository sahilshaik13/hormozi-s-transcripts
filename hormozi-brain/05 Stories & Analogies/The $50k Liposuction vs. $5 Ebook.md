# The $50k Liposuction vs. $5 Ebook

> how is these two things are so different in price right well it's the other three variables

## The Story
Both products promise the exact same dream outcome of weight loss. However, the surgery costs $50,000 while the ebook costs $5 because the surgery has a much higher perceived likelihood of achievement, takes less time to see results, and requires zero effort or sacrifice from the patient.

## What It Illustrates
The bottom variables of the value equation (time and effort) dictate the massive price discrepancy between products with the same dream outcome.
