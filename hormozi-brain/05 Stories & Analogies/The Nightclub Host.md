# The Nightclub Host

> Think about it: when people go to a party, it's not the host that makes the party, it's the other people.

## The Story
Explaining the digital community business model by comparing it to hosting a party. The host doesn't make the party; the guests do. The host just sets the rules, provides the venue, and collects the money at the door.

## What It Illustrates
Complex business models can be easily sold if framed through a familiar, low-friction analogy.
