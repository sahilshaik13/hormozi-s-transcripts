# The Covid Cold Outreach

> Cold Outreach save gym launch during its hardest time during covid

## The Story
During Covid, 30% of Gym Launch's market permanently closed. Hormozi hired a guy whose job offer had been pulled, and together they built a cold outbound machine. In 8 months, they scaled from zero to $480,000 in monthly revenue.

## What It Illustrates
Cold outreach is a bulletproof, reliable way to generate leads in secrecy, even during terrible economic conditions.
