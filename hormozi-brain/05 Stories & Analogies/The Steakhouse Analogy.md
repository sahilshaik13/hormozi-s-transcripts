# The Steakhouse Analogy

> you walk to the restaurant you're starving I say hey how hungry are you you say I'm starving and I say cool you want two staks

## The Story
If a starving person walks into a restaurant, you ask how hungry they are and sell them two steaks immediately. You don't sell them one steak, wait for them to eat it and feel full, and then ask if they want to buy a second one.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates why you must pitch your full package at the point of greatest pain, rather than after providing initial relief.
