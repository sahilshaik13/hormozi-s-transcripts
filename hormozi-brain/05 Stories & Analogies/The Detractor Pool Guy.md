# The Detractor Pool Guy

> I was Absolut attracted when my neighbor like oh yeah who's your pool service I'm like don't use my guy

## The Story
Hormozi had a pool guy who did a terrible job. Hormozi kept paying him out of convenience, but actively told his neighbors not to hire him.

## What It Illustrates
Customers might keep paying you out of inertia, but a mediocre product creates invisible negative word-of-mouth that destroys your marketing ROI.
