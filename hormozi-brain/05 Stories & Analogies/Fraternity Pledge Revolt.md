# Fraternity Pledge Revolt

> this is what hard feels like you just didn't know what hard felt like

## The Story
Every year between day 10 and 16, the fraternity pledge class would revolt because it was too hard. Hormozi sat them down and reminded them they expected it to be hard, and that this is simply what 'hard' feels like.

## What It Illustrates
When you hit the valley of despair, you must reset your reality and realize that the discomfort is the exact difficulty you signed up for.
