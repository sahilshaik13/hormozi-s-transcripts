# The Shower Door Pivot

> even though the ticket was higher they could productize that service to such a higher degree and the LTV to CAC was actually higher even though the average ticket was lower

## The Story
A glass repair business shifted from custom million-dollar home installations to exclusively selling shower doors. The doors were highly productized, fast to deliver, and had a higher LTV to CAC ratio despite a lower ticket price.

## What It Illustrates
Productized, high-margin, fast-cycle services scale much better than complex, high-ticket custom work.
