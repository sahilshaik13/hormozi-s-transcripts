# The Arithmetic vs. Algebra Teacher

> it's not that that teacher was an idiot it's that that was the sequence

## The Story
When you learn algebra, you don't call your arithmetic teacher an idiot for not teaching you algebra. You recognize that you needed the arithmetic first to be ready for the multiplication and algebra.

## What It Illustrates
Don't disrespect early mentors just because you've moved on to more advanced strategies; they prepared you for the next level.
