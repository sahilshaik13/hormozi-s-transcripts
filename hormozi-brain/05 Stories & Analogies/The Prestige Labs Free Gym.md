# The Prestige Labs Free Gym

> what if we told people that if they were on our meals they could get the gym membership for free

## The Story
Hormozi tested a model where he offered a completely free gym membership to anyone who bought his high-margin meal prep and supplements. The gym acted as a free lead magnet to drive demand for the profitable backend.

## What It Illustrates
You can disrupt an industry by giving away its core product for free if you have an innovative backend monetization model.
