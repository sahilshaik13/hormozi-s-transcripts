# Chick-fil-A vs. Boston Market

> he believed that if they were better and they just kept getting better he said our customers will demand that we get bigger

## The Story
Boston Market grew rapidly using debt and franchising, acting as mercenaries, but eventually went bankrupt. Chick-fil-A grew slowly, focusing on being better (missionaries), and ultimately dominated because quality always wins in the end.

## What It Illustrates
Better leads to bigger; bigger leads to bloat. Missionaries beat mercenaries.
