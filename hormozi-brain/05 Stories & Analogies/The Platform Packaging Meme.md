# The Platform Packaging Meme

> platforms are all a little bit different same person just packaged a little differently

## The Story
There is a meme showing the same person with different profile pictures for Tinder, LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram. Content works the same way.

## What It Illustrates
The core subject matter remains the same, but it must be packaged differently to fit the native culture of each specific platform.
