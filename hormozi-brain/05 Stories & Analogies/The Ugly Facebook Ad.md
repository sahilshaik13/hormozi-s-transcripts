# The Ugly Facebook Ad

> I put up the ugliest ad you've ever seen just like I'd learned at the workshop

## The Story
Hormozi signed a gym lease, but his partner backed out, leaving him broke and sleeping on the floor. With only one month of rent saved, he ran an ugly, text-only Facebook ad he learned at a weekend seminar. It got him 29 members and saved his business.

## What It Illustrates
Paid ads can save a dying business and work even if you have no reputation, no audience, and a tiny budget.
