# The Car Factory

> It's almost like having a car factory built inside of the car. It's very difficult to do.

## The Story
Bootstrapping is like trying to build a car factory inside a car while it's driving. Raising capital is building the factory first, accepting upfront losses, but knowing every car produced later will be profitable.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates why bootstrapping is slower and why raising capital is necessary for massive, infrastructure-heavy opportunities.
