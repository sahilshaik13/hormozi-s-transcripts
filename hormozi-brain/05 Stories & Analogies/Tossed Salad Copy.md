# Tossed Salad Copy

> he calls toss salad copy which is just you just jumble everything together and you hope you know it's throw everything in the wall and see what sticks

## The Story
When copywriters or business owners try to make an offer compelling by stacking as many different great things inside as possible, hoping one will stick. It jumbles everything together into a messy mix.

## What It Illustrates
Throwing everything at the wall dilutes the message; a single, focused idea is far more powerful than a random assortment of benefits.
