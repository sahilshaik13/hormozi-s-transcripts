# The Whale Client

> Having many small customers is safer than depending on one large “whale.”

## The Story
A massive customer is described as a 'whale' that can force a company to over-hire and over-extend to service them, creating a fragile dependency.

## What It Illustrates
Chasing massive clients can introduce fatal risk; a diverse base of smaller customers is much safer.
