# Arnold Schwarzenegger's Fundraiser

> When demand increases, cut supply.

## The Story
At a charity auction, the organizers raised ticket prices from $15k to $25k and reduced the number of available seats. They raised $5.4M from just 100 people by auctioning one-of-a-kind items.

## What It Illustrates
Decreasing supply while increasing exclusivity and status dramatically increases demand and the prices people are willing to pay.
