# Killing Zombies

> it's a lot easier to kill a zombie when it's far away than when it's on top of you

## The Story
Dealing with objections is like fighting zombies. It is much easier to shoot a zombie when it is far away than to fight it when it is already on top of you.

## What It Illustrates
Address and diffuse potential objections (obstacles) early in the discovery phase before they blow up in the close.
