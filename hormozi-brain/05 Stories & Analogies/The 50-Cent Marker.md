# The 50-Cent Marker

## The Story
If a marker costs 50 cents to make, you can let an affiliate sell it for $5 and keep 100% of the money. You then get the buyer's contact info for 50 cents and can upsell them the blue, red, and green markers.

## What It Illustrates
Giving away front-end profit to affiliates is a highly efficient way to acquire customers for back-end upsells.
