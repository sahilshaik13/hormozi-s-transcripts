# The 10-Second vs 10-Day Tower

> the fact that they're in a rush causes them to build the thing they want to build differently

## The Story
If asked to build a tower in 10 seconds, you stack things vertically and flimsily. If given 10 days, you build a wide, stable foundation. People rush to build businesses vertically, hit a ceiling, and collapse.

## What It Illustrates
You must build a solid product foundation first before scaling marketing, otherwise growth will break the business.
