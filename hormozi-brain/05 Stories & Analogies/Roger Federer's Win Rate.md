# Roger Federer's Win Rate

> Because he won the points that mattered. He scored the points that would make him a winner. He knew how to close.

## The Story
Roger Federer only won 54% of his sets but won 80% of his matches. He didn't win every point, but he won the points that mattered most.

## What It Illustrates
In sales, closing is the 'point that matters'. You can be average at other parts, but if you close, you win the game.
