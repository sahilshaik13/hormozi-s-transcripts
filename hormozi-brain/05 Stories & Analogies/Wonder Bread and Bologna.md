# Wonder Bread and Bologna

> everyone who has your food literally tells everyone that it [ __ ] sucks and never come back

## The Story
Many entrepreneurs rush to market a terrible product, comparing it to slapping a slice of bologna between two pieces of Wonder Bread and marketing it aggressively. They wonder why they fail, not realizing everyone who tries it tells others it sucks.

## What It Illustrates
Shortcutting product quality creates negative word-of-mouth that makes marketing impossibly expensive.
