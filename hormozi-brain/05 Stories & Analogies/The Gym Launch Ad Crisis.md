# The Gym Launch Ad Crisis

> The difference between the top performers and the bottom performers was one simple thing: proof.

## The Story
Hormozi received a text from the fund manager who bought Gym Launch saying ad costs had doubled. Panicking with only 4 hours until a meeting, he called a mentor who told him to compare his historical best ads to the current ones. He discovered that 80% of his winning ads were purely proof-based, while the failing ones were educational and offer-focused.

## What It Illustrates
When marketing breaks, look at historical winners to find the missing variable, which is almost always proof.
