# The Rock and the Bag of Money

> The bigger the Rock in general the bigger the bag of money

## The Story
Business problems are like a giant rock blocking your path. It takes sweat, duct tape, and leverage to move it. But on the other side of that rock is a giant bag of money.

## What It Illustrates
The bigger and more complicated the problem you solve, the bigger the financial reward and the deeper the moat against competitors.
