# The Mispriced Steakhouse

> more marketing would just mean more angry people who got dressed up, pulled up to the restaurant they wanted to go to and can't get in.

## The Story
A steakhouse owner runs ads because his restaurant is empty on Tuesdays, but on Fridays and Saturdays, he has a 90-minute wait and turns people away. The ads just make more people angry on the weekends.

## What It Illustrates
Sometimes a demand problem is actually a supply problem caused by mispriced capacity.
