# Hormozi's Broad Content Mistake

> all of those metrics were actually down despite our views being up

## The Story
Hormozi broadened his content to general business, relationships, and fitness to get more views. While vanity metrics went up, his actual business metrics like sales and opt-ins went down.

## What It Illustrates
Broad content dilutes the buyer pool; niche content drives sales.
