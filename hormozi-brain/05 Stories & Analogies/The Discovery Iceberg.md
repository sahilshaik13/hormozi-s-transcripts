# The Discovery Iceberg

> this is where all the meat is and that's where all the money is is the questions that are below the surface

## The Story
Surface-level questions (like 'how much money do you want to make') are just the tip of the iceberg. The real meat and money lie below the surface in understanding the deep 'why' and intentions driving the prospect.

## What It Illustrates
Deep discovery provides the ammunition needed to close the sale later.
