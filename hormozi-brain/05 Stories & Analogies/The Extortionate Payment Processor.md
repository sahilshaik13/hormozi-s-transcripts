# The Extortionate Payment Processor

> there was nothing I could do it was basically extortion I paid the bill

## The Story
Hormozi had millions in recurring revenue tied to one payment processor. When he tried to leave due to shady behavior, they demanded a $150,000 'transfer fee' because he had no leverage.

## What It Illustrates
Always have redundant vendors for critical business functions to avoid being blackmailed by partners who hold leverage over your operations.
