# The Porn Industry Predictor

> whatever porn adopts first eventually makes its way down.

## The Story
The adult industry is historically the bleeding edge of technological adoption. They are already using AI avatars and chatbots to replace human actors and customer service, eliminating drama and production costs.

## What It Illustrates
Whatever technology the adult industry adopts first will inevitably become standard business practice.
