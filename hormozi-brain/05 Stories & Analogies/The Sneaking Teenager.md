# The Sneaking Teenager

> you punish a kid for coming home and then the surprise the kid doesn't want to come home

## The Story
A teenager sneaks out of the house and comes back in the middle of the night. As soon as they walk in the door, the parent punishes them. The parent thinks they are punishing the sneaking out, but they are actually punishing the act of coming home.

## What It Illustrates
Delayed punishment teaches the wrong lesson; you accidentally punish the immediate behavior that preceded the consequence.
