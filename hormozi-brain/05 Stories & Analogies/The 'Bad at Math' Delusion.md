# The 'Bad at Math' Delusion

> i thought i was bad at math until 26. think about that for a second

## The Story
Hormozi used to tell people he was bad at math until he was 26 years old. Now, people consider him very good at math, proving that the trait he claimed was just a limiting belief he spoke over himself.

## What It Illustrates
We claim limiting traits as our own when they are actually just false beliefs we reinforce through speech.
