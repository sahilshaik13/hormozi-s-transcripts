# The Origin of the Pi Equation

> one day i was driving and it actually just hit me i was like what if i just divided it by this and that would just tell me the number and it turns out i was right

## The Story
Hormozi was running his gyms and struggling to track data or predict future revenue. While driving and trying to improve his math skills, he suddenly realized he could just divide his inflow by his outflow to see into the future.

## What It Illustrates
Complex business forecasting can be reduced to a simple, back-of-the-napkin division equation.
