# Persian Family Revolution

> why would you not invest in the one thing that can never be stolen can never be taken and compounds with time

## The Story
During the revolution in Iran, the government seized all of his family's land, houses, and bank accounts. His father fled to the US with nothing but his skills as a doctor, which allowed him to rebuild his wealth.

## What It Illustrates
Education and skills are the only investments that no government, divorce, or disaster can ever take away from you.
