# The Rock Concert

> if someone sells a rock concert for example you're selling access to an experience but you might also sell drinks and t-shirts while you're there

## The Story
A rock concert sells access to an experience, but also sells physical products (drinks, t-shirts) and digital products (recordings).

## What It Illustrates
The most lucrative offers often combine multiple categories of the 14 things you can sell.
