# The 5-Block Sprint

> the Smart Cookie then says huh I could take a car and I would go a hell of a lot faster

## The Story
If told to go one block, you might run. If told to go five blocks as fast as possible, you wouldn't just run harder; you would get a car and beat the runner easily.

## What It Illustrates
10x goals force you to change your vehicle and leverage rather than just working harder.
