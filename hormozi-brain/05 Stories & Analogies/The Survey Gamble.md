# The Survey Gamble

> If customers like your stuff, they want to buy more stuff like it!

## The Story
Hormozi and the founders of a volatile company disagreed on what back-end product to build. The founders wanted a passion project; Hormozi wanted a product closely related to the front-end. They surveyed the customers, and Hormozi's offer won by a landslide.

## What It Illustrates
Customers want more of what they already bought, not a completely new business model based on the founder's unrelated passions.
