# The Historic Car Appraisal

> if you're not being valued it's because of the people that are placing the value on you

## The Story
A dad tells his son to appraise a clunker car at a dealership ($5k), a scrap yard ($500), and a collector ($100k). The car never changed, only the appraiser did.

## What It Illustrates
If you are not being valued, it is because of the people placing the value on you.
