# The Advanced Lifter

> advanced lifters aren't doing different tactics than novice lifters they just lift more weight

## The Story
Advanced weightlifters don't use secret or different tactics compared to novices. They simply lift more weight, sleep, recover, and eat their macros consistently over a longer period of time.

## What It Illustrates
Advanced performance is defined by relentless consistency in the basics and the resulting outcomes, not by novel tactics.
