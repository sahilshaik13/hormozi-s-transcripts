# Weight Loss Measurement

> you can actually just have someone measure their weight and they will lose weight

## The Story
If you want someone to lose weight, the first step is just getting them to measure their weight daily. The act of measuring changes behavior.

## What It Illustrates
Measurement acts as an intervention in business performance.
