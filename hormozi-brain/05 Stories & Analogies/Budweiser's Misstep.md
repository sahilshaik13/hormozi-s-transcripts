# Budweiser's Misstep

> for Their audience for their who that was a bad Association and so it was bad branding but it was good advertising

## The Story
Budweiser ran a campaign with Dylan Mulvaney. It reached millions of people, but paired the beer with an association the core audience actively disliked.

## What It Illustrates
Advertising (making known) and branding (what is made known) are different. You can have highly effective advertising that creates terrible branding.
