# The Boot Factory

> They charged three times the price for a single pair of boots because they came with two more pairs.

## The Story
A boot store in Nashville sold boots with a 'Buy 1 Pair Get 2 Pairs Free' offer. They simply tripled the price of a single pair of boots, making the math identical to buying three pairs at normal price, but the framing drove massive foot traffic.

## What It Illustrates
Reframing a standard price as a massive 'Free' offer attracts significantly more customers than a standard discount.
