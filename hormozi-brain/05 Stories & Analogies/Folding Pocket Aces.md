# Folding Pocket Aces

> it's like getting dealt pocket aces and then choosing to fold because you're saying I can't have pocket aces on every single turn

## The Story
A poker player gets dealt pocket aces but chooses to fold them because they know they won't get dealt pocket aces on the next hand.

## What It Illustrates
Refusing to use your small business advantage (doing unscalable things) just because you can't do it forever is a foolish waste of your current leverage.
