# The Eulogy Test

> No one cares if you were the richest man in Babylon. No one cares if you built a bunch of sky skyscrapers.

## The Story
When writing a eulogy, the accomplishments a person achieved (like building skyscrapers or apps) take up only one or two sentences. The vast majority of what people remember and care about is the person's service to others and their character.

## What It Illustrates
At the end of your life, your behavioral skills and how you treated people matter far more than your worldly achievements.
