# The Physics Teacher vs. The Lamborghini

> if you want to educate... what I actually have to do is edify the person

## The Story
If a video promises to smash a Lamborghini, the creator just needs to show the car to prove they can do it. But if a video promises to teach physics, the creator must edify themselves (e.g., having a PhD) to prove they are worth listening to.

## What It Illustrates
Entertainment requires visual confirmation of the spectacle; education requires authority edification of the teacher.
