# The Bollywood Instagram Account

> there's a an Instagram page that's in Bollywood that posts a hundred times a day and gets like 9 billion views

## The Story
There is an Instagram page in Bollywood that posts 100 times a day. Because algorithms only show posts to a small fraction of an audience, posting constantly just reaches different segments of the audience, resulting in billions of views.

## What It Illustrates
The fear of 'over-posting' on social media is a myth; volume wins if the quality is maintained.
