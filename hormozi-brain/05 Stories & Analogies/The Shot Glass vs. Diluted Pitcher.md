# The Shot Glass vs. Diluted Pitcher

> You're going to get the same amount of time... they're not as good as me. it's going to be really diluted X factor versus would you rather just have a little bit less but you have the full me.

## The Story
If you have a unique 'X-factor', you can either pour it into big glasses and dilute it with water (hiring inferior coaches), or you can pour the pure liquid into smaller shot glasses (changing to a 1-to-many group model).

## What It Illustrates
It is better to give clients less total time with the true expert than lots of time with a diluted, inferior version of the expert.
