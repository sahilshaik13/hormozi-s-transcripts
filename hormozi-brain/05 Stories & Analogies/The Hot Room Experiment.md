# The Hot Room Experiment

> you are that little rat in the experiment and you want to create the Box around you that lubricates the activities that you want to do

## The Story
You can get everyone in a building to take their clothes off simply by locking the doors, putting them in one room, and cranking the heat to 200 degrees.

## What It Illustrates
Environment dictates behavior; you must design your environment to make hard work inevitable and distractions impossible.
