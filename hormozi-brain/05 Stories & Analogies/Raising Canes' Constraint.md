# Raising Canes' Constraint

> If we only did this one thing, would we accomplish all of our goals?

## The Story
Raising Canes built a massive business using only seven ingredients on their menu. They resist the urge to add an eighth item to maintain extreme focus.

## What It Illustrates
Limiting your offerings allows you to spend a disproportionate amount of time making that one thing exceptional.
