# Jerry Seinfeld's Windowless Room

> Focus occurs when you remove everything else what's left is focus

## The Story
Jerry Seinfeld writes his standup by locking himself in a room with no windows, a yellow notepad, and a pen. He doesn't force himself to write, but he isn't allowed to do anything else.

## What It Illustrates
Focus is not achieved by pushing harder to concentrate, but by ruthlessly eliminating all other alternatives until focus is the only thing left.
