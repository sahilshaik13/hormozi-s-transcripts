# The Eighth Grade Boyfriend

> you let a bad decision burn you twice once when you made your poor investment but a second time when you let that bad investment stop you from a good investment

## The Story
Having a terrible boyfriend in middle school shouldn't make you swear off all men for the rest of your life. Similarly, buying one bad coaching program shouldn't make you swear off all future investments.

## What It Illustrates
Don't let a past bad decision burn you twice by preventing you from making a good decision now.
