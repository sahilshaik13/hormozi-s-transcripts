# Skinning a Cat with a Dull Knife

> And the sharpness of your knife is your ability to execute whatever path you have

## The Story
There are many ways to skin a cat (strategy), but if your knife is dull (execution), none of the methods will work.

## What It Illustrates
Your ability to execute is the tool that makes any strategy effective.
