# The Viagra Warning

> every single guy heard that warning and was like i could have an erection for four hours like sign me up

## The Story
Viagra included a warning label stating to contact a doctor if an erection lasts longer than four hours. While a genuine medical risk, it acted as a massive selling point.

## What It Illustrates
Highlighting the extreme negative consequence of a positive result amplifies the perceived power of the product.
