# Waste Management's Roll-Up

> he just bought Waste Management routes and he kept buying them over and over and over again

## The Story
The founder of Waste Management didn't start a new trash business from scratch. Instead, he bought up highly fragmented, mom-and-pop trash routes and combined them to achieve massive economies of scale.

## What It Illustrates
Acquiring and consolidating competitors is a faster path to a monopoly and massive wealth than competing against them.
