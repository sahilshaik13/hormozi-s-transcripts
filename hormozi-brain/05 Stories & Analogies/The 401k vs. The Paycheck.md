# The 401k vs. The Paycheck

> the email is kind of like your 401k it's kind of like your investment account

## The Story
Running daily ads is like earning a paycheck—you put in work and get paid directly. Building an email list is like contributing to a 401k—you put assets in, and over time it compounds into a massive, highly profitable asset.

## What It Illustrates
Treat email as a long-term wealth-building vehicle that generates pure profit, separate from daily operational cash flow.
