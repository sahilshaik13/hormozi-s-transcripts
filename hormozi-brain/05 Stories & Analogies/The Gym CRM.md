# The Gym CRM

> I was like, '3% a month?' And he said, 'Yeah, the biggest reason is that gyms just go out of business.'

## The Story
A friend ran a CRM software company for gyms and experienced a 3% monthly churn rate. When asked how to fix it, the friend explained that the gyms were simply going out of business, making the churn impossible to prevent.

## What It Illustrates
Some customer churn is structural and cannot be fixed by improving your product; you must change the customer.
