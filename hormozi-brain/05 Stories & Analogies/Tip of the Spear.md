# Tip of the Spear

> is this the most centralized focused concept that i can point as the tip of my spear of my communication message

## The Story
Communication is compared to a spear, where the single most centralized, focused concept acts as the sharp point that penetrates the market's attention.

## What It Illustrates
A single, sharp idea cuts through noise better than multiple blunt ideas.
