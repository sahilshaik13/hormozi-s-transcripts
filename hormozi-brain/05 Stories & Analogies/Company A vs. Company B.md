# Company A vs. Company B

> you want time to be on your side not your enemy because time is going to pass no matter what

## The Story
Company A acquires 100 customers but loses them all, forcing them to double marketing just to grow. Company B acquires 100 customers and keeps them, allowing them to grow without increasing marketing spend.

## What It Illustrates
Retaining permanent customers makes time your ally and builds a far less risky, more valuable business.
