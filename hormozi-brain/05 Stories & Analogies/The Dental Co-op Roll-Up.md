# The Dental Co-op Roll-Up

> All they saw was, “Wait, you have 700 other dentists here? Well, that’s our egg pipeline for the next five years.”

## The Story
An association of 700 dentists formed a co-op to buy consumables in bulk, saving more than their membership fee. They rolled up the top 11 practices and sold for $120 million. The private equity firm also bought the association itself because they saw it as a pipeline for future acquisitions.

## What It Illustrates
Structuring a business to produce a continuous pipeline of sellable assets maximizes enterprise value.
