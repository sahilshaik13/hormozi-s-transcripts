# Maintaining Weight

> it's kind of like maintaining weight if you've ever like lost weight or gained weight like once you get to that level it's much easier to stay and maintain

## The Story
When you try to lose or gain weight, breaking homeostasis requires immense effort. But once you reach your target weight, staying there is relatively easy.

## What It Illustrates
Acquiring a new business skill requires intense, focused volume, but maintaining that skill requires minimal time.
