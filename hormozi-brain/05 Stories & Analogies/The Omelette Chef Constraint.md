# The Omelette Chef Constraint

> what's the problem he ain't got no eggs

## The Story
You can have the best chef in the world who knows how to make an omelette, wants to make it, and knows you want it now—but if he has no eggs, he can't do it.

## What It Illustrates
Sometimes employees fail not out of malice or incompetence, but because they are blocked by a lack of resources or infrastructure.
