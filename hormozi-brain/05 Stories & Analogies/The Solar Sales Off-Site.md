# The Solar Sales Off-Site

> Don't say accountability. No one knows what it means.

## The Story
A company created six abstract pillars of leadership like 'accountability' at a retreat, but no one's behavior changed. They had to redefine accountability as specific actions like texting the team every morning and doing end-of-week reports.

## What It Illustrates
Abstract values must be translated into concrete, measurable behaviors for training to be effective.
