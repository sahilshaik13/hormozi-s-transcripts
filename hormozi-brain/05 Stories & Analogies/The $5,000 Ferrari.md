# The $5,000 Ferrari

> if we were giving away ferraris right now for five grand do you think they'd find a way to get the money yes

## The Story
If someone says they can't afford a program, ask them if they would find the money if you were selling brand new Ferraris for $5,000. They always say yes.

## What It Illustrates
A price objection is never actually about a lack of funds; it is a lack of perceived value.
