# The Kidnapped Family

> if your family were kidnapped you would probably act differently right when you were trying to save them

## The Story
If your family were kidnapped, you would immediately take massive, unprecedented action to save them without needing a step-by-step tutorial.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates that a lack of action is rarely a lack of knowledge; it is a lack of identity-driven motivation. You already know what to do when the stakes align with who you are.
