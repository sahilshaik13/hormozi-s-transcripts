# Cash Flow is Oxygen

> cash flows oxygen so you can buy yourself time to have more perspective to make better judgments about the business

## The Story
A business suffocating from a lack of cash cannot be saved by long-term structural improvements. It needs an immediate infusion of air to survive long enough to fix the underlying issues.

## What It Illustrates
Prioritize immediate cash generation over long-term systemic fixes when the business's survival is threatened.
