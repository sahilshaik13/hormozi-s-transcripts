# Breaking Bad Retention Point

> they found that if someone made it to the third episode they had like an 87% likelihood of finishing the entire Five Seasons

## The Story
Netflix found that if a viewer made it to the third episode of Breaking Bad, they had an 87% likelihood of finishing all five seasons.

## What It Illustrates
Every product has an activation point that hooks the user for the long haul; your goal is to find it and drive users to it immediately.
