# Who Wants to Be a Millionaire Lifelines

> For us you've got four 911 calls or two. If it's a 911 emergency then by all means let us know and we'll figure it out.

## The Story
In the game show, contestants have a limited number of lifelines to use when they are truly stuck. You should treat client access the same way.

## What It Illustrates
Give clients a limited number of '911 emergency calls' rather than unlimited access, forcing them to prioritize true emergencies.
