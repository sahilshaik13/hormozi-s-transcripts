# The Blind ALN Launch

> if he launched it I know it's good I had never had that happen in my life

## The Story
When Hormozi launched a new software for gym owners, people who missed the webinar blindly handed over their credit cards just because his previous products had delivered so well.

## What It Illustrates
A track record of keeping product promises creates a brand reputation that makes selling effortless.
