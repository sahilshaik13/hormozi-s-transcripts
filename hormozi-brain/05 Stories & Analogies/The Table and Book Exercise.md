# The Table and Book Exercise

> And if you can't go through that exercise with your business, you don't understand your business well enough.

## The Story
To prove that LTV maximization applies to anything, Hormozi takes a physical book and a table and lists how to increase their value: raise the price, make a marble version (upsell), make a plastic version (downsell), sell chairs with it (cross-sell), or add financing.

## What It Illustrates
Every business owner must know how to apply the LTV maximization levers to their specific product or service.
