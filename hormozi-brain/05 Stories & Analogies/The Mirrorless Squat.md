# The Mirrorless Squat

> as many of those variables as you can control for you want to have in the prep so that the prep as closely as possible approximates the performance

## The Story
Hormozi trained for a powerlifting competition in a gym with a mirror in front of the squat rack. At the actual competition, there was no mirror, which completely threw off his performance because it felt different.

## What It Illustrates
Preparation must closely approximate the actual performance environment to be effective.
