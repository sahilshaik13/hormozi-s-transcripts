# The $3,000 Secret to Sales

> make people an offer so good they would feel stupid saying no

## The Story
At 23, Hormozi spent $3,000 he didn't have to attend a business conference where he felt completely out of his depth. During a break, the host, Travis Jones, saw he was overwhelmed and shared the ultimate secret to sales.

## What It Illustrates
You don't need to be good at sales if your offer is overwhelmingly valuable.
