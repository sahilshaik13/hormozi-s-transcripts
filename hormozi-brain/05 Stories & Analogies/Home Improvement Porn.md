# Home Improvement Porn

> people who are like want to improve their home, it's kind of like porn for them. Like they're just like they get super obsessive with it.

## The Story
Homeowners looking to improve their houses become highly visual and obsessive about transformations. Showing them before-and-after pictures feeds this obsession.

## What It Illustrates
Visual proof and aesthetic transformations are the strongest marketing hooks for home improvement buyers.
