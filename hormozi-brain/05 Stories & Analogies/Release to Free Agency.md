# Release to Free Agency

> get rid of the people that aren't necessarily as good or as I like to say release to free agency so they have better opportunities somewhere else

## The Story
Instead of viewing firing as a negative event, view it as releasing an employee to 'free agency' so they can find a team where they are a better fit.

## What It Illustrates
Reframes the emotional burden of letting go of underperforming staff during a consolidation.
