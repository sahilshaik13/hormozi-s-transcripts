# Rocky vs. Drago Training

> i see it as the difference between the rocky cut scene of him versus drago and rocky iv

## The Story
In Rocky IV, Drago relies on fancy scientific machines, supplements, and perfectly dialed-in routines to train. Rocky trains outdoors in the elements, just getting the work done without special equipment.

## What It Illustrates
Resilience and adaptability are superior to fragile, highly optimized routines.
