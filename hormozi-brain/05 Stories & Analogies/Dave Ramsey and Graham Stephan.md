# Dave Ramsey and Graham Stephan

> if we were going to put a portfolio together of your knowledge base it would be 80 real estate 20 stocks maybe

## The Story
Graham Stephan asked Dave Ramsey if having 80-85% of his net worth in real estate meant he was over-indexed. Ramsey asked Graham what percentage of his investing knowledge was in real estate. When Graham replied 80-85%, Ramsey told him his portfolio was perfectly distributed because it reflected his knowledge base.

## What It Illustrates
Your investment portfolio should be a direct reflection of your knowledge base, not an arbitrary diversification metric.
