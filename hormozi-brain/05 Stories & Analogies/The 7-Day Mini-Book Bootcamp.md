# The 7-Day Mini-Book Bootcamp

> what it was was to break the belief and to prove the concept that they could really do it

## The Story
A book publishing company struggled with the long time delay of writing a book. They created a 7-day bootcamp where clients publish a 'mini-book' quickly to prove the concept and break their limiting beliefs before writing the real book.

## What It Illustrates
You don't have to deliver the ultimate result immediately, but you must deliver a tangible outcome quickly to provide immediate Time to Value.
