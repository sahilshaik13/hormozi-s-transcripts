# Carl Icahn's 11-Story Building

> he fired everyone and he said the weirdest thing happened nothing

## The Story
Carl Icahn bought a company and found an 11-story building full of employees whose purpose he couldn't figure out. After a foreman told him they did nothing, he fired all 11 floors. Nothing happened to the business.

## What It Illustrates
Adding bodies doesn't solve problems; it often just creates bloat and layers of incompetence.
