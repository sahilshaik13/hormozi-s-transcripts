# The Eye Surgeon Mom

> my mom is a doctor she's an eye surgeon so I know a lot of stuff about eyes that would be a category of stuff that I actually know a lot more about than you might expect

## The Story
Hormozi explains that because his mother is an eye surgeon, he passively learned a lot about eyes. Conversely, someone with a mechanic father would know about carburetors.

## What It Illustrates
You absorb specialized knowledge from the people who raised you, giving you an unfair advantage in certain niches.
