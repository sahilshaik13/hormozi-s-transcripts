# Warren Buffett's Boat

> it is far more important what boat you are in rather than how hard you row

## The Story
Warren Buffett had a brilliant, hardworking friend from Columbia who went into the steel industry while Buffett went into finance. The friend achieved only modest success because the steel industry was a bad market.

## What It Illustrates
The market you choose dictates your success far more than your individual effort or intelligence.
