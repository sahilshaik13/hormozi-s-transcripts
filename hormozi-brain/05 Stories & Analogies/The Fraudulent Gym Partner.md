# The Fraudulent Gym Partner

> I wake up one morning cuz I would always check the bank account every morning... all of a sudden it was at zero

## The Story
Hormozi partnered with a guy who previously had a 'bad credit situation' (which was actually fraud). After Hormozi funded and launched the new gym, the partner drained the bank account on day one, sent the money to Sweden, and filed for bankruptcy.

## What It Illustrates
Never ignore red flags or give bad actors the benefit of the doubt.
