# Mining for Gold

> no matter what it always ends you only lose when you quit before you see it all the way through

## The Story
You are underground swinging a pickaxe at rock, trying to reach a pile of gold. You don't know how far away it is, but the difficulty of swinging the axe will eventually end.

## What It Illustrates
Hardship doesn't last forever. It ends in one of three ways: you quit, the environment gets easier (soft dirt), or you get stronger at swinging the axe.
