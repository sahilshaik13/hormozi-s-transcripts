# The $42,000 Mastermind

> if you were able to pay a dollar and get $5 back would you do it

## The Story
Hormozi's father was shocked that people paid $42,000 a year for a mastermind. Hormozi explained it as a machine where you put in $1 and get $5 back after a year of hard work.

## What It Illustrates
Value is based on the ROI and outcome provided to the customer, not the cost of fulfillment.
