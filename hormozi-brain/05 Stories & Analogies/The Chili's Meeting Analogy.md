# The Chili's Meeting Analogy

> every day all the time you're taking the whole team out to chilies 10 times a day this is why people stay poor

## The Story
If you took 10 employees out to Chili's and spent $400 on drinks and chips, it would be scrutinized as a major expense. Yet, putting those same 10 employees on a one-hour call costs the company the exact same amount in hourly wages.

## What It Illustrates
Meetings have a hard financial cost that is often ignored because it's paid in time rather than an invoice.
