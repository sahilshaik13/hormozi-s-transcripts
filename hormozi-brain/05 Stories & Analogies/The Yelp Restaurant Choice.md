# The Yelp Restaurant Choice

> The proof demonstrates the implied authority that obviously these people have been around.

## The Story
If you are choosing between a 5-star restaurant with 3 reviews and a 4.6-star restaurant with 4,000 reviews, you will always choose the 4.6-star restaurant.

## What It Illustrates
Massive social proof and implied authority will always beat a perfect, flawless promise.
