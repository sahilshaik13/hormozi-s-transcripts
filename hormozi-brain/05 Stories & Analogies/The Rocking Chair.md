# The Rocking Chair

> you're not gonna like go home sit in the rocking chair you know smoke a cigarette... you get in your car and then you're gonna realize you got to pick up timmy from soccer practice

## The Story
When a prospect says 'I need to think about it', they aren't going to sit in a rocking chair staring at the clouds pondering the offer. They are going to go back to their busy lives, get distracted, and fail to change.

## What It Illustrates
Time does not help people make decisions; information does.
