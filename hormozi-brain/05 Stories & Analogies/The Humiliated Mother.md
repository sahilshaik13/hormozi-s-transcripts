# The Humiliated Mother

> If there's a funnel of experience and it's painful... My mom always uses the word for anything uncomfortable for humiliate.

## The Story
Tony's mother left 10 days of voicemails claiming she was 'humiliated' simply because she couldn't reach him while he was in Europe.

## What It Illustrates
People have a default vocabulary that forces all negative experiences through a specific, often exaggerated, emotional funnel.
