# The Dentist Rollup

> now you have a 300 million year exit rather than a $25 million year he was like that sounds better

## The Story
A founder wanted to sell his association of 500 dentists for $20-30M. Hormozi suggested bundling 10 dentists a year, selling them for $100M, taking 20%, and eventually selling the rollup machine itself for $300M.

## What It Illustrates
Thinking outside the standard options (unknown unknowns) and applying leverage can exponentially increase an exit valuation.
