# Riverstones vs. Pointy Objects

> I don't want to be Riverstone I don't want to be well-rounded I want to be really really pointy at at this thing

## The Story
Corporate tracks try to smooth people out into well-rounded 'riverstones.' Shopify encourages people to be 'pointy objects' (highly specialized in one craft) and pairs them with other pointy objects to create an 'Avengers' team.

## What It Illustrates
Specialization and complementary skills beat well-roundedness in building massive companies.
