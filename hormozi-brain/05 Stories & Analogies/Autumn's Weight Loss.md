# Autumn's Weight Loss

> which one matters more to you being right or being thin

## The Story
A gym client named Autumn believed she defied the laws of physics and couldn't lose weight. Hormozi confronted her, asking if she wanted to be 'right' or be 'thin'. She chose thin, followed the program, lost the weight, and became a top referring client.

## What It Illustrates
Level 5 (Negative Active) individuals have the highest potential to become Level 1 (Positive Active) champions if you can get them to redirect their energy from proving the system wrong to proving it right.
