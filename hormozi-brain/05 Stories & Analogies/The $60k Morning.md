# The $60k Morning

> I actually said a number that I wanted the person to say no to and then they said yes. That was how that actually that belief was broken for me.

## The Story
Hormozi was burnt out on doing done-for-you gym launches. To blow off a prospect, he quoted an absurd $6,000 for a service he usually sold for $500. The prospect immediately said yes. Shocked, Hormozi quoted $8,000 on the next call, and $10,000 on the next. He collected $60,000 in a single morning.

## What It Illustrates
Your pricing is often limited entirely by your own beliefs, not the market's willingness to pay. You must pitch numbers that scare you to discover what people actually have.
