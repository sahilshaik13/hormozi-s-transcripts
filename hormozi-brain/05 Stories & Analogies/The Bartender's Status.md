# The Bartender's Status

> if you control the good stuff that people want, then you will have status no matter what it is

## The Story
A bartender in a busy bar has high status because people wave money at him to get a drink. Outside the bar, he has no status.

## What It Illustrates
Status is contextual and derived entirely from controlling scarce resources in a specific environment.
