# The Baby Bird

> think about you as a baby bird just kind of feeding your prospects the value

## The Story
When creating a lead magnet, you should do all the heavy lifting and preparation for the prospect. You just drop the finished, easy-to-consume value directly to them.

## What It Illustrates
Lead magnets must be risk-free, easy, and fast for the user, requiring effort from the business, not the prospect.
