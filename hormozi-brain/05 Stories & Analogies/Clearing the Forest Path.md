# Clearing the Forest Path

> they move through the path that we tread three times faster than we did because we already cleared all the trees

## The Story
One generation hacks away at trees to clear a path. The next generation runs through that cleared path three times faster, reaching the end of the clearing where they must start hacking away at the new frontier.

## What It Illustrates
Mentors accelerate the progress of mentees, allowing them to reach new heights faster and forge the next path.
