# The John Henry Fallacy

> compare it to something that's been optimized over the last 6 years... and say, 'See, it doesn't work as well. John Henry wins again.'

## The Story
Business owners install a half-built AI function, compare it to a human process refined over decades, and celebrate when the human wins, much like the folk hero John Henry beating the steam drill.

## What It Illustrates
It is a flawed comparison; AI needs proper SOPs and training time just like humans to be effective.
