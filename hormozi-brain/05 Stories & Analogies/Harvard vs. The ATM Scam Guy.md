# Harvard vs. The ATM Scam Guy

> on one side you have Harvard most people don't call Harvard a scam... on this side you've got a guy who tells you how to start an ATM business and promises that you're going to make a hundred thousand dollars a day

## The Story
Harvard charges $250k a year, promises nothing but an education, rejects most applicants, and is universally respected. Meanwhile, a scammer promises $100k a day, accepts anyone with a credit card, and is widely considered a fraud.

## What It Illustrates
Legitimacy in education comes from exclusivity, data-backed expectations, and rigorous standards, not sensational promises.
