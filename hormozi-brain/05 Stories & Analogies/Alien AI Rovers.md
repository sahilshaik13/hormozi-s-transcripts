# Alien AI Rovers

> if aliens are further ahead of us then it means that they probably have ai if they're going to send something they would probably send a robot

## The Story
When humans explore space, we send robots like the Mars Rover because they survive harsh climates, don't need food, and can adapt. Therefore, if advanced aliens visit Earth, they likely wouldn't send biological bodies; they would send AI.

## What It Illustrates
AI is the ultimate adaptable, durable form of intelligence for extreme environments and future exploration.
