# The Yiddish Proverb

> your nose is an inch above your mouth but need somebody else to tell you your breath breath smells bad.

## The Story
Your nose is an inch above your mouth, but you still need someone else to tell you if your breath smells bad.

## What It Illustrates
We are often blind to our own obvious execution failures and mistakenly think we need a completely new strategy.
