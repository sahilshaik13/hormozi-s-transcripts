# Starbucks' $14,000 Coffee

> It turns out that when you boil hot water and you sell for six bucks you make a lot of money

## The Story
Starbucks has a customer LTV of $14,099, built $5 at a time. Because local food CAC is incredibly low, their LTV to CAC ratio is astronomical, allowing them to scale to 38,000 corporate-owned stores.

## What It Illustrates
High purchase frequency and low delivery costs create massive LTV to CAC ratios, funding massive scale.
