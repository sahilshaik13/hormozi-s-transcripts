# The $200 Jordans

> if you wouldn't buy those shoes for $6,000 then maybe don't buy them today for $200

## The Story
If a 25-year-old invests the $200 they would have spent on Jordans into the S&P 500 at 9% for 40 years, it becomes $6,000. Buying the shoes means permanently destroying $6,000 of future wealth.

## What It Illustrates
Every consumer purchase has a massive, hidden opportunity cost due to the loss of compound interest.
