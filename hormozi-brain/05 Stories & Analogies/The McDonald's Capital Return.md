# The McDonald's Capital Return

> if you look at a McDonald's for example it costs 1.1 1.2 million dollars to open a McDonald's they make 150 000 a year

## The Story
McDonald's costs over a million dollars to open but only yields about $150k a year, which is a 15% return. Because the return on capital is slow and low, it makes sense to franchise it.

## What It Illustrates
Businesses with low returns on capital should be franchised to scale using outside capital.
