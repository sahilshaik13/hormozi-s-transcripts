# New Balance's 18-Month Desert

> for 18 months it basically didn't pay off at all and then at month 19 it just and then it just started taking off like a rocket

## The Story
The CMO of New Balance flipped their ad budget from 70% direct-ask to 70% brand storytelling. For 18 months, the strategy showed no payoff, but at month 19, sales skyrocketed.

## What It Illustrates
Brand building and value creation require immense patience, but the delayed compounding effect is massive.
