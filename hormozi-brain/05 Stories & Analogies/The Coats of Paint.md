# The Coats of Paint

> I think about this like coats of paint... it's like sanding down the rough edges and you just keep getting at it

## The Story
When writing a book or editing a video, you don't do it all at once. You do one pass for punctuation, one for data, one for flow. It's like applying different grades of sandpaper to smooth out rough edges.

## What It Illustrates
Quality is engineered through repeated exposure and specific, focused iterations, not a single perfect draft.
