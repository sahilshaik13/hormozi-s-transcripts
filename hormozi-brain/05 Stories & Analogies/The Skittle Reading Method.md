# The Skittle Reading Method

> he came to train the child on how to read with a bag of Skittles

## The Story
Dr. Kashi taught an autistic kid to read by giving him a Skittle for every word read, and two Skittles for breaking a record. He never punished mistakes, only rewarded progress. Eventually, the kid liked the story enough to read without the Skittles.

## What It Illustrates
Reward changes behavior longer and more effectively than punishment; eventually, the mastery of the skill becomes its own reward.
