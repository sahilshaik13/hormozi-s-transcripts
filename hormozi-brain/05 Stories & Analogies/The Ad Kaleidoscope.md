# The Ad Kaleidoscope

> Then we’ll run them through a kaleidoscope to make 50 more hooks and with it, generate 3-5 variations on the meat.

## The Story
Instead of making 5 distinct ads, you take the best ads, chop them into hook, meat, and CTA, and run them through a 'kaleidoscope' to generate 50 hooks and 3-5 meats.

## What It Illustrates
Modularizing ad components allows for exponential multiplication of creative assets.
