# Warren Buffett and Paul Graham

> Warren passed him in his career you'll notice that Warren isn't like man I'm so much better than Paul Graham

## The Story
Warren Buffett offered to work for his mentor for free, but the mentor said he was still overpriced because the mentor would be giving far more value. Buffett eventually surpassed him, but never spoke down about him.

## What It Illustrates
True giants remain grateful to the mentors whose shoulders they stand on, even after surpassing them.
