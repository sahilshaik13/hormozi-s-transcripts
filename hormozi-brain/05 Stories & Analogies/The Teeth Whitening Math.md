# The Teeth Whitening Math

> spend 50 make 250 you're talking to 5x right very different

## The Story
A teeth whitening business costs $50k to open and nets $250k a year. Because the return is 5x, it makes more sense to own the locations privately rather than franchise.

## What It Illustrates
Businesses with extremely high returns on capital should be kept privately owned to maximize wealth.
