# The Stage Association

> branding is this big fancy word but all it means is the association between two things

## The Story
If Hormozi stood on stage with Republican candidates, the audience would view him one way. If he stood with Democrats, another way. If he stood with porn stars, a completely different way.

## What It Illustrates
Branding is simply the automatic psychological pairing of you with the things or people you stand next to.
