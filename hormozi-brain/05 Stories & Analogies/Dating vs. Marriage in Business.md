# Dating vs. Marriage in Business

> I think most of you guys are dating your business but you need to get married to your business

## The Story
When you are single, 30-40% of your mental energy goes toward chasing potential partners and 'what ifs'. Getting married reallocates all that wasted energy back into your main focus. The same applies to committing to one business versus flirting with side hustles.

## What It Illustrates
Commitment eliminates the mental overhead of exploring alternatives, allowing you to channel all your energy into scaling your primary asset.
