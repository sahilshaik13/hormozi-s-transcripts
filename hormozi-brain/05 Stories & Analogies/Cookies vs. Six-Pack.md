# Cookies vs. Six-Pack

> if I like cookies and I'm good with that and I also want a six-pack, I just prefer cookies to a six-pack. It's just that's the trade.

## The Story
If you like eating cookies but also want a six-pack, you can't have both. You simply prefer the cookies over the six-pack.

## What It Illustrates
Dissatisfaction comes from wanting two mutually exclusive things instead of accepting the trade-off of your choices.
