# Zuckerberg's Focus

> Zuckerberg didn't have airbnbs he was flipping on the side while he was building Facebook

## The Story
Mark Zuckerberg did not become a billionaire by flipping Airbnb properties on the side while building his main company. He focused entirely on building Facebook.

## What It Illustrates
Massive wealth requires singular focus on your primary compounding asset, not distraction by small passive income side hustles.
