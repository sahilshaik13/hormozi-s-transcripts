# The Three Partners Negotiation

> I couldn't help but notice all three of us have had the same problem delivered to us. And all three of us have radically different emotional responses.

## The Story
Tony and two partners faced a bad negotiation outcome. One partner was 'enraged,' Tony was 'pissed off,' and the third was merely 'annoyed' or 'peeved.'

## What It Illustrates
Different words attached to the exact same event produce radically different emotional realities and responses.
