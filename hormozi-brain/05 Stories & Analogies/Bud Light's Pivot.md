# Bud Light's Pivot

> It was good advertising but bad branding.

## The Story
Bud Light partnered with Dylan Mulvaney, which generated massive awareness but alienated their core audience, resulting in lost sales. To fix it, they partnered with Shane Gillis and the UFC to re-pair the product with associations their ideal audience actually liked.

## What It Illustrates
Good advertising (awareness) can still be bad branding (negative association). Branding must be tailored to the ideal customer's preferences.
