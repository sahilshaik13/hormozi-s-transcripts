# The Gym Launch Epiphany

> we just need leads like just more leads that's it like if you just give us more leads we'll be happy

## The Story
Hormozi asked gym owners how to improve the product, expecting to add complex value benefits. The owners cut him off and said they just wanted more leads.

## What It Illustrates
If you solve the one core problem your customer cares about, they don't care about any other added features.
