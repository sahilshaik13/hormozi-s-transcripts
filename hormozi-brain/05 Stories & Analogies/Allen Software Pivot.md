# Allen Software Pivot

> once I realized that gym owners were not the customer and agencies were the customer I was able to start asking agencies for the correct feedback

## The Story
Hormozi built software for gym owners to work leads, but realized gym owners didn't have the skill to use it. He pivoted to selling the software to marketing agencies who served small businesses, because agencies had reliable revenue and the skill to use the tool.

## What It Illustrates
If your target market lacks the skill to utilize your solution, pivot to the layer above them (selling shovels instead of panning for gold).
