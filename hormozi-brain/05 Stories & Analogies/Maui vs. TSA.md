# Maui vs. TSA

> we're selling maui we're selling the final destination

## The Story
When selling a trip to Hawaii, you don't sell the security lines, the turbulence, or the cramped plane seats. You sell the beach, the drinks, and the destination.

## What It Illustrates
Sales pitches should focus entirely on the desired outcome, not the painful or tedious steps required to achieve it.
