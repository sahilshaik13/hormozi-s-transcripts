# The Bloated Team Purge

> if you want to stay on this team then it means that you're agreeing to that and I don't want to say that you're inherently agreeing to that I want to let you guys sleep on it

## The Story
Hormozi's sales team got fat and bloated, so he fired more than half of them. He then held a meeting with the survivors, set a new standard for a 'world-class' organization, and told them to sleep on it—offering them the chance to quit the next morning with no hard feelings if they didn't want to meet the new standard.

## What It Illustrates
To build a culture of excellence, a leader must be willing to drastically cut dead weight and explicitly demand high standards from those who remain.
