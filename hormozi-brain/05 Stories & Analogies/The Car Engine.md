# The Car Engine

> If you don't have an engine the car doesn't move you don't have wheels the car doesn't move you don't have a chassis there is no car.

## The Story
A business is like a car. You need all the core components (engine, wheels, chassis) just to make it move from zero to one. But to go from one to ten, you have to upgrade specific components, like swapping an 8-cylinder engine for a 16-cylinder engine.

## What It Illustrates
You must build all foundational departments of a business first, and then systematically upgrade the specific skills or leaders in the bottleneck departments to scale.
