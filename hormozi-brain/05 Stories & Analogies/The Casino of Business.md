# The Casino of Business

> You basically just have to put your shifts on the table and hope.

## The Story
Business is like placing chips on a casino table where the odds aren't predetermined. You incur a fixed cost of pain to make a bet, but you don't know if it will pay out 30x or double.

## What It Illustrates
Illustrates the inherent uncertainty of entrepreneurship and why it goes against our biological survival instincts.
