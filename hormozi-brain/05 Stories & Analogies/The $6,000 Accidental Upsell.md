# The $6,000 Accidental Upsell

> I actually said a number that I wanted the person to say no to and then they said yes. That was how that actually that belief was broken for me.

## The Story
Hormozi was exhausted and tried to blow off a gym owner by quoting $6,000 for a service he usually sold for $500. To his shock, the owner immediately agreed. He repeated this all day, raising the price to $8,000 and then $10,000, collecting $60,000 in a single day.

## What It Illustrates
Your own limiting beliefs about money are often the only thing keeping your prices low; the market has far more money than you realize.
