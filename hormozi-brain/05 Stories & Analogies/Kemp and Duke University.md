# Kemp and Duke University

> It was this really humbling lesson for me that none of the colleges cared that I worked less than him.

## The Story
In high school, Hormozi mocked a less naturally gifted student named Kemp for studying constantly. Ultimately, Kemp got into Duke University while Hormozi did not, teaching Hormozi that sheer volume of work beats natural talent.

## What It Illustrates
Institutions and markets do not care how easily you do something; they only care about the final output and who did the work.
