# The Kale Ad

> I will use and abuse it until it doesn't work at all and so when you have your winners you usually just need to run them way longer way harder in a lot of different variations

## The Story
Kale, a GM, recorded a perfect, unscripted testimonial ad in his gym with his wife, detailing their journey from near-bankruptcy to a full gym. Instead of constantly making new ads, the team created 100 variations of this single video—changing filters to black and white or sepia, swapping hooks, altering headlines, and changing fonts.

## What It Illustrates
When you find a winning creative, do not move on to a new idea. Reskin it, iterate on it, and abuse it until it completely stops working.
