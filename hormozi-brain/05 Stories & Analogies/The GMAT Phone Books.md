# The GMAT Phone Books

> the number of problems that you solved was in direct correlation with the score you got on the test

## The Story
To study for the GMAT, Hormozi read a study linking the number of problems solved to the final score. He did a stack of phone books worth of problems, 4 hours a day for 16 weeks, and scored above a Harvard average.

## What It Illustrates
Success is often a simple inputs-to-outputs equation. If you do the sheer volume of work, the result is guaranteed.
