# The T-Shirt Arbitrage

> if you believe what we believe vote with your dollars about the things that you care about align with us and that's fundamentally what branding is

## The Story
A direct response marketer will aggressively sell a meme t-shirt using discounts, bundles, and continuity programs to squeeze out margins. A brand builder will spend a year communicating their values, then simply put their logo on a shirt and sell it effortlessly for $100.

## What It Illustrates
Branding allows you to command premium pricing through alignment of values, bypassing the need for complex, low-margin marketing arbitrage.
