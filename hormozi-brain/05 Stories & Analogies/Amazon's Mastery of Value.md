# Amazon's Mastery of Value

> over time they've really nailed down hitting each and every single one of these variables that drive value

## The Story
Amazon spent 30 years perfecting the Value Equation for e-commerce. They use reviews for likelihood of achievement, Prime for time delay, one-click buy for effort, stock levels for scarcity, lightning deals for urgency, and 'try before you buy' for guarantees.

## What It Illustrates
Amazon is the ultimate theoretical ideal for e-commerce value creation; smaller brands should use them as a lens to take steps toward improving their own offers.
