# The $1M Dietitian

> $1 million in profit from less than 6,000 Instagram followers

## The Story
A registered dietitian makes nearly $1M in profit with only 5,800 Instagram followers and 19 likes per post. She does this by exclusively posting about how to bill insurance correctly for other dietitians.

## What It Illustrates
Hyper-niche content monetizes incredibly well because it attracts buyers, not just viewers.
