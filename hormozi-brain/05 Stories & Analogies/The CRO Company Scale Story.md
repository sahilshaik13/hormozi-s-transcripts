# The CRO Company Scale Story

> when I go to an e-commerce store that does a100 million and I add 10% it's the same amount of work

## The Story
A CRO agency compared adding a 10% improvement to a $1 million e-commerce store versus a $100 million store. The work was identical, but the value created jumped from $100,000 to $10 million, allowing them to charge much more.

## What It Illustrates
The size of the client dictates the absolute value of your work.
