# The Goose and the Golden Egg

> But when the deed was done, not a single golden egg did he find, and his precious golden goose was dead.

## The Story
A countryman had a goose that laid one golden egg a day. Impatient to get rich faster, he killed the goose to get all the eggs at once, but found nothing inside and lost his wealth engine entirely.

## What It Illustrates
Never sell the engine that creates value (the goose); only sell the outputs it produces (the eggs).
