# The Coca-Cola Reoccurring Revenue Model

> Coca-Cola has no recurring basically no recurring members member like memberships but we buy Coca-Cola products all the time so we have reoccurring

## The Story
Coca-Cola does not have recurring memberships or subscriptions. However, consumers reliably buy their products over and over again.

## What It Illustrates
You do not need a SaaS subscription to have a highly valuable, predictable revenue stream; you just need data proving reoccurring purchases.
