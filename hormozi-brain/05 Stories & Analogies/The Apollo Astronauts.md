# The Apollo Astronauts

> Now, what the [ __ ] do you do after you've walked on the moon for adventure?

## The Story
Astronauts beat out thousands to go to the moon using basic technology. After achieving the ultimate human adventure, they returned to Earth and many fell into depression and addiction.

## What It Illustrates
Achieving the ultimate goal without having a next vision for growth leads to misery.
