# The $10 Pen Illusion

> I'm actually spending $50 to make $10 in gross profit lifetime not a good equation

## The Story
A business sells a pen for $10 that costs $9 to make. A customer buys 10 pens, generating $100 in revenue but costing $90 to fulfill. If the team spends $50 to acquire that customer, they think they are doubling their money ($50 to make $100).

## What It Illustrates
Confusing revenue with gross profit hides the fact that you are actually spending $50 to make only $10 in profit, destroying the business.
