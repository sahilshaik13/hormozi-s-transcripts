# The New Balance CMO

> for the first 18 months they made less money... but on month 19 it went like this Boop it started going up

## The Story
The CMO of New Balance flipped their ad spend from 70% bottom-funnel to 70% top-funnel (brand awareness). Revenue dropped for 18 months, but on month 19, it skyrocketed and created a massive rebirth for the company.

## What It Illustrates
Brand building requires a long time horizon and delayed gratification, but yields massive compounding returns.
