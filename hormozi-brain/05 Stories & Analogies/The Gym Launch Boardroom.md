# The Gym Launch Boardroom

> every single person on his team was better than every single person on my team.

## The Story
When selling Gym Launch, Hormozi sat across from a private equity firm. He noticed that every single person on the PE firm's side of the table was vastly superior in talent to his own team.

## What It Illustrates
To attract world-class talent, you need a massive vision that can accommodate their high-level aspirations and compensation requirements.
