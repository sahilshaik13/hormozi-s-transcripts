# The Rocky Cutscene

> The rocky cut scene in the movie lasts a couple seconds and the rocky cut scene in real life might last a few years.

## The Story
In the movie Rocky, the training montage lasts only a few seconds on screen, showing him struggling and then succeeding. In real life, that 'cutscene' of struggling and sucking lasts for years.

## What It Illustrates
Do not quit during the long, boring, and painful period of skill acquisition; it is a necessary phase that everyone must go through.
