# Hormozi's Worldview Pillars

_The root beliefs that underpin everything he teaches._

## Identity is a Lagging Indicator (Doing is Being)
> Identity is indistinguishable from action; you do not need to change who you are to change your actions, you must change your actions and your identity will automatically follow.

_Appears in 16 videos_

**Related concepts:** [[[[Doing is Being]]]], [[[[Fluid Identity]]]], [[[[Identity Consistency]]]], [[[[Evidence-Based Character]]]]

## The Work Equation (Volume x Leverage)
> Hard work is objectively measured by output (Volume x Leverage), not by time or physical effort; outsized success requires an unreasonable volume of inputs.

_Appears in 14 videos_

**Related concepts:** [[[[The Work Equation]]]], [[[[The Leverage Ladder]]]], [[[[100x Not 2x]]]], [[[[Optimizers vs. Maximizers]]]]

## Ignorance Debt & The Ultimate Asset
> The financial cost of not knowing how to do something is vastly more expensive than the cost of acquiring the skill; you are the ultimate compounding asset.

_Appears in 9 videos_

**Related concepts:** [[[[Ignorance Debt]]]], [[[[S&Me vs S&P]]]], [[[[Skill Stacking]]]], [[[[Time vs. Money for Skill Acquisition]]]]

## Volume Negates Luck
> Success is an indifferent mechanism that responds to high-volume inputs; doing the work for an unreasonably long time guarantees success over variance.

_Appears in 9 videos_

**Related concepts:** [[[[Volume Negates Luck]]]], [[[[Quantity Precedes Quality]]]], [[[[Actions Dictate Outcomes]]]], [[[[The Rule of 100]]]]

## Math Over Emotion (LTV to CAC)
> Business scaling is a math problem driven by the LTV to CAC ratio, not an opinion or a passion project; optimize this ratio to ethically monopolize your market.

_Appears in 8 videos_

**Related concepts:** [[[[LTV to CAC Ratio]]]], [[[[8 Levers of LTV]]]], [[[[The Attention Auction]]]], [[[[Scientific Goal Setting]]]]

## The Value Equation & Premium Pricing
> De-commoditize your offer by maximizing certainty and speed while minimizing effort, allowing you to charge premium prices and escape the race to the bottom.

_Appears in 8 videos_

**Related concepts:** [[[[The Value Equation]]]], [[[[Price to Value Discrepancy]]]], [[[[Values-Based Decision]]]], [[[[Niche Specificity Dictates Pricing]]]]

## Extreme Ownership & Action
> The marketplace is indifferent to excuses; success is dictated entirely by your active execution, output volume, and willingness to sacrifice ego for outcomes.

_Appears in 7 videos_

**Related concepts:** [[[[Extreme Ownership of Learning]]]], [[[[Outcome over Ego]]]], [[[[The Marketplace is Ruthlessly Indifferent]]]], [[[[Inversion Thinking]]]]

## Radical Focus & Elimination
> Commitment is the active destruction of alternatives; you create more value by permanently eliminating low-value tasks and distractions rather than trying to optimize them.

_Appears in 6 videos_

**Related concepts:** [[[[Commitment as Elimination]]]], [[[[Productivity via Elimination]]]], [[[[The One Thing Forcing Function]]]], [[[[Opportunity Cost of Distraction]]]]
