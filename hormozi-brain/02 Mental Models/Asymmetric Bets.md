# Asymmetric Bets


> 🧠 **Core Belief** — foundational to Hormozi's worldview
> The downside is nothing and the upside is everything.

## Description
Taking risks where the downside is virtually zero (or just a bruised ego) and the upside is life-changing.

## How to Apply
When you are young or broke, take as many shots as possible because you have nothing to lose and everything to gain.
