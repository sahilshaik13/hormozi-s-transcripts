# Sell the Vacation


> 🧠 **Core Belief** — foundational to Hormozi's worldview
> sell the vacation not the plane flight

## Description
Focusing on the final desired outcome rather than the painful, complex process required to get there.

## How to Apply
When pitching a product, describe the end result (Maui) instead of the features, modules, or hard work (the plane flight, TSA, turbulence).
