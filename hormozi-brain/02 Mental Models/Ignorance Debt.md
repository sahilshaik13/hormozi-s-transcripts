# Ignorance Debt


> 🧠 **Core Belief** — foundational to Hormozi's worldview
> right now you pay reality $950,000 every single year for not knowing how to make a million dollars

## Description
The massive financial cost of making mistakes or missing out on revenue because you lack specific knowledge or experience. The gap between your current reality and your ultimate goals is a literal tax you pay every year.

## How to Apply
Deploy money to hire experts, buy courses, or acquire skills to shortcut the time it takes to learn, effectively paying down the debt faster.
