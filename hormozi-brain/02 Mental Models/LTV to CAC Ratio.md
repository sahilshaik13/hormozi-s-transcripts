# LTV to CAC Ratio


> 🧠 **Core Belief** — foundational to Hormozi's worldview
> if you in the purchase of the eyeball or of the click you can outspend 100% of your competition then you can ethically take all of their money

## Description
The fundamental economic engine that dictates a business's ability to scale. Calculated as Lifetime Gross Profit divided by Cost to Acquire a Customer.

## How to Apply
Optimize for the highest possible return between these two numbers to afford to outspend competitors and buy up all advertising space.
