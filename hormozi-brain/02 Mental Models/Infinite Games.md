# Infinite Games


> 🧠 **Core Belief** — foundational to Hormozi's worldview
> the point isn't to win at business the point is to stay in business and literally keep playing

## Description
Games where the objective is not to win, but to keep playing.

## How to Apply
Reframe business, health, and marriage as infinite games so you don't feel empty after hitting arbitrary finite goals.
