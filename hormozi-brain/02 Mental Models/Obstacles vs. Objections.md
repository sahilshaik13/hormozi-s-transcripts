# Obstacles vs. Objections

## Description
The timing of prospect resistance relative to the price reveal. Addressing potential deal-breakers before mentioning the price kills zombies upfront.

## How to Apply
Address resistance before asking for the sale (obstacles) because it is much easier to break beliefs before the stakes are raised. Once price is revealed, resistance becomes an objection.
