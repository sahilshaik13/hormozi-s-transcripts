# ICE Framework

## What It Is
A prioritization matrix evaluating tasks based on Impact, Confidence, and Ease.

## Steps
1. Impact: How big of a difference will this make?
2. Confidence: How likely is it to succeed?
3. Ease: How hard is it to execute?
