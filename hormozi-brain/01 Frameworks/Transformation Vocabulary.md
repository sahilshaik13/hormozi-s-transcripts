# Transformation Vocabulary

## What It Is
Downgrade negative emotional intensity and upgrade positive emotional intensity by changing the labels applied to experiences.

## Steps
1. Notice the negative word you habitually use to describe an experience
2. Identify the emotional state that word triggers
3. Replace it with a less intense or more empowering word (e.g., 'annoyed' instead of 'enraged')
