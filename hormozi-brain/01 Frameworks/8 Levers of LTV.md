# 8 Levers of LTV

## What It Is
A systematic checklist to maximize the lifetime gross profit of a customer without needing more leads.

## Steps
1. Raise the price
2. Decrease the cost of delivery
3. Get people to buy more times
4. Cross-sell additional things
5. Upsell a better version
6. Sell a higher quantity
7. Downsell a smaller/worse version to save a lost sale
8. Sell fewer units
