# BANT Qualification

## What It Is
A filtering mechanism to score leads and prioritize high-intent prospects for sales calls.

## Steps
1. Budget: Do they have the money?
2. Authority: Can they make the decision?
3. Need: How bad is their problem?
4. Timing: When do they want to start?
