# Moonshot Goal Setting

## What It Is
Reignite passion and pull motivation when standard business goals feel hollow or routine by setting unreasonable goals tied to deep emotional causes.

## Steps
1. Identify a cause you are deeply, emotionally connected to
2. Set an unreasonable scale or target that forces new strategic thinking
3. Compress the timeline to achieve it
4. Get into the physical trenches to feel the impact firsthand
