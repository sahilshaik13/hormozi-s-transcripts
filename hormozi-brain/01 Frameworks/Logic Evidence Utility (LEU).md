# Logic Evidence Utility (LEU)

## What It Is
A method to dismantle limiting beliefs, mystical thinking, and emotional excuses by grounding them in objective reality.

## Steps
1. Logic: Define the vague belief in terms of specific, observable behavior.
2. Evidence: Ask how you know it's true and count the actual occurrences.
3. Utility: Ask 'who cares?' and determine if this behavior actually prevents you from hitting your goals.
