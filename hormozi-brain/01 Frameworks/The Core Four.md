# The Core Four


> 🔑 **Core Framework** — appears in multiple videos
## What It Is
The only four methods available to let people know about your products or services.

## Steps
1. 1-to-1 to people who know you (Warm Outreach)
2. 1-to-1 to strangers (Cold Outreach)
3. 1-to-many to people who know you (Content)
4. 1-to-many to strangers (Paid Ads)
