# More, Better, New


> 🔑 **Core Framework** — appears in multiple videos
## What It Is
A sequential prioritization framework for scaling advertising, marketing, and business operations without getting distracted.

## Steps
1. Do more of what is currently working
2. Do it better by optimizing the process
3. Try something new only when you cannot do more or better
