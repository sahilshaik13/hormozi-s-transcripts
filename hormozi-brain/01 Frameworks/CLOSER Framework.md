# CLOSER Framework


> 🔑 **Core Framework** — appears in multiple videos
> A step-by-step conversational milestone system to guide a prospect from identifying their problem to committing to a solution.

## What It Is
A step-by-step conversational milestone system to guide a prospect from identifying their problem to committing to a solution.

## Steps
1. Clarify why they are there
2. Label them with a problem
3. Overview their past pain
4. Sell the vacation
5. Explain away their concerns
6. Reinforce the decision
