# The CLOSER Framework


> 🔑 **Core Framework** — appears in multiple videos
## What It Is
A scalable, question-only script structure and psychological path to navigate a transactional sales call or customer onboarding.

## Steps
1. Clarify why the person is there
2. Label them with a problem
3. Overview the past pain
4. Sell the vacation (tell 30-second illustrative stories)
5. Explain away their concerns (overcome objections)
6. Reinforce the decision (prevent buyer's remorse)
