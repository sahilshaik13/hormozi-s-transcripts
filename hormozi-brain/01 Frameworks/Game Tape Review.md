# Game Tape Review


> 🔑 **Core Framework** — appears in multiple videos
## What It Is
A daily sales training protocol where recorded calls are reviewed collectively to improve skills without triggering defensiveness.

## Steps
1. Play the recorded sales call for the team
2. Have the rep critique themselves first
3. Have the rest of the team praise what the rep did well
4. Drill specific obstacles
