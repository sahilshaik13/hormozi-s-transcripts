# 4-Point Market Selection


> 🔑 **Core Framework** — appears in multiple videos
## What It Is
A filter to ensure you are selling to a market capable of supporting premium prices and massive scale.

## Steps
1. Ensure the market has massive pain
2. Ensure they have purchasing power
3. Ensure they are easy to target
4. Ensure the market is growing
