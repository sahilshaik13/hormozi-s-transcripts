# How to Build a Valuable Company You Can Sell Someday

**Core Idea:** To build a highly valuable business, you must separate the 'goose' (the unsellable lead-generation engine or personal brand) from the 'eggs' (the recurring, faceless, scalable assets) and focus on selling the eggs while keeping the goose.
