# I'm Broke, What Business Do I Start

**Core Idea:** To start a successful business, identify a specific problem based on your pain, profession, or passion, narrow your target audience to charge premium prices, and clearly articulate how your unique mechanism delivers their desired outcome without the usual sacrifices.
