# How to Build a LEGIT Online Course (2023)

**Core Idea:** To build a legitimate, highly profitable education business, you must emulate elite institutions by rejecting unqualified applicants, relying on data over sensational claims, giving away the curriculum for free, and failing students who don't meet your brand standards.
