# $100M Money Models: How To Make Money -- Alex Hormozi -- 2025

**Core Idea:** A $100M Money Model is a deliberate sequence of offers—Attraction, Upsell, Downsell, and Continuity—designed to make more profit from a customer in the first 30 days than it costs to acquire them, eliminating cash as a bottleneck for growth.
