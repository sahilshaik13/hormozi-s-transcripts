# BAMFAM (Book a Meeting From a Meeting)

## When to Use
At the end of any sales call, consultation, or onboarding where a final decision hasn't been made or next steps are required.

## Expected Outcome
Prevents prospects from falling through the cracks by forcing a scheduled follow-up.
